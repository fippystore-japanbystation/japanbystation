Osaka · Getting there

Kansai Airport to Osaka

Two railways leave the airport. Pick the one that stops at your hotel and you won't need to change trains.

NambaUmedaTennoji

The short version: Nankai trains go to Namba. JR trains go to Tennoji, Osaka Station and Shin-Osaka. Both leave from Kansai Airport Station, across the walkway from Terminal 1 (Terminal 2 passengers take a shuttle bus first). Choose by where you're sleeping.

Which train for your hotel

To Namba

Nankai Rapi:t

34–39 min

Reserved seats, big luggage space, the fastest way to Namba.

¥970 + seat charge

Nankai lists the seat charge at ¥700. Older guides still say ¥520.

To Namba

Nankai Airport Express

43 min

No reservation, just tap in. Only a few minutes slower than Rapi:t.

about ¥970

The best value if you're travelling light.

To Tennoji

JR Haruka

30 min

Reserved and unreserved cars. Also stops at Osaka Station and Shin-Osaka.

about ¥1,840

Base fare plus the unreserved express charge.

To Shin-Osaka

JR Haruka

50 min

For a bullet train connection, stay on Haruka to the end of its Osaka stops.

check fare

Going straight to Universal Studios?Take the JR Airport Rapid to Nishikujo and change to the line for Universal City, 65–75 minutes in total. Or take the direct airport bus, about 50–70 minutes.

Sort it before you land

Staying in Namba?

Reserve your Rapi:t seat before you fly, and walk straight onto the train.

  • 34–39 minutes to Namba, no change
  • Reserved seat, luggage racks on board
  • Dotonbori is 3–5 minutes from Namba Station
