First Trip to Fukuoka: Hakata, Tenjin or Nakasu?

💡 The quick answer

For most first trips we'd stay in Hakata: the airport is 5 minutes away by subway and Kyushu's biggest station is downstairs for day trips.

Japan's easiest airport city: off the plane and in the centre in five minutes, with street-stall dinners waiting after dark.

Where to stayEatFirst evening

For most first trips we'd stay in Hakata: the airport is 5 minutes away by subway and Kyushu's biggest station is downstairs for day trips. Pick Tenjin if shopping and Dazaifu are the plan, and Nakasu-Kawabata, between the two, if your evenings will be spent at the yatai food stalls by the river. All three sit on one subway line, so the choice is about your mornings and nights, not about being stuck.

Who Fukuoka suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Short trips: almost no time lost between plane and hotel
  • Food-first travellers: ramen, markets and about 100 yatai stalls
  • Kyushu explorers using Hakata as a hub for day trips
  • Travellers flying in from Seoul, Busan, Taipei or Shanghai on short direct flights

Think twice

  • Travellers after big temple sights: those are a train ride away, in Dazaifu
  • Big groups at the yatai: most stalls seat no more than about 10
  • Light sleepers right in Nakasu's bar streets, which stay busy late

Getting there from the airport

The subway station is inside the Domestic Terminal. Kuko Line trains reach Hakata in 5 minutes and Tenjin in 11, every 3 to 6 minutes at rush hour and every 6 to 8 minutes during the day, with Nakasu-Kawabata in between. Landing at the International Terminal? Take the free shuttle bus to the Domestic Terminal first, about 10 minutes. Once in town, Hakata to Tenjin is 6 minutes and ¥210.

Where to stay: three good bases

For trains and day trips

Hakata

Shinkansen downstairs

JR Hakata City is one of Japan's biggest station buildings, with limited expresses and shinkansen across Kyushu. Behind the offices are quiet temple streets and Sumiyoshi Shrine, about 10 minutes' walk. Downside: the station side feels like a business district at night.

For shopping and Dazaifu

Tenjin

Downtown Kyushu

Department stores, the underground Tenjin Chikagai mall, and Nishitetsu trains straight to Dazaifu. Downside: 11 minutes from the airport instead of 5, and no shinkansen.

For yatai nights

Nakasu -Kawabata

Walk home from the stalls

The river yatai, Kushida Shrine and Kawabata, Hakata's oldest shopping arcade, are on your doorstep, one stop from both Hakata and Tenjin. Downside: the bar streets nearest the river are loud late, so pick a hotel on the Kawabata side.

Eat like a local

  • Yatai (from about 6 pm): Fukuoka has about 100 street stalls, mainly in Nakasu and Tenjin. Tonkotsu ramen, yakitori and oden are on almost every menu, and yaki-ramen (stir-fried ramen) is the newer favourite. The house rules are simple: order at least one thing each, leave room for the next person, and drop your luggage at the hotel first because there's nowhere to put it.
  • Nagahama yatai: the stalls by the fish market came back in 2023. Nagahama no Hiroshi starts many evenings there with a pork-wrapped lettuce roll at ¥900.
  • Hakata Ramen Shin-Shin, Tenjin main shop: open since October 2003, 30 seats, about 5 minutes on foot from Tenjin Station. Its hours and closed days are on the shop's own calendar, so check before you go. There's also a branch at Fukuoka Airport for a last bowl before you fly.
Rain or wind? A yatai may simply not open in stormy weather. Keep a ramen shop like Shin-Shin as your plan B for that evening.
🏮 Nervous about your first yatai? Small-group and private food walks around the Nakasu stalls take about two hours, and some add a night cruise on the Naka River, so someone shows you how ordering and sharing a counter works.

A simple first afternoon and evening

  1. 14:00 Land, ride the subway 5 minutes to Hakata, drop your bags.
  2. 15:00 Walk to Sumiyoshi Shrine, the oldest of Japan's Sumiyoshi shrines, and the Rakusui-en garden next to it.
  3. 16:30 Over to Kushida Shrine and the Kawabata arcade in Nakasu-Kawabata.
  4. 18:00 The yatai open. Start by the river in Nakasu, then try a second stall rather than staying at one.
  5. 21:00 Still hungry? Hop to Tenjin's stalls, one subway stop away.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Yatai are weather-dependent: on a stormy night, stalls may stay shut. If yatai are the reason for your trip, give yourself two evenings.
  • Markets close on Sundays: Yanagibashi Rengo Market, "Hakata's kitchen", is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Plan a weekday or Saturday morning for it.
  • Flying in from abroad: the International Terminal has no subway, so allow about 10 minutes for the free shuttle before your 5-minute train.
  • Most hotels on booking sites show a free-cancellation date, so booking early costs nothing if your dates move.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Dazaifu: Nishitetsu trains run straight from Tenjin; fares start at ¥170 for short hops.
  • Uminonakamichi and Shikanoshima: reachable by road, but the 15-minute ferry from Bayside Place Hakata or Marizon is the nicer way.

One more thing: breakfast in Hakata's kitchen

Yanagibashi Rengo Market is where Fukuoka's chefs and home cooks shop: fishmongers packed along lanes about a metre wide, plus mentaiko, pickles and sweets, and a few shops with eat-in counters. It opens at 8:00 (shop hours vary) and is about 3 minutes from Watanabe-dori Station on the Nanakuma Line, or about 5 minutes by car from Hakata. Go early on a weekday or Saturday; it's closed on Sundays and holidays.

Quick answers

Is it better to stay in Hakata or Tenjin in Fukuoka?

Stay in Hakata if you'll take the shinkansen or day trips around Kyushu, since JR Hakata is Kyushu's largest station. Stay in Tenjin for shopping and the Nishitetsu train to Dazaifu. They are 6 minutes and ¥210 apart on the subway, so neither choice is a mistake.

How do I get from Fukuoka Airport to Hakata?

The subway station is inside the Domestic Terminal. Kuko Line trains reach Hakata in 5 minutes and Tenjin in 11, every 3 to 6 minutes at rush hour and every 6 to 8 minutes during the day. From the International Terminal, take the free shuttle bus to the Domestic Terminal, about 10 minutes.

What time do Fukuoka's yatai open?

Yatai open from about 6 pm. A stall may stay closed in stormy weather or if the owner is unwell. Most seat no more than 10 people, so go in small groups, order at least one item each and don't linger.

Where are the yatai in Fukuoka?

Fukuoka has about 100 yatai, mainly in Nakasu (along the river) and Tenjin. The Nagahama area near the fish market came back to life in 2023 with a new line-up of stalls.
