Our short answer · Where to stay

Stay where your evenings are.

In Osaka, most people should sleep in Namba: Dotonbori is 3–5 minutes on foot and the airport train goes straight there. Umeda suits day trips, Tennoji suits families and tighter budgets. All three are on the same subway line, so none of them is far.
