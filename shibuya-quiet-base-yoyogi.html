Shibuya, Tokyo · Where to stay

Would You Stay Outside Shibuya for a Quieter Morning?

Yoyogi-Hachiman and Yoyogi-Uehara feel close to Shibuya. On the rail map, neither is one stop away.

Yoyogi-HachimanYoyogi-UeharaCentral Shibuya

For three nights with evenings out in Shibuya, we would stay in central Shibuya. Both "quiet" neighbours need a change of train every night, and on our dates they were not cheaper: about ¥61,300 for three nights near Yoyogi-Hachiman against about ¥57,900 for the cheapest central hotel rated 8+. Yoyogi-Hachiman wins only on the morning walk, with Yoyogi Park 6 minutes from the station.

Answer A · For morning walks in the park

Yoyogi-Hachiman

≈¥61,300 for 3 nights

A small Odakyu Line station on the west side of Yoyogi Park. The Tokyo park association puts the park 6 minutes' walk away, and the park is always open and free, so a dawn walk is easy. The catch is the trip home: from Shibuya you ride the Keio Inokashira Line to Shimokitazawa and change to the Odakyu Line, about 18 to 19 minutes (¥256). On our dates, MI-HOTEL ATELIER HATSUDAI (rated 8.3, about 500 m from Yoyogi Hachimangu Shrine) came to about ¥61,300 for a double with private bathroom.

Answer B · For breakfast and a neighbourhood feel

Yoyogi-Uehara

few hotels, mostly apartments

A residential area where the Chiyoda Line meets the Odakyu Line. For breakfast, Katane Bakery opens at 7:00 from Wednesday to Saturday (closed Sunday to Tuesday), about 10 minutes' walk from the station; Tabelog notes that credit cards are not accepted. Back from Shibuya, the quickest route is the Ginza or Hanzomon Line to Omotesando, then the Chiyoda Line: 11 to 13 minutes, ¥178. Hotels are scarce: the nearest Booking.com listing, a private twin 400 m from the station, was about ¥73,400 for our three nights, and the cheaper well-rated options were apartments about 1 km away toward Hatagaya, from about ¥39,000.

Our verdict

We suggest: for three nights with evenings out, book central Shibuya, ideally on the north side of the station, toward Yoyogi Park. On our dates it cost the same or less and saved about an hour and a half of evening trains. Choose Yoyogi-Hachiman when central rooms rated 8+ cost about ¥5,000 a night more than the Yoyogi side, or when you are usually back before midnight and the 6-minute walk to the park is the point of the trip. Choose Yoyogi-Uehara for a week or more in an apartment.

One more thing: Japan's largest mosque, five minutes from the station

Stay near Yoyogi-Uehara and you're five minutes from Tokyo Camii, the largest mosque in Japan, a white Ottoman-style building tucked into a quiet residential street. Visitors are welcome every day and entry is free; the domed prayer hall with its stained glass is one of the most beautiful interiors in Tokyo. Hardly anyone puts it on a Shibuya itinerary.

Tokyo Camii & Diyanet Turkish Culture Center, 1-19 Oyamacho, Shibuya-ku; about 5 min from Yoyogi-Uehara Station. Open daily 10:00–18:00, free. Dress modestly: no shorts; women cover their hair (scarves are provided). Be quiet and don't take photos during prayers. Sources: Tokyo Camii official FAQ, GLTJP.

Quick answers

Is Yoyogi-Uehara one stop from Shibuya?

No. There is no direct train. The quickest trip takes about 11 to 13 minutes with a change at Omotesando to the Chiyoda Line (¥178 with an IC card).

How do I get from Shibuya to Yoyogi-Hachiman?

Take the Keio Inokashira Line to Shimokitazawa and change to the Odakyu Line toward Shinjuku, about 18 to 19 minutes and ¥256. Yoyogi-Hachiman Station is a 6-minute walk from Yoyogi Park.

What time is the last train from Shibuya to Yoyogi-Uehara or Yoyogi-Hachiman?

On a Wednesday in December 2026, the last workable trains leave Shibuya around 00:33 for Yoyogi-Uehara and 00:27 for Yoyogi-Hachiman, both arriving at 00:48 with one change.

Is it cheaper to stay in Yoyogi than in Shibuya?

Not on the dates we checked. For 16–19 December 2026, a well-rated double near Yoyogi-Hachiman was about ¥61,300 for three nights, while the cheapest central Shibuya hotel rated 8+ was about ¥57,900.
