Food Near Kyoto Station: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner, Side by Side

💡 The quick answer

Eat breakfast inside the gates, lunch on the Karasuma side and dinner eleven floors up.

Seven picks from inside the gates to the Karasuma ramen pair, with the hours that matter.

Eat breakfast inside the gates, lunch on the Karasuma side and dinner eleven floors up. That's the short answer for Kyoto Station, where the best food is split between the station building, the underground malls and two old ramen shops five minutes north. These seven places were each rated 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked, with hours and prices from their own listings.

Who this list suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Early trains: two picks open by 6:30, one inside the JR gates
  • Travellers staying around the station on a first trip
  • Anyone with an hour between a day trip and the Shinkansen

Think twice

  • A kaiseki-style Kyoto evening: book in Gion or Pontocho instead
  • Queue-haters at 6:00: the ramen line starts before opening
  • Vegetarians: none of these lists vegetarian dishes officially
We suggest: breakfast at Shiokoji Cafe if you already have a ticket, Daiichi Asahi or Shinpuku for a ramen lunch, curry udon at Mimikou on a cold day and Fujino's tofu for a calm dinner. New to the station? Our Kyoto Station exit guide shows which side is which.

A breakfast that fills you up before the Shinkansen (from 6:00)

  • Shiokoji Cafe (塩小路カフェ), inside the JR Central gate: go through the gate, turn right, and it's on your left by Platform 0. A Japanese set with grilled fish, or an udon set, for under ¥1,000. Open 6:30–22:00 on weekdays and 6:30–21:00 at weekends and on holidays; the morning menu runs until 11:00. No cards, but transit IC cards work.
  • Tsukumo Udon (つくもうどん), Underground East Exit (B2): order from the ticket machine; there's an English menu. The toriten keiran curry udon combines chicken tempura with egg-drop curry broth. Under ¥1,000. Listings agree it's open by 7:00 (some say 6:00 on weekdays) until about 22:00.

A ramen lunch that's older than the station building

  • Honke Daiichi Asahi (本家 第一旭), Karasuma Exit, 4 min: ramen since 1947. Ramen ¥980, tokusei with double chashu ¥1,230, gyoza ¥350. Open 6:00 to 1:00, closed Thursdays. A line forms before 6:00; gltjp suggests 9:00–10:00 for a shorter wait. Cards and QR accepted.
  • Shinpuku Saikan (新福菜館), next door: started as a street stall in 1938. Order the chuka soba (¥950) with a plate of yakimeshi fried rice (¥600). 9:00–20:00 (last order 19:30), closed on some Wednesdays. Cash only; English menu.
  • Mimikou (味味香), Porta, by the subway's Karasuma East gate: Kyoto curry udon, about ¥1,500, 11:00–20:00 (last order 19:30) with no closing day.

Want a choice of ten? Kyoto Ramen Koji on the station building's 10th floor has ten shops, open 11:00–22:00 (last order 21:30), including Kyoto-born ones. Take the lift on the Isetan south side.

A slow Kyoto dinner eleven floors up

  • Kyo-tofu Fujino (京豆冨 不二乃), Isetan 11F: tofu, yuba and soy milk from its own factory in Tango, northern Kyoto. About ¥2,400 at lunch and ¥4,000 at dinner, 11:00–22:00 (last order 21:15). It takes reservations, which helps on busy evenings.
  • Kyoto Obuya (京都おぶや), Isetan 11F: dashi chazuke, rice with hot dashi poured over at the table, about ¥1,100. 28 seats, ten at the counter, so it works for solo travellers. 11:00–22:00 (last order 21:15).

Later than that? Honke Daiichi Asahi serves until 1:00, and the basement food hall at Kyoto Tower Sando, two minutes from the Karasuma Exit, stays open 11:00–23:00 with Kyoto craft beer, wine and gin counters.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • From 14 December 2026: three Isetan 11F restaurants (Grill Capital Toyotei, Kyoto Wakuden and Molet) close for the new station works, with no end date announced. Fujino and Obuya stay open.
  • Thursdays and Wednesdays: Daiichi Asahi closes on Thursdays, Shinpuku on some Wednesdays.
  • Cash: Shinpuku is cash only; Shiokoji Cafe takes cash and IC cards but no credit cards.
  • New Year: Porta, Isetan and Ramen Koji hadn't published 2026/27 holiday hours when we checked.

One more thing: look for 本家 above the door

There are two ramen shops called Daiichi Asahi near the station. The original's own site warns about similar shops, and its Tabelog page says it has no link to "the nearby Daiichi Asahi". The one from 1947 has 本家 (Honke, "the original house") on its sign. And because Daiichi Asahi closes on Thursdays and Shinpuku only on some Wednesdays, at least one of the two famous neighbours is usually open.

Quick answers

Where can I eat breakfast at Kyoto Station?

Inside the JR Central gate, Shiokoji Cafe by Platform 0 serves Japanese breakfast sets from 6:30 every day. Outside, Honke Daiichi Asahi ramen opens at 6:00 (closed Thursdays) and Tsukumo Udon at the Underground East Exit from 7:00.

What's the famous ramen near Kyoto Station?

Honke Daiichi Asahi (since 1947) and Shinpuku Saikan (since 1938) sit next to each other about 5 minutes from the Karasuma Exit. Daiichi Asahi closes on Thursdays, Shinpuku on some Wednesdays, so at least one is usually open.

Where can I eat late near Kyoto Station?

Honke Daiichi Asahi, about 4 minutes from the Karasuma Exit, serves ramen until 1:00 (closed Thursdays). Kyoto Tower Sando's basement food hall is open 11:00–23:00, Tsukumo Udon at the Underground East Exit until 22:00, and Isetan's 11F restaurants take last orders around 21:15.

Are any Kyoto Station restaurants closing in December 2026?

Yes. From 14 December 2026, three Isetan 11F restaurants (Grill Capital Toyotei, Kyoto Wakuden and Molet) close for station works, with no end date announced. Fujino and Obuya are not on that list.
