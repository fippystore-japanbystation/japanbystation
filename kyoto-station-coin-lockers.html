Who this page suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Arriving by Shinkansen or HARUKA before check-in
  • A day trip through Kyoto between two other cities
  • A last morning of sightseeing before an afternoon train

Think twice

  • Two big cases each: plan on delivery, not lockers
  • Kintetsu travellers with no coins: its lockers take ¥100 coins only
  • Late nights on the subway: its lockers open about 5:30–23:30 only
We suggest: book a large JR Central smart locker on the Hachijo side the day before (¥700 plus ¥500 to reserve), or skip lockers and send the case to your hotel by 14:00. New to the station? Our Kyoto Station exit guide shows which side is which.

Paying: older key lockers want ¥100 coins. The touch-panel lockers take ICOCA, Suica or a credit card and give you a PIN instead of a key. JR Central's smart lockers are cashless only. Most lockers allow up to three days in a row. On the smart lockers a new day starts at 2:00.

Where the big lockers are, by where you're going

  • Taking the Shinkansen, or arriving on one? The Hachijo exit side has the largest cluster, including the extra-large lockers. JR Central's 72 smart lockers (40 of them large) are on 1F of Miyako Yume Terrace at the Hachijo exit.
  • Heading for the city buses or Kyoto Tower? On the Karasuma side, the biggest banks are by the police box (about 500 lockers) and in the B1 locker room down the escalator in front of the Central gate (about 400, many medium and large).
  • Changing to Kintetsu for Nara? The Kintetsu station has 45 coin lockers, only 10 of them large, and they take ¥100 coins only. Since May 2026 there are also 17 pikuraku smart lockers inside the Kintetsu gates, 5:00–24:00, ¥400–900 a day.
  • Coming back for a JR train? Inside the JR gates there are lockers at the Central gate and the Shinkansen transfer gate.

All full? Plan B, C and D

  1. Look Check the live map. The free Kyoto Station Smart Navi map shows which lockers are free right now. No app needed.
  2. Hand over Crosta Kyoto, B1. Take the escalator after the JR Central gate. Listings give about ¥1,000 per item per day, 8:00–20:00. Nothing over 30 kg or 2 m, and no cardboard boxes.
  3. Send it Hotel delivery the same day. Hand the case to Crosta or put it in a pikuraku PORTER locker (Karasuma central gate or Hachijo exit) by 14:00. The case reaches about 280 Kyoto hotels the same day, at ¥1,900–2,800 per item at pikuraku. JR Central's smart lockers deliver for ¥1,500–1,700 if you book by 9:30 and drop off by 11:00.
  4. Ask Kyoto Tourist Information Center, 2F, 8:30–19:00, also sends luggage to lodgings in Kyoto Prefecture and central Osaka, when it has space.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Large lockers first: in busy seasons the large lockers fill before the small ones, so arrive early or book a JR Central smart locker up to a month ahead.
  • Buses: Kyoto City Bus drivers may refuse luggage that's too big, and the city asks visitors not to bring suitcases on buses.
  • Peak weekends: on autumn 2026 peak days the city added a temporary luggage counter in front of Hotel Granvia (the last ones are 28–29 November).
  • Shinkansen: bags over 160 cm in total size need a seat booked with an oversized baggage area.

One more thing: the quiet lockers by Platform 0

When the main banks are full, try the lockers near Platform 0, inside the JR gates. A local guide counts about 50 there, tucked away from the crowds, and notes that the Central side is often less crowded than Hachijo. Platform 0 is right by the Central gate, so the lockers are a short walk once you're inside, and you collect the case on your way to the train.

Quick answers

How much are coin lockers at Kyoto Station?

About ¥400 for a small locker, ¥500 medium, ¥700 for a suitcase-size large one and ¥1,000 for an oversized locker, per day. JR Central's smart lockers on the Hachijo side cost ¥400/500/700, and the Kintetsu side's coin lockers ¥400–1,000. Checked 26 November 2026.

Where are the big coin lockers at Kyoto Station?

Most of them, including the extra-large ones, are on the Hachijo (south, Shinkansen) side. JR Central's 72 smart lockers there have 40 large ones, 86 cm tall, and they can be booked ahead. The Karasuma (north) side has big banks by the police box and in the B1 locker room.

What can I do if Kyoto Station's lockers are full?

Hand your case to Crosta Kyoto on B1 (about ¥1,000 a day) or send it to your hotel the same day: drop it by 14:00 at Crosta or a pikuraku PORTER smart locker. The free Kyoto Station Smart Navi map shows which lockers are free right now.

Can I take a big suitcase on a Kyoto city bus?

Kyoto City Bus says drivers may refuse luggage that is too large, and the city asks visitors not to bring big suitcases on buses. Leave it at the station or send it to your hotel, or take a taxi or train.
