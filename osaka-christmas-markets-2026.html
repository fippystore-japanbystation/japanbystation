Tenshiba, Tennoji: the big free one

The Christmas Market in Osaka Tenshiba fills the lawn and the Tenshiba i:na area at the Tennoji Park entrance from 20 November to 25 December. Entry is free. Hours are 11:00–21:00 until 18 December and 11:00–21:30 from 19 December, with last orders 30 minutes before closing. It runs in rain and closes only in severe weather.

  • What's there in 2026: 24 shops (3 new), three giant Christmas trees, a tunnel of lights, two workshop booths, and live music on December weekends and every day from 19 to 25 December.
  • New this year: a Santa House at Tenshiba i:na. Photos with Santa are paid; when he's out, the house is free to photograph.
  • The mug: a dark-red original mug. Hot drinks bought in it cost ¥100 less, according to Rurubu. A two-mug set can be pre-ordered on KKday from the official site.
  • Getting there: a short walk from Osaka Metro Tennoji (Midosuji and Tanimachi lines), JR Tennoji and Kintetsu Osaka-Abenobashi.

The area is smoke-free, and saving seats with bags isn't allowed, so bring a friend to hold the table.

Umeda Sky Building: the tree and the light shows

This year's event is called THE STARLIGHT TOWN, and the market is the Wonder Christmas Marche on Wonder Square, the plaza at the foot of the building. It runs 13 November–25 December: market 12:00–22:00 (last order 21:30), tree lit 12:00–23:00. Entry is free.

The star is a tree of about 25 m with about 130,000 LEDs. Light shows run on the hour and on the half hour; from 1 to 25 December the main story show (about 6 minutes) runs 17:00–21:30. On the menu: Glühwein, hot chocolate, a long waffle "tree", smoked turkey and custard buns. The mug is reported at ¥900, in mint or grey.

Set passes went on sale on 2 November: a Trial Pass (Glühwein and a Thüringer sausage), an Enjoy-It-All Pass (with two mugs) and a Premium Pass that adds the observatory. Each includes a ¥200 coupon for Takimi-koji downstairs. We couldn't find the prices published, so check them on the official page before you go.

Hankyu Umeda: the indoor one

Hankyu's flagship store turns its 9th-floor event hall and Festival Square into a market from 18 November, under the theme "Tie the Magic". About 120 brands take part, including Käthe Wohlfahrt with German wooden ornaments and advent calendars, plus European food, German-style sausages and sweets.

  • The tree: an 8 m mirror-ball tree with a light show on the hour, 11:00–19:00, with a scent sprayed during each show. The last show is at 16:00 on 25 December.
  • Merry-go-round: ¥350 a ride, booked by time slot on eplus only (not sold in the store).
  • Santa from Rovaniemi: 19–20 December, ¥2,200 per group. There's also an icing-cookie workshop (¥2,200).
  • Mind the dates: the event hall part ends on 20 December and part of the hall is closed on 1 December. On the 8th floor, the GREEN AGE Christmas Market runs 2–25 December.

It's the warm, dry choice for a rainy day or with small children, and two or three minutes from the Umeda stations. Hankyu warns of waits and entry limits when it's crowded, so book the merry-go-round slot ahead.

Which one is for you

Our picks

  • The full market night: Tenshiba. Free, the most stalls, and the lights with Abeno Harukas behind them.
  • One evening near Osaka Station: Umeda Sky Building, timed for the 17:00–21:30 shows in December.
  • Small children or rain: Hankyu Umeda, indoors, with the merry-go-round.
  • Ornament shopping: Hankyu (Käthe Wohlfahrt and about 120 brands).

Think twice

  • Hankyu after 20 December: only the Festival Square part is left.
  • Umeda Sky passes: prices weren't published when we checked, so don't assume a pass saves money.
  • Tenshiba on December weekend evenings: the busiest time; come by late afternoon for a table.

One December evening, two markets

  1. 15:00 Tenshiba in daylight: walk the stalls, get a mug and a hot drink while tables are easier to find.
  2. 16:30 Midosuji Line from Tennoji to Umeda, one straight ride north.
  3. 17:00 Hankyu Umeda 9F for ornaments and the mirror-ball tree (last show of the day at 19:00).
  4. 18:30 Walk to Umeda Sky Building (about 7 minutes from JR Osaka) and catch a light show; they run every half hour until 21:30.
  5. 20:30 Dinner in Takimi-koji downstairs, or the observatory if you still have energy (last entry 22:00).

📅 Good to know before you book

  • All three end on 25 December; Hankyu's event hall part ends earlier, on 20 December.
  • Hankyu's merry-go-round is sold only by time slot on eplus, and Santa from Rovaniemi is there only on 19–20 December.
  • Tenshiba's mug sets and workshops are pre-sold on KKday in limited numbers (linked from the official site).
  • Most hotels offer free cancellation, so holding a December room near Tennoji or Umeda costs nothing if plans change.

One more thing: the Showa street under the tree

Most visitors watch the light show and leave, but under Umeda Sky Building, on B1F, sits Takimi-koji, a free-to-walk restaurant street built to look like an old Showa-era Osaka lane, decorated for Christmas in the season. Every one of this year's market passes includes a ¥200 coupon to spend there, so it's the natural place for dinner after the show.

Quick answers

Is the Osaka Christmas market free in 2026?

Yes. The Christmas Market in Osaka Tenshiba (Tennoji Park, 20 Nov–25 Dec 2026) is free to enter; you pay for food, drinks and the mug. The Wonder Christmas Marche at Umeda Sky Building is also free to walk into, with optional set passes.

When are Osaka's Christmas markets in 2026?

Umeda Sky Building opens first, on 13 November; Hankyu Umeda's 9th-floor market starts 18 November; Tenshiba starts 20 November. All three run until 25 December, but Hankyu's event-hall section ends on 20 December.

Which Osaka Christmas market is the biggest?

Tenshiba in Tennoji Park: 24 shops, three large Christmas trees and a tunnel of lights in 2026, and about 295,000 visitors in 2025 according to Rurubu.

What are the opening hours of the Tenshiba Christmas market?

11:00–21:00 from 21 November to 18 December, and 11:00–21:30 from 19 to 25 December (opening day 20 November: 16:00–21:00). Last orders are 30 minutes before closing. It goes ahead in rain but closes in severe weather.
