About Japan by Station

Japan by Station (japanbystation.com) answers 100+ real Japan trip questions in the first line: which exit, which train, where to stay, in Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Sapporo, Fukuoka and beyond. It is an independent English guide. We do the checking so you don't have to, and we keep it short, so you can decide in a minute and get back to planning the fun part. We're on Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest as @japanbystation.

Every guide starts with the quick answer to one real trip question: where to stay, what to eat near the station, which train, what to do with a free afternoon. Then come the details that make it work, and the things most lists miss: the lunch counter locals queue for, the quieter exit, the free view at sunset.

How we check our facts

Train times come from the operators themselves, the airports and city tourism bureaus wherever they publish them. When we can only find a figure in travel guides, we say so. Each guide shows the date we checked it, and we re-check every three months.

We have not stayed in every area or hotel we write about, and we don't pretend to. Our guides are built from published timetables, maps and official sources, not from paid stays.

Photos

Our photos come from Pexels and, for real photos of a specific market, shop or dish, from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr under Creative Commons licences. Each photo is credited to its photographer, with its licence, on the page where it appears.
