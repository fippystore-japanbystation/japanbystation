Monzen-nakacho: Is Market Day the Best Day to Visit?

You want a calm neighbourhood morning around Tomioka Hachimangu and Fukagawa Fudoson. Here is how the calendar changes it.

Market morningOrdinary weekdayWeekend, no market

For a quiet morning, skip market day. Come on a Tuesday to Friday that is not the 1st, 15th or 28th. Those three dates are fair days at both the shrine and the temple, and Koto City calls them very busy. Sundays bring the antique market to Tomioka Hachimangu: 6, 13 and 27 December 2026. Go on a market day only if browsing antiques is the reason for your visit. Mondays are quiet too, but the shrine-grounds lunch spot, Fukagawa Juku, is closed.

Answer A · Market morning

Sunday antique market at Tomioka Hachimangu

6, 13, 27 Dec · from 6:00

The Tomioka Hachimangu antique market, run by the organiser Rakuichi Rakuza, takes place on the 1st (except January), 2nd, 4th and 5th Sundays of each month, 6:00–17:00. It is not held when one of those Sundays falls on the 15th or the 28th. In December 2026, that gives Sundays 6, 13 and 27 December. The organiser says about 140 stalls line both sides of the shrine approach. They sell ceramics, kimono, glassware and toys, and old postcards and paper items are popular. Entry is free and the market goes ahead in light rain. From Exit 1 of Monzen-nakacho Station (Tozai Line), turn left and walk about 150 m to the shrine's torii gate.

Answer B · Ordinary weekday

Temple, shrine, then a fukagawa-meshi lunch

Tue–Fri · goma at 9:00

Fukagawa Fudoson's gates open at 8:00. Exit 1 of Monzen-nakacho Station on the Tozai Line is 2 minutes away; on the Oedo Line, use Exit 6 (5 minutes). The approach street to the temple is about 150 m long. The goma fire ritual takes place every day at 9:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00, and GLTJP says visitors can attend for free. The inner hall opens at 9:00, and its 4th floor shows a ceiling painting of Dainichi Nyorai over a lotus pond, which the temple calls one of the largest in Japan. Next door, the grounds of Tomioka Hachimangu are open 24 hours, and the shrine office opens 9:00–17:00. Finish at Fukagawa Juku, inside the shrine grounds, for fukagawa-meshi (clams in a miso broth over rice). Weekday lunch runs from 11:00 with last orders at 14:30. It has 30 seats and is closed on Mondays.

Answer C · Weekend without a market

Saturdays and the 3rd Sunday

lunch L.O. 16:30

If your free day falls on a weekend, Saturdays and the 3rd Sunday (20 December 2026) have no antique market. The temple keeps the same 8:00–18:00 gate hours and the same goma times on weekends. For a food-focused visit, the weekend works well at lunch: Fukagawa Juku serves from 11:00 with last orders at 16:30 and no afternoon break, so you can eat late. We could not confirm weekend morning hours for the other shops on the approach street from an official source. Plan the temple and shrine for the morning and eat from 11:00.

Our verdict

We suggest: a Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday or Friday that is not the 1st, 15th or 28th. Arrive at Fukagawa Fudoson for the 9:00 goma, walk over to Tomioka Hachimangu, and sit down at Fukagawa Juku when it opens at 11:00. The answer changes with the calendar. If antiques are why you are coming, pick a market Sunday (6, 13 or 27 December 2026). If you can only come on a weekend and care most about food, choose Saturday or 20 December and enjoy the 16:30 last order. Avoid Mondays for lunch here unless it is the 15th or 28th.

One more thing: the crystal corridor inside Fudodo

Most people watch the fire ritual and leave, but look for the corridor inside Fukagawa Fudodo's main hall, the Inori no Kairo (prayer corridor). Its walls are lined with about 10,000 small crystal stupas, each holding a tiny Fudo figure, and they glow when the light catches them. It's free, and photos are not allowed inside, so it stays a quiet surprise.

Naritasan Fukagawa Fudodo, 1-17-13 Tomioka, Koto-ku; about 2 min from Monzen-nakacho (Tozai line). Open 8:00–18:00 daily, free. No photography inside the hall. Sources: GLTJP (facility-verified), Rurubu.

Quick answers

When is the Tomioka Hachimangu antique market?

On the 1st (except January), 2nd, 4th and 5th Sundays of each month, 6:00–17:00, inside the shrine grounds. It is not held when one of those Sundays falls on the 15th or 28th. In December 2026 that means Sundays 6, 13 and 27 December. Entry is free and it goes ahead in light rain.

What are the fair days at Fukagawa Fudoson and Tomioka Hachimangu?

The 1st, 15th and 28th of every month. Koto City describes the shrine's monthly festival days as very busy. Fukagawa Fudoson holds special prayers after the 11:00, 13:00 and 15:00 goma on the 15th, and a large goma on the 28th. 28 December is the last fair day of the year.

What time is the goma fire ritual at Fukagawa Fudoson?

Every day at 9:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00, including fair days. The temple gates open 8:00–18:00, including weekends.

Where can I eat fukagawa-meshi near Tomioka Hachimangu?

Fukagawa Juku's Tomioka Hachiman branch is inside the shrine grounds, 3 minutes from Exit 1 of Monzen-nakacho Station. On weekdays it serves lunch from 11:00 with last orders at 14:30, and on weekends and holidays from 11:00 with last orders at 16:30. It is closed on Mondays, except when the 15th or 28th falls on a Monday.
