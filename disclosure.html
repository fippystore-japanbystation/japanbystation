How we make money

Some links on Japan by Station go to booking sites such as Agoda, Booking.com and Klook. If you book through one of those links, we may earn a small commission. You pay the same price either way.

Commissions never decide which area we recommend. We recommend areas based on train times, walking distances and who is travelling. If an area isn't a good fit for you, we say so, even when it would be easier to sell.

These commissions pay for keeping our guides checked and up to date.
