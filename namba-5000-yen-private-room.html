Our verdict

We suggest: on a weeknight like the Wednesday we checked, ¥5,000 gets you a private room (with a shared toilet) or, for ¥15 more, a room with its own bathroom. On a Saturday like the one we checked, ¥5,000 buys a capsule, so take the capsule or move your Namba nights midweek. These were the two nights we sampled; prices change daily, so check your own dates.

One more thing: the mossy statue you splash

Two minutes from the capsule hotels is a quiet stone lane, Hozenji Yokocho, and at its end the Mizukake Fudo: a statue so covered in moss that you can't see its face, because people have splashed it with water for luck since just after the war. Scoop water from the well and throw it gently, three times on Fudo in the centre, once on each attendant, then once more on Fudo. It's free, open all day, and lovely late at night when the lane is lit.

Hozenji, Namba 1-2-16, Chuo-ku; about 5–10 min from Namba Station. Open all day, free. The lane is about 80 m of stone paving lined with old restaurants and bars. Sources: Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau (osaka-info.jp), GLTJP (supervised by Hozenji).

Quick answers

Can I get a private room near Namba for ¥5,000?

On the Wednesday we checked (16 December 2026), yes: a private single with a shared toilet was ¥2,998, and an economy single with its own bathroom at Toho Hotel Namba Motomachi was ¥5,015 including taxes, ¥15 over budget. On the Saturday we checked (19 December), the cheapest private room within 1 km was ¥11,130.

How much is a capsule hotel near Namba?

On the same Wednesday the cheapest capsule within 1 km was about ¥3,200 (Cabin & Capsule Hotel J-SHIP Osaka Namba, male lower bunk). On the Saturday the cheapest was about ¥5,000 at Capsule Hotel Asahi Plaza Shinsaibashi.

Do cheap Namba rooms have a private bathroom?

Not always. The cheapest single we found (Hotel LEAP, about ¥3,000 on a Wednesday) lists an attached shower room but a shared toilet. Read the room facilities before booking if a private toilet matters.
