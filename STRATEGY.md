# Japan by Station: strategy (North Star)

Owner: Ling. Project lead: Claude (Ling, 24 Nov: "you are head of the Japan by Station project — plan and think through the whole logic").
EVERY producer (website, Reels, Facebook, Pinterest, SEO, performance) reads this file first. When a detailed rule in PLAYBOOK.md conflicts with this file, this file wins. Ling's words are the direction, not a script to follow 100%.

## 1. The goal
Reach many people and earn **followers and likes** by giving **unique, informative details** about Japan, **without pushing**.
- First: follows + engagement (likes, comments, saves, shares) on Instagram and Facebook.
- Second: visits to japanbystation.com. Nice to have, never forced.
- Not now: selling. No "book now", no hard sell, no hotel promotion on social.

## 1b. Facebook goal (Ling, 25 Nov)
- **5,000 Facebook Page followers** is the target: that is when Facebook monetisation can start. Everything on Facebook serves it.
- Mix on Facebook: **70% engagement + follow** (place cards, guess-where, and DEEP INFORMATIVE posts that give the full value inside the post, ending with a question or a soft "follow for one Japan tip a day"; no link) and **30% link to the website** (practical posts with the guide link). Reels count in the 70%.
- The daily report tracks FB followers toward 5,000 and which post types bring new followers.

| Channel | Job | Hotels? |

## 3. What a social post is about (content mix, Facebook + Reels)
Lead with **Japan itself**: what's fun, special, surprising or useful, at a specific station or area.
- ~35% WONDER: a unique local detail most visitors miss (a market where they cook the fish you buy, a torii that only floats at high tide, a food only one area does well).
- ~25% SMART TRAVEL: one practical thing that makes the day easier (which train, which exit, last bus, luggage trick, a mistake to avoid).
- ~15% SCENIC: one beautiful place and why people love it (snow, quiet, a view, the season), photo- or footage-led, few words, an easy question. On Facebook this is the PLACE CARD / GUESS WHERE format; on Reels the SCENIC Reel (PLAYBOOK 2b and 3). Research 25 Nov: stunning photo + one line = 1.4K likes on a peer page; link/long-text posts 1–6.
- ~15% PLAY: easy engagement: A-or-B picks, guess the price, "which would you choose", "how would you say this?", "tag who you'd go with".
- ~10% STAY, LIGHT: the area to base yourself in and why (food, mood, transport), never a hotel sales pitch; the hotel shortlist is "on the site".
Keep ~20% of all posts as experiments; let PERF.md move the mix.

## 4. How a post earns the click (without pushing)
The post must be worth reading on its own: deliver the interesting part IN the post. Then one light reason to go further: "we filtered X spots / hotels / time slots on the site", "the full route and map are here", or a guess-then-check game. Never tease the answer to force a click. No money talk.

## 5. Engagement is part of the job
- End most posts with a question people can answer in one word or a pick (A or B, which city, what would you eat first).
- Soft follow line sometimes (not every post): "Follow for one Japan station tip a day."
- Comments: reply to real comments within a few hours with something useful (plan, see Roadmap). Never argue, never spam.
- Post times are spread (n8n random delay); no bursts.

## 6. Quality bar (unchanged)
Facts from sources checked this session; no invented personal stays; evergreen topics; not always Dotonbori/Shinjuku; licensed images only (if we can't use it, we don't); clean captions.

## 7. What we measure (daily, PERF.md)
North Star: **new followers per day** (IG + FB) and **likes/reactions per post**. Then saves, shares, comments, reach. Website clicks are tracked but are not the goal.

## 7b. Targets and the levers behind them (project lead, 25 Nov)
Two goals grow together: FB followers (5,000 → monetisation) and website visits (1,000 a day → monetisation). Checkpoints, reviewed every Sunday in PERF.md (adjust the plan, not the facts):
- 31 Dec 2026: FB 500 followers · website 300 visits/day.
- 28 Feb 2027: FB 2,000 · website 1,000 visits/day.
- Then FB 5,000.
Levers, in order of expected impact:
1. Reels (IG + FB) — our widest reach; every Reel ends with a soft follow line; December SEASON Reels 4/7.
2. FB 70/30 mix — no-link posts that people react to and share; 30% link posts for the site.
3. Website → followers: every guide carries a "Follow on Facebook / Instagram" block (readers from Google become followers).
4. Search: SEO audit 6×/day, station clusters + "More around" links, exit-card downloads, December pages, Pinterest practical pins.
5. Community (25 Nov): Facebook's own "invite people who reacted to follow the Page" runs 2×/day (max 25 a run, task "FB follow invites", log in COMMUNITY.md). Ling replies to comments himself — no task replies to comments.
