Christmas Food in Osaka 2026: Umeda and Namba Cake Deadlines, Market Food and a No-Booking Christmas Eve

Same city, four deadlines: Osaka's department stores stop taking Christmas cake orders anywhere from 13 December (Takashimaya in Namba) to 20 December (Hankyu Umeda, in store). If you'll be in Osaka on Christmas Eve, we suggest an Osaka-made cake from Daimaru Umeda (orders to 18 December, pick-up 23–25) or Hankyu Umeda, which keeps taking orders longest. Booked nothing? Hankyu's food hall sells a whole roast chicken on 20–25 December, and Tenshiba's market stalls need no reservation.

Who this suits, and who it doesn't

This is for you if

  • You're in Osaka between 19 and 25 December with a room to eat in.
  • You'd like a Kansai-made Christmas cake rather than a national chain's.
  • You'd rather eat at a market or in your room than book a set dinner.

Look elsewhere if

  • You want a restaurant Christmas course: book the restaurant directly.
  • You leave Osaka before the 23rd: Daimaru, Hanshin and Takashimaya hand out cakes on 23–25 or 24 December only.

Hankyu Umeda: the longest order window, and a roast chicken

Hankyu takes cake orders at its B1 sweets shops until 20 December (online until 15 December). Cakes are handed out at the brand's B1 shop on 19–21 December and in the 9th-floor event hall on 22–25 December; some brands only from the 22nd.

  • No knife needed: Masahiko Ozumi Paris's Christmas assortment of four small cakes (¥4,050), including its "zabuton" Mont Blanc. Ideal for a hotel room.
  • Strawberries first: A la Campagne's Conte de Noël strawberry-and-pistachio tart (¥5,940).
  • The classic, small: Henri Charpentier's The Shortcake Noël, 12 cm (¥4,968), and Juchheim's Short Decoration (¥3,780, pick-up 23–25 December).
  • The chicken: on the B1 deli floor, Chicken Deli TORIYOSHI sells a whole roast Satsuma herb Yuzen-dori (a Kagoshima brand chicken), ¥3,980, on 20–25 December. More regional chicken: Japanese Christmas chicken 2026.

Daimaru Umeda: Osaka-made cakes in the station building

Daimaru Umeda sits in the Osaka Station building. Order on the Daimaru Matsuzakaya online store until 10:00 on 18 December (some cakes can also be ordered at the B1 west sweets shops), and collect on 23–25 December, 10:00–20:00. The Kansai picks:

  • Dojima Roll Canvas, Bonjour! Noël: strawberries and the cream from Mon cher's famous Dojima Roll, topped with a mini roll. 12 cm ¥4,320, 15 cm ¥5,400.
  • Nakashima Taishodo: a Tanba strawberry cream Noël and a Tanba chestnut chocolate Noël, 12 cm, ¥4,536 each. Tanba is the hill country north-west of Osaka, known for its chestnuts.
  • Morozoff (from Kobe): a 12 cm Christmas chocolate cake with walnuts for ¥1,944, the gentlest price we found in Umeda.
  • THE KAEN: a premium Christmas cake with Fukuoka's Amaou strawberries (11.5 × 15 cm), ¥6,480.

Takashimaya Osaka (Namba): Kansai chefs, Christmas Eve only

Takashimaya's Kansai cakes are a different list from Tokyo's, made by Kansai pastry chefs. Order by 13 December (online by 10:00) and collect on 24 December. Besides Namba, consider the shop behind one of them: Patisserie Crochet is in Osaka's Fukushima ward, one stop from Osaka Station, a neighbourhood of small restaurants and a calmer alternative to Namba for an evening out. Its Takashimaya cake, Holy Night (12 cm, ¥5,670), is hazelnut, caramelised nuts and chestnut mousse with orange. Also on the list: Patissier Es Koyama's Marron Vanille (12 cm, ¥4,806) and Gâteau des Bois's Le Bourgeois Rouge (12 cm, ¥6,264) by Hayashi Masahiko, the first Japanese winner of the Coupe du Monde de la Pâtisserie.

What to eat at Osaka's Christmas markets

  • Tenshiba, Tennoji Park (to 25 December, 11:00–21:30 from 19 December): Hiroshima oysters at Berlin Haus, as a cream risotto (¥1,500) or an oyster-and-mushroom ajillo (¥1,300); goulash in a bread bowl (¥1,600); Glühwein from ¥800, ¥100 less in the ¥1,000 mug. Many stalls take cash only; meal tickets are sold by card at the information booth.
  • Umeda Sky Building (to 25 December, stalls 12:00–22:00): ten huts, from the Glühwein & Wurst Haus to Schick, which bakes 100 doughnut pretzels a day, first come, first served.

Christmas Eve with no restaurant booking

  1. From 15 Dec Any Lawson: a 3-gō mini Christmas cake (strawberry ¥896, chocolate ¥950), sold with no reservation, if you didn't order one.
  2. 15:00 Tenshiba in daylight: Hiroshima oysters and a Glühwein while tables are easier to find.
  3. 16:30 Midosuji Line from Tennoji to Umeda, one straight ride north.
  4. 17:00 Hankyu Umeda B1: the whole Satsuma-chicken roast for later.
  5. 18:00 Umeda Sky Building: the tree's story show runs on the hour and half hour, 17:00–21:30, in December.
  6. Night Back to your room with the chicken and the cake.

📅 Good to know before you order

  • 13 December: Takashimaya Osaka's cake orders close (pick-up 24 December only).
  • 15 December: Hanshin's flaneru and Hankyu's online orders close.
  • 18 December, 10:00: Daimaru Umeda's online store pick-up orders close.
  • 20 December: Hankyu's in-store cake orders close; its roast chicken goes on sale the same day.

One more thing: Umeda's newest cake counter

flaneru, a "flour and sugar" bakery brand that opened on Hanshin Umeda's B1 in June 2026, makes three Christmas cakes sold only at that store, including a kibi-sugar Christmas shortcake (12 cm, three to four people, ¥4,320) decorated with spice cookies. Order by 15 December for pick-up on 23–25 December; it may sell a few without a booking, but mostly makes the pre-ordered ones.

Quick answers

When do Osaka department stores stop taking Christmas cake orders in 2026?

Takashimaya Osaka on 13 December (online at 10:00), Hanshin Umeda's flaneru on 15 December, Daimaru Umeda's online store at 10:00 on 18 December, and Hankyu Umeda on 20 December in store (15 December online). Popular cakes can sell out sooner.

Where do you pick up a Christmas cake at Hankyu Umeda?

From 19 to 21 December at the brand's own shop on B1, and from 22 to 25 December at the 9th-floor event hall. Some brands only hand out cakes from the 22nd.

Can I eat a Christmas dinner in Osaka without a reservation?

Restaurant Christmas courses are sold by reservation, but you can build one: food stalls at the Tenshiba or Umeda Sky Christmas markets (free to enter, no seat reservations), a whole roast chicken from Hankyu Umeda's B1 deli (20–25 December) and a Lawson mini cake (from 15 December).

Which Osaka Christmas cake is the most local?

Look for Osaka and Kansai makers: Dojima Roll Canvas (from Mon cher, the Dojima Roll company) at Daimaru Umeda, Nakashima Taishodo's Tanba strawberry and Tanba chestnut cakes, and Patisserie Crochet from Osaka's Fukushima ward at Takashimaya.
