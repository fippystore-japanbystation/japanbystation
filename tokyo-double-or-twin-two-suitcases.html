Answer A · The double: what the plan really shows

ICI HOTEL Ueno Shin Okachimachi, double

¥15,250 · 13.80 m²

This is a typical well-reviewed Ueno-side hotel (8.4 on Booking.com), a 3-minute walk from Shin-Okachimachi Exit A2 on the Oedo Line. Its standard double was ¥15,250 for our night, the only room type for two there within ¥15,000-ish.

The hotel publishes the floor plan: 13.80 m² in total, of which the sleeping area is 4.9 tatami mats. The bed is 140 cm wide and pushed against one wall. Reading the plan with the bed as a ruler, that leaves one strip of floor roughly 85–90 cm wide beside the bed, narrowed further by the desk at the window end. One large case opened flat fits there; a second one ends up on the bed.

Answer B · The twin at the same hotel

Same hotel, standard twin

¥19,650 · 18.40 m²

The twin is the obvious upgrade: 18.40 m² on the official plan, 4.6 m² more than the double. But the plan draws the two 120 cm beds side by side, with almost no gap between them, and the free floor is again one strip, this time at the foot of the beds, about 95 cm wide where the desk is not. The bigger win is the entrance: the area inside the door is wider, so a case can stand open there too.

It cost ¥19,650 on our night, ¥4,400 more than the double and over budget. A tip from the same price list: the hotel's deluxe double is about 18 m² with one 180 cm bed and was ¥18,750, ¥900 less than the twin. One bed in the same floor area should leave more floor, but the hotel does not publish that plan, so ask for a photo before you book.

Answer C · A bigger room, one area farther out

Brillia ist Kinshi Park, Kinshicho

¥10,968 · 25 m²

East of the Sumida River, Kinshicho is a busy local hub on the JR Sobu Line and the Hanzomon subway. A 25 m² one-bedroom flat here (a double bed plus a futon, kitchen and washing machine, 9.0 on Booking.com) was ¥10,968 for 2 adults including taxes, a 5-minute walk from the station. If you want even more room, the 40 m² version was ¥18,804.

The commute: leaving around 9:00 on our Wednesday, Kinshicho to Shinjuku was 21 minutes (one change at Ochanomizu, ¥253) and to Shibuya 31 minutes with no change on the Hanzomon Line (¥252). From the Ueno hotel the same trips took 27 and 33 minutes. Where Kinshicho loses is Ueno and Ameyoko: about 16 minutes with a change, which the Ueno hotel covers on foot. The catch: no staffed desk, check-in 16:00–22:00 only, and quiet hours from 21:00.

Our verdict

We suggest: for two big suitcases at ¥15,000, book the bigger room one area out. In Kinshicho ¥10,968 bought 25 m², almost double the Ueno double, with no extra time to Shinjuku or Shibuya. The answer changes if you land in the morning (an unstaffed flat cannot take bags before 16:00) or if your days centre on Ueno and Asakusa: then the Ueno double works for one open case, and the deluxe double at ¥18,750 is a better upgrade than the twin, if you can stretch the budget by about ¥3,750.

One more thing: the craft street under the tracks

If you pick Ueno–Okachimachi, walk four minutes from Okachimachi's South Exit to 2k540 AKI-OKA ARTISAN, about 50 small workshops, studios and cafés built into the arches under the JR line. Everything is made by hand in Japan, including leather, wood, ceramics and denim, and some shops let you try making something yourself. It's a far better souvenir run than the airport.

Quick answers

Does a double room in Tokyo have space for two suitcases?

Often not much. At ICI HOTEL Ueno Shin Okachimachi the double is 13.80 m² on the hotel's own plan, with a 140 cm bed and one strip of floor roughly 85–90 cm wide beside it. It was ¥15,250 for 2 adults on Wed 16 Dec 2026 on Booking.com, including taxes.

Is a twin room bigger than a double in Tokyo hotels?

Usually yes, but the second bed eats most of it. At the same Ueno hotel the twin is 18.40 m² with two 120 cm beds drawn side by side with almost no gap, and it cost ¥19,650 on our night, ¥4,400 more than the double.

Where can two people get a big room in Tokyo for under ¥15,000?

On Wed 16 Dec 2026 a 25 m² one-bedroom flat at Brillia ist Kinshi Park, a 5-minute walk from Kinshicho Station, was ¥10,968 for 2 adults including taxes on Booking.com. Check-in there is 16:00–22:00 with no staffed desk.

Is Kinshicho too far from central Tokyo?

Not for western Tokyo. On a Wednesday morning Kinshicho to Shinjuku was 21 minutes and to Shibuya 31 minutes with no change, about the same as or quicker than from Shin-Okachimachi (27 and 33 minutes). It is slower for Ueno: about 16 minutes with a change, where the Ueno hotel is walking distance.
