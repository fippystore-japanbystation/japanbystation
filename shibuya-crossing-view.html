Shibuya Crossing: Is It Worth Paying to See It From Above?

💡 The quick answer

Watch one light change from Hachiko Square, cross it yourself, then go up to the MAG8 rooftop if you want the overhead photo. That covers the scramble for ¥1,800.

Why we say this ↓

Free from the kerb, a drink for a window, or a ticket to the roof. Here is what each one buys you.

Street levelWindow seatShibuya Sky

If the crossing is what you came for, you don't need Shibuya Sky. Watching one light change from Hachiko Square is free, and the best paid view of the scramble itself is the MAG8 rooftop right above it at ¥1,800 with a drink. Shibuya Sky (from ¥2,700) is worth it for the skyline, not for the crossing.

Answer A · Free

Street level: Hachiko Square

¥0

Leave JR Shibuya Station by the Hachiko Exit. The south-east corner of the crossing is at Hachiko Square, about 50 m away. Stand back from the kerb for one full cycle: the lights change about every 2 minutes, and at peak times around 3,000 people cross on a single green. Watch once, then cross with the next wave.

Suits: first-timers who want to feel the crowd and take a quick video. Does not suit: anyone hoping for the classic overhead photo.

Answer B · The price of a drink

Window seat: Starbucks SHIBUYA TSUTAYA

buy a drink, no seat guaranteed

The famous Starbucks now fills the whole 2nd floor of the QFRONT building on the north-west corner. You must order to use it, payment is cashless, and there are no reservations. Since the 2024 renovation the seats are set back from the glass and the window strip is kept for standing and photos, so you will get a look, but not a guaranteed seat with the view.

Answer C · For the skyline

Shibuya Sky

from ¥2,700 online

An open-air rooftop 229 m up on Shibuya Scramble Square, directly connected to the station. You can look down on the crossing and Center-gai, but from that height the people are dots. What the ticket really buys is the 360-degree view: Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Skytree, and Mt. Fuji to the west on a clear day. Bags, hats and selfie sticks go in lockers on the 46th floor, and the roof closes in strong wind or rain.

Our verdict

We suggest: watch one light change from Hachiko Square, cross it yourself, then go up to the MAG8 rooftop if you want the overhead photo. That covers the scramble for ¥1,800. Book Shibuya Sky instead only if you care about the skyline: buy a slot before 15:00 to pay ¥2,700, or around sunset if the day is clear (sunset in early December is about 16:30). If the forecast says strong wind or rain, skip both rooftops and use the Starbucks window.

One more thing: a free view with a masterpiece beside it

Before you pay for a view, walk into the passage between JR Shibuya and the Keio Inokashira line inside Shibuya Mark City. One wall is Myth of Tomorrow, Taro Okamoto's 30 m mural of the moment an atomic bomb explodes. It was painted for a hotel in Mexico, lost for decades, found in 2003, and installed here in 2008. The windows opposite look straight down on the crossing, free, on two levels; the glass is meshed, so it's better for watching than for photos.

Shibuya Mark City connecting passage (2F), Shibuya Station. Free and open whenever the station is. Sources: Time Out Tokyo, Lonely Planet, JR Pass blog.

Quick answers

Can you see Shibuya Crossing for free?

Yes. Stand at Hachiko Square, about 50 m from the JR Shibuya Station Hachiko Exit, watch one full signal cycle (the lights change about every 2 minutes), then cross with the crowd.

Do you have to buy something at the Shibuya Starbucks to see the crossing?

Yes. Starbucks SHIBUYA TSUTAYA is on the 2nd floor of the QFRONT building and you need to order to use it. It takes cashless payment only, there are no reservations, and the seats are set back from the glass, so a window spot is never guaranteed.

How much is the MAG8 Crossing View rooftop?

CROSSING VIEW & ROOFTOP LOUNGE MAG8 on the roof of MAGNET by SHIBUYA109 costs ¥1,800 per person including one drink. It opens 10:00 to 22:00 with last entry at 21:30 and does not take cash.

Can you see Shibuya Crossing from Shibuya Sky?

Yes, you look straight down on the scramble from 229 m, but people look tiny. Adult web tickets cost ¥2,700 before 15:00 and ¥3,400 from 15:00, and the ticket is really for the 360-degree skyline, with Tokyo Tower, Skytree and Mt. Fuji on clear days.
