Isetan Shinjuku Food Hall: Would You Try These?

💡 The quick answer

Would you try it? Six things, strangest first

1. Kuruma prawn heads, fried to a crunch

Tokyo Kitcho fries the heads of kuruma prawns until they crack like crisps. It's the part most people throw away, and in Japan it's a prized drinking snack. Isetan's own list of under-the-radar picks says they're "available in the morning, if at all" (Isetan, October 2026). Go at opening time, 10:00.

2. Shrimp crackers sold twice a month

Keito Shindo's Shurimpo are shrimp senbei that appear on only two days a month, per the same Isetan list. There's no published calendar, so ask at the counter which days are next. They're light, keep well and make a good gift for someone who doesn't like sweets.

3. A dorayaki with butter inside

Nakamata from Maebashi opened its first permanent Tokyo shop on Isetan's B1 in October 2022. Its "fuwafuwa wanuki" dorayaki, fluffy pancakes around sweet bean paste and butter, is sold only at Isetan Shinjuku (Time Out Tokyo). Sweet and salty together: eat it the same day.

4. The dorayaki that comes as one long roll

Sasaya Iori, a Kyoto maker founded in 1716, sells its dorayaki as a single rolled log wrapped in bamboo skin, and only on the 20th, 21st and 22nd of each month (Isetan brand story). Slice it like a cake. If your Tokyo days include one of those three dates, put Isetan on that day.

5. Sweets you can only buy here

Isetan is renewing its Western sweets area in stages (May 2026 to about March 2027), and many new counters are Isetan-only. From its 2026 lists: CUSTA custard pudding ¥432, Ekberg cinnamon roll ¥540 (the Finnish bakery's first permanent shop in Japan), Sébastien Bouillet's goûter flan ¥756, and POMOLOGY's lemon cookie box, 39 pieces for ¥1,944 (Isetan, July 2026). Prices include tax. There's also a weekly pop-up called Ma Pâtisserie at Café et Sucré, which features a different famous pâtisserie every Wednesday to Tuesday.

6. The safe classic: Toraya

If you want one name everybody in Japan knows, Toraya's small yokan bars appear on Isetan's best-seller list. There's also a Toraya café on B1 serving anmitsu and oshiruko, a good seat after an hour on your feet.

Free vs paid: tastings, fruit and discounts

  • Paid tastings: the B1 floor guide lists a Tasting Counter that pours a different promoted drink each week, a paid spirits tasting at the Grand Cave wine shop, and World Meat Bar Nisshin pairing charcuterie with drinks.
  • Free tastings are event-only. Example: the first 100 guests got a free peach tasting at the Fresh Market eat-in corner during August's Aomori fair. Don't expect samples every day.
  • Fruit: sold in the Fresh Market on B1. During that fair, Fukuchi Farm peaches started at ¥1,080 each. Cut watermelon is sold all year and is on Isetan's own list of hidden hits.
  • Discounts: Isetan publishes no markdown time. The general depachika pattern, and how to play it, is in our Depachika 101. The store's big winter sale started on 2 January in 2026.
  • Kitchen Stage: a counter where guest chefs change the menu every two to three weeks. Recipes are handed out, so you can take the dish home as an idea, too.

Upstairs: Eat Paradise and the rooftop

The restaurant floor, Eat Paradise, is on the main building's 7th floor, 11:00 to 22:00. Isetan's own pages name tempura at Ginza Tenichi (a sashimi-and-tempura set was listed at ¥4,400 in 2025), Oraga Soba, Tonkatsu Wako Takumian, eel at Miyagawa Honten, sukiyaki at Asakusa Imahan Kirakutei, and Chinese at Ginza Aster. Soba or tonkatsu is the easy, lower-priced lunch; tempura and sukiyaki are the treat.

On the same level, the rooftop I-Garden is free and you may eat there: 10:00 to 18:00 from November to February, 10:00 to 19:00 from March to October, closed in bad weather. Buy a bento downstairs and eat it outside.

Our suggestion: arrive at 10:00 for the morning-only items, take lunch up to the rooftop, and leave the restaurant floor for an early dinner. 📅 Good to know before you go

  • Dates to plan around: Sasaya Iori on the 20th to 22nd; the Ma Pâtisserie pop-up changes every Wednesday.
  • Tax-free works differently since 1 November 2026: you pay the tax and get it back at departure, minimum ¥5,000 before tax per store per day, and only for things that leave Japan. Details in Depachika 101.
  • Some restaurants change hours in December 2026 (Isetan names Isetan Dining and Merroir), so check the 7F page before a late dinner.

One more thing: the gate that only works one way

The Fukutoshin Line's Isetan Front gate at Shinjuku-sanchome is about 30 seconds from the store, but it changes direction during the day: exit-only from 7:00 to 15:00, entry-only from 15:00 to the last train. Arrive in the morning and leave after 3 p.m., and it works both ways for you. On the Marunouchi Line, use the Isetan-direction gate and Exit B4, which has a lift (6:00–23:00) for heavy shopping bags.

Quick answers

What are Isetan Shinjuku's food hall hours?

The store, including the B1 food hall, is open 10:00 to 20:00. The Eat Paradise restaurants on the main building's 7th floor are open 11:00 to 22:00. Check the store's own calendar for holiday changes.

What is only sold at Isetan Shinjuku?

Isetan lists several Isetan Shinjuku-only sweets in 2026, including CUSTA custard pudding (¥432), Sébastien Bouillet's goûter flan (¥756), Ekberg's cinnamon roll (¥540, the Finnish bakery's first permanent shop in Japan) and POMOLOGY's lemon cookie box (¥1,944). Nakamata's anko-butter dorayaki is also an Isetan Shinjuku exclusive.

Are there free samples at Isetan Shinjuku's food hall?

Not as a fixed service. Free tastings happen at specific events and fairs. The regular tasting counters, the wine and spirits tasting and the meat bar are paid. Individual counters may offer a taste, but no store page promises it.

How do I get to Isetan Shinjuku from the subway?

From Shinjuku-sanchome Station. On the Marunouchi Line, take the Isetan-direction gate and Exit B4, which has a lift. On the Fukutoshin Line, the Isetan Front gate is about 30 seconds from the store, but it is exit-only from 7:00 to 15:00 and entry-only from 15:00.
