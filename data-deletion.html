Data deletion instructions

Japan by Station keeps very little data: if you commented a keyword on one of our Instagram posts, we keep your Instagram username and the ID of that comment so we don't message you twice.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @japanbystation on Instagram with the words "Delete my data".
  2. We delete every record linked to your Instagram username within 7 days and reply to confirm.

You can also delete your own comment on our post at any time. Deleting the comment does not delete the message we already sent you; you can delete that conversation in your Instagram inbox.
