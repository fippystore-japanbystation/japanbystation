Tokyo · Where to stay

Narita In, Three Nights, Then the Shinkansen: Ueno, Tokyo Station or Shinagawa?

💡 The quick answer

Stay in Ueno, close to Keisei Ueno. On our sample dates it saved about ¥40,000 over three nights compared with Tokyo Station, and cost about 13 minutes on departure morning.

Why we say this ↓

You land at Narita, spend three nights in Tokyo, then catch a morning Nozomi to Kyoto. We priced the same three nights in Ueno, at Tokyo Station and in Shinagawa, so you can see what the saved minutes cost.

UenoTokyo StationShinagawa

For most travellers we suggest Ueno. The Skyliner runs there from Narita in as little as 41 minutes, three nights for two cost about ¥41,000–50,000 on our sample dates, and departure morning only adds about 13 minutes on the way to Kyoto. Tokyo Station saves those minutes but cost about twice as much. Shinagawa wins when your sightseeing days are around Shibuya and the west side.

Answer A · Best value for this exact trip

Ueno

3 nights from ≈¥41,000

Arrival: the Skyliner goes straight from Narita to Keisei Ueno in as little as 41 minutes for ¥2,470, with every seat reserved. Keisei Ueno is a separate station from JR Ueno: the route planner allows about 9 minutes on foot between them, so pick a hotel on the Keisei side if you can. On our dates, well-rated hotels within 400 m came to about ¥41,300 (HOTEL LiVEMAX Ueno-Ekimae, rated 7.6), ¥45,800 (APA Hotel Ueno Ekikita, 8.0) and ¥49,700 (Ueno Touganeya Hotel, 8.8) for all three nights.

Departure morning: Ueno to Kyoto took 2 hours 28 minutes on our sample 9:00 search: 5 minutes on the Ueno-Tokyo Line, a 10-minute change at Tokyo Station, then the Nozomi. That is 13 minutes longer than starting at Tokyo Station, with one change and your bags.

Answer B · Walk to the platform

Tokyo Station

3 nights from ≈¥84,000

Arrival: the Narita Express reaches Tokyo Station in 53 minutes at its fastest for ¥3,140 (our sample afternoon train took 60 minutes). Departure: the Tokaido Shinkansen starts here, so the 9:00 Nozomi reached Kyoto at 11:15 with no change.

The catch is the room. On the same three nights, the cheapest well-rated hotels within 700 m were lyf Ginza Tokyo (8.8, 700 m) at about ¥83,600, remm Tokyo Kyobashi (8.7, 600 m) at about ¥88,700 and Super Hotel Premier Tokyo Station Yaesu-Chuoguchi (8.4, 400 m) at about ¥98,500. That is about ¥28,000–33,000 a night, roughly double Ueno, to save 13 minutes once.

Answer C · When your days are on the west side

Shinagawa

3 nights from ≈¥59,000

Arrival: the Narita Express continues past Tokyo Station to Shinagawa; our sample train took 71 minutes, for ¥3,330. Departure: the same Nozomi leaves Shinagawa 7 minutes after Tokyo, so Shinagawa to Kyoto was 2 hours 8 minutes, the shortest of the three.

The sightseeing day: Shibuya is 13 minutes away on the Yamanote Line, against 18 minutes from Tokyo Station (one change) and 30 from Ueno. Rooms sat in the middle: Shinagawa Prince Hotel N Tower (7.9, twin with light breakfast) about ¥58,900, Shinagawa Tobu Hotel (8.1) about ¥62,300 and Miyako City Tokyo Takanawa (8.8) about ¥64,000 for three nights.

Our verdict

We suggest: stay in Ueno, close to Keisei Ueno. On our sample dates it saved about ¥40,000 over three nights compared with Tokyo Station, and cost about 13 minutes on departure morning. The answer changes in two cases: if two or more of your days are around Shibuya and the west side, book Shinagawa (about ¥18,000 more than Ueno for three nights, but 13 minutes to Shibuya and the shortest ride to Kyoto). If you have a very early train, very heavy bags or limited mobility, Tokyo Station's no-change departure is worth paying for on the last night only.

One more thing: Tokyo Station from above, free

Whichever base you choose, before your Shinkansen leaves, cross from the Marunouchi South Exit to KITTE and take the lift to the 6th-floor rooftop garden. It looks straight down on the red-brick station building and the bullet trains sliding in and out, and it costs nothing. Go at dusk, when the station lights come on.

KITTE Garden, KITTE Marunouchi 6F, about 1 min from Tokyo Station Marunouchi South Exit. Free; open until about 23:00 (22:00 Sundays and holidays); may close in bad weather; no tripods. Sources: GO TOKYO, nightscape.tokyo.

Quick answers

Where should I stay in Tokyo if I arrive at Narita and leave by Shinkansen?

For most trips we suggest Ueno: the Skyliner runs there from Narita in as little as 41 minutes for ¥2,470, and well-rated doubles cost about ¥14,000–17,000 a night on our sample dates. On departure day you only add about 13 minutes to reach Kyoto compared with sleeping at Tokyo Station.

Is it worth staying at Tokyo Station for the Shinkansen?

Only if budget is not the main concern. Tokyo Station is where the Tokaido Shinkansen starts, but on our sample dates well-rated rooms within 700 m cost about ¥28,000–33,000 a night for two, roughly double Ueno.

Does the Nozomi stop at Shinagawa?

Yes. The same Nozomi we checked left Tokyo at 9:00 and Shinagawa at 9:07, reaching Kyoto at 11:15 (2 h 8 min from Shinagawa). Shinagawa is also 13 minutes from Shibuya on the Yamanote Line.

How long is Ueno to Kyoto by Shinkansen?

On our sample morning, Ueno to Kyoto took 2 hours 28 minutes: 5 minutes on the Ueno-Tokyo Line, a 10-minute transfer at Tokyo Station, then the Nozomi. From Tokyo Station the same trip is 2 hours 15 minutes.
