Food Near Hakata Station: Seven Places From a 7am Beef-Tongue Breakfast to Midnight Ramen

Hungry right now? In the station basement, Hakata 1bangai has beef tongue from 7:00. After dark, there's ramen in DEITOS until midnight. Hakata Station is one of the easiest big stations in Japan to eat at, early or late. Most of what's good sits in three places: Hakata 1bangai (B1 of JR Hakata City), DEITOS by the Hakata exit, and the Kuten restaurant floors on 9F–10F. Two of our seven picks are a short walk out of the Chikushi exit. All of them had 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked, and the hours come from their own listings.

Who this list suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Early Shinkansen or airport subway: two picks open by 7:30
  • Solo travellers: ramen counters and hot pot for one
  • Late arrivals: two ramen shops serve until midnight

Think twice

  • Halal or vegetarian diets: none of these lists it officially
  • Cash-free travellers at Hakata Issou: it takes cash only
  • A yatai night: the stalls are in Nakasu and Tenjin, not here
We suggest: beef tongue at Tanya for breakfast, mizutaki for one on 10F for dinner, and Shin-Shin if you get in late. New to the station? Our Hakata Station exit guide shows which side is which.

A breakfast that fills you up before the first Shinkansen (from 7:00)

  • Tanya HAKATA (たんやHAKATA), Hakata 1bangai B1: grilled beef tongue with barley rice, from 7:00. Recent diners put the breakfast sets at about ¥700 to ¥1,600, depending on the meat. Open 7:00–23:00, cards and Suica-type IC cards accepted.
  • Kisuimaru (喜水丸), Hakata 1bangai B1: a seafood breakfast from 7:30 to 10:00, then lunch and dinner from 10:30 to 23:00. The fish comes in three times a day, the shop says. Keep reading for the mentaiko trick.
  • Ishikura (石蔵), DEITOS B1: opens at 8:30 on weekends and holidays, 9:30 on weekdays. See lunch.

A quick lunch you can finish before your train

  • Ishikura (玄海名物 鯛めし 石蔵), DEITOS B1, Ajiwai Street: tai-meshi, sea bream from the Genkai Sea cooked with rice. About ¥1,000–3,000. Open until 22:00 (last order 21:30), cards accepted.
  • Hakata Ramen Shin-Shin (博多らーめん Shin-Shin), DEITOS, by the Hakata exit: thin noodles in a light pork-bone soup. The shop traces its recipe to a yatai street stall. Under ¥1,000. Open 11:00 (10:00 at weekends) to 24:00, cards and IC cards accepted.
  • Hakata Issou (博多一双), main shop, 6 min east of the Chikushi exit: a rich, frothy tonkotsu that many ramen fans walk out of the station for. Under ¥1,000. Open 11:00–24:00, irregular days off. Cash only.

A slow hot-pot dinner, then ramen until midnight

  • Hakata Mizutaki Hamadaya (博多水たき 濱田屋), Kuten 10F: mizutaki is Hakata's chicken hot pot: chicken simmered in its own broth, dipped in ponzu made with Itoshima bitter orange. Here it's served for one person, and the staff handle the pot. About ¥2,000–5,000. Open 11:00–22:00, closed 1 January.
  • Ganso Motsunabe Rakutenchi (元祖もつ鍋楽天地), 4 min from the Chikushi exit: motsunabe, Fukuoka's beef-offal hot pot with cabbage and garlic. Finish with the champon noodles. About ¥3,000–4,000. Dinner 17:00–22:00, cards accepted; 80 seats, and when it's fully booked the chain points you to its 270-seat main shop in Imaizumi.
  • Late: Shin-Shin's last order is 23:30, and Hakata Issou closes at midnight.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Kisuimaru's breakfast stops at 10:00, and the restaurant reopens at 10:30.

One more thing: the free mentaiko at breakfast

At Kisuimaru in Hakata 1bangai, any set meal or rice bowl comes with the shop's own mentaiko, and you can take as much as you like. Mentaiko is spicy pollock roe, Fukuoka's signature food. Come for the 7:30 breakfast, order a set, and you've had Hakata's most famous breakfast flavour before your train, with no gift box to carry home.

Quick answers

Where can I eat breakfast at Hakata Station?

Hakata 1bangai, in the basement of JR Hakata City, opens early: Tanya HAKATA serves beef-tongue breakfast sets from 7:00 and Kisuimaru serves a seafood breakfast from 7:30 to 10:00. On weekends Ishikura in DEITOS B1 opens at 8:30. Checked 26 November 2026.

Where can I eat late at night near Hakata Station?

Hakata Ramen Shin-Shin in DEITOS serves until 24:00 (last order 23:30), and Hakata Issou's main shop, about 6 minutes east of the Chikushi exit, is open 11:00–24:00 and takes cash only.

Can I try mizutaki alone near Hakata Station?

Yes. Hakata Mizutaki Hamadaya on the 10th floor of JR Hakata City (Kuten) serves its chicken hot pot from one person, and the staff handle the pot. Open 11:00–22:00, closed 1 January.

Is there halal or vegetarian food at Hakata Station?

None of the seven picks on this page lists halal or vegetarian dishes on its own pages, so we don't tag them. Ask at the Fukuoka tourist information centres for current listings.
