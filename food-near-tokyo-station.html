Food at Tokyo Station: Seven Places From a 5:30 Bento to Monja at 22:00, Inside and Outside the Gates

💡 The quick answer

Inside the JR gates, Ekibenya Matsuri sells more than 150 kinds of station bento from 5:30, so nobody boards a Shinkansen hungry.

Tokyo Station feeds more people than most towns. These are the seven we'd send you to, by time of day.

EarlyLunchDinner and late

Inside the JR gates, Ekibenya Matsuri sells more than 150 kinds of station bento from 5:30, so nobody boards a Shinkansen hungry. Just outside the Yaesu gates, the basement streets do the rest: Rokurinsha's tsukemen from 7:30, vegan tantanmen, standing sushi from Hakodate, black-pork tonkatsu and Tokyo's own monjayaki, most of it until 23:00. All seven were rated 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked (26 November 2026).

Who this list suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Catching a Shinkansen or Narita Express and wanting food for the ride
  • Arriving early before check-in, or late after a long day
  • Vegans: a fully vegan ramen shop sits just outside the gates

Think twice

  • Already through the gates with 10 minutes: only the inside-the-gates pick works
  • Halal: we found no officially listed halal restaurant in the station
  • Weekend lunch at the famous ramen shops: expect queues
We suggest: already inside the gates? Buy an ekiben at Matsuri. Got 45 minutes before your train? Leave by the Yaesu gates and eat tsukemen at Rokurinsha, or monja at Tamatoya if it's dinner time.

A breakfast that's ready before the first Shinkansen (from 5:30)

Ekibenya Matsuri · inside the gates, Gransta Tokyo 1F central passage · 5:30–22:00 every day. More than 150 kinds of ekiben, the boxed meals made for train journeys: famous bentos from stations across Japan, Tokyo-only boxes and seasonal ones. It's open before most of the station wakes up, so it's the easy answer for an early train. Cards and Suica accepted.

Rokurinsha · Tokyo Ramen Street, Ichibangai B1, just outside the Yaesu gates · morning 7:30–9:45 (last order 9:30), then 10:00–23:00 (last order 22:30), no closing day. The tsukemen pioneer: thick, chewy noodles dipped into a rich pork-and-seafood broth. Before 9:45 it serves a morning menu found only at this branch; a food site lists the morning bowl from ¥750. You buy a ticket at the machine first, and cards are accepted. Holidays and noon bring queues, so the morning slot is the calm one.

A lunch you can finish in 20 minutes and still talk about

T's Tantan · Gransta Tokyo B1, Square Zero area, outside the gates (turn right out of the B1 north gate) · 10:00–22:00 every day. Everything is vegan: no meat, fish, eggs or dairy, yet the golden sesame tantanmen is thick and creamy. Add the veggie gyoza. The full special set with 12 gyoza is ¥3,300. Cards and Suica accepted.

Hakodate Standing Sushi Kantaro · Ichibangai B1, Nippon Gourmet Kaido · 10:00–23:00 (last order 22:30), no closing day. Fish sent from Hakodate and other ports every morning, shaped in front of you at a counter with 14 standing places. Its house-cured mackerel, salted and marinated to suit the season, is the one to try. It's built for a quick stop between trains.

Sanchoku Kaitenzushi Kantaro Tokyo · Ichibangai B1, left from the Yaesu B1 central gate · 11:00–22:00 (last order 21:25). The same company's seated conveyor-belt shop, with live whelk and surf clam, and a kids' sushi menu. The one for families who'd rather sit.

A hot-plate dinner, and the bowls that keep going until 23:00

Tsukishima Monja Tamatoya · Kurobei Yokocho, Gransta Yaesu-kita, outside the Yaesu gates · 11:00–23:00. Monjayaki is Tokyo's own dish: a runny batter cooked on the hot plate and eaten straight off it with tiny spatulas. Here the staff cook it at your table, so you don't need to know how. The top seller is mentaiko and mochi monja (¥858 half, ¥1,628 full). It's run by a Toyosu fish wholesaler founded in 1871, which shows in the seafood side dishes. 98 seats.

Kagoshima Kurokatsutei · Ichibangai B1, Nippon Gourmet Kaido · 10:00–23:00 (last order 22:30). Tonkatsu made only from aged Kagoshima black pork. The top loin set uses a cut that gives only about ten portions per pig; the cheaper loin lunch set runs 10:00–15:00. Sets can be taken away for the train.

Late night: Rokurinsha, Kantaro's standing sushi and Kurokatsutei all take last orders at 22:30. The Kurobei Yokocho and Yaesu-kita restaurants stay open to 23:00. After that, the station's food mostly closes.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Inside or outside: Gransta Tokyo has shops on both sides of the gates. Going in only to eat needs a platform ticket, so eat outside if you're not travelling.
  • Opening times: most of Gransta's bento and sweet shops open at 8:00 (closing at 21:00 on Sundays and holidays); restaurants from 10:00.
  • No bookings: the ramen and sushi counters don't take reservations, so arrive before noon or after 14:00 at weekends.
  • Luggage: big cases and tight counters don't mix. Our Tokyo early-arrival luggage guide covers the lockers.

One more thing: rescue tonight's leftover bentos with an app

Gransta takes part in TABETE, a free Japanese food-sharing app. Near closing time, shops list bentos, bread and sweets that are still good but would otherwise be thrown away, at a lower price. You reserve in the app, pay by card and collect at the counter at your chosen time. Shops taking part include Mamedanuki's inari sushi, Izasa-chaya's persimmon-leaf sushi, Tonkatsu Maisen and the BURDIGALA bakery by the Silver Bell. Some days nothing is listed.

Quick answers

Where can I eat breakfast at Tokyo Station?

Ekibenya Matsuri inside the JR gates opens at 5:30 every day with more than 150 kinds of ekiben. Outside the Yaesu gates, Rokurinsha on Tokyo Ramen Street serves a morning menu from 7:30 to 9:45, only at this branch.

What can I eat inside the ticket gates at Tokyo Station?

Ekibenya Matsuri in Gransta Tokyo's central passage sells station bentos from 5:30 to 22:00, made for eating on the Shinkansen. Gransta's bento and sweet shops inside the gates open from 8:00 on most days.

Is there vegan or vegetarian food at Tokyo Station?

Yes. T's Tantan in Gransta Tokyo B1 (outside the gates, 10:00–22:00) uses no meat, fish, eggs or dairy; its golden sesame tantanmen is the best-known bowl. We didn't find an officially listed halal restaurant in the station when we checked (26 November 2026).

What's open late at Tokyo Station?

Rokurinsha, Hakodate Standing Sushi Kantaro and Kurokatsutei just outside the Yaesu gates serve until 23:00 (last orders 22:30), and the Kurobei Yokocho restaurants, including monja at Tamatoya, are open to 23:00.
