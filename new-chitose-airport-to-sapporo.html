New Chitose Airport to Sapporo: Train, Bus or Car?

💡 The quick answer

Take the JR Rapid Airport train: it's 33 to 43 minutes to Sapporo Station for ¥1,230, runs six times an hour in the daytime, and you just tap Suica at the gate.

The three optionsWinterWhere to stay

Take the JR Rapid Airport train: it's 33 to 43 minutes to Sapporo Station for ¥1,230, runs six times an hour in the daytime, and you just tap Suica at the gate. The airport bus (¥1,500) is the better pick when it stops outside your hotel in Odori or Susukino and you'd rather not drag a suitcase through a station. A car or taxi only makes sense if Sapporo isn't your final stop, or on a snow day when trains stop.

Who the train suits, and who it doesn't

Train is right for

  • Hotels near Sapporo Station, Shin-Sapporo or along the subway
  • Anyone with Suica or another IC card: no ticket queue
  • Travellers heading on to Otaru: some trains run straight through

Think about the bus or a car if

  • Your hotel is a named bus stop in Odori or Susukino
  • You have more than two big bags each (the train limit)
  • You're driving on to Niseko, Furano or the countryside anyway

Three ways into the city

A · JR Rapid Airport

33 min · ¥1,230

Fastest, and no booking needed

From 9:00 to 16:00 there's one Special Rapid (33 min), three Rapids (37 min) and two Semi-Rapids (43 min) an hour. Cars 1–3 and 5–6 are unreserved; car 4 is the reserved u-seat car, ¥1,000 extra (¥800 as a ticketless booking), worth it at busy times when you want a guaranteed seat and space for a big case. The station is directly under the Domestic Terminal.

B · Airport bus

¥1,500 · 65 min+

Door to your hotel

Hokuto Kotsu's buses stop at Sapporo Station (about 65 minutes on direct runs), Susukino and hotels including Keio Plaza Hotel Sapporo, Grand Mercure Sapporo Odori Koen, Sapporo Prince Hotel and ANA Crowne Plaza. No reservation; pay as you get off by cash, contactless card or PayPay. Suica doesn't work, and it's one suitcase per person in the hold.

C · Car or taxi

60 min · ¥17,000+

Only for onward trips

JR Hokkaido puts a taxi at about 60 minutes and from about ¥17,000 per vehicle. A rental car is for travellers continuing into the countryside, not for Sapporo itself, where trains and the subway do the job. Groups with lots of luggage may prefer a pre-booked transfer.

Our verdict: take the train unless your hotel is on the bus route. The answer changes in two cases: more luggage than two pieces each, or a winter storm warning on your arrival day, when it's worth checking JR Hokkaido's service status before you leave baggage claim.

How the train works on arrival

Follow signs down to the JR station under the Domestic Terminal. With Suica, ICOCA, PASMO or Kitaca, tap through and take any non-reserved car. Without one, buy a ticket at the machines, which take cash only; the orange reserved-seat machines and the JR counter take cards. Luggage is limited to two items each, up to 250 cm in total size and 30 kg. The Special Rapid stops at Minami-Chitose, Shin-Sapporo and Sapporo, then continues to Teine, Minami-Otaru and Otaru. Heading for Otaru? Check the destination: trains marked Teine or Hoshimi end before Otaru.

Eat before you leave the airport

The third floor of the Domestic Terminal is lined with ramen counters from all over Hokkaido, a good first meal if you land hungry.

  • Baikoken: Asahikawa soy-sauce ramen from a shop founded in 1969. 9:30 to 21:00, last order 20:00.
  • Ajisai: Hakodate salt ramen with clear soup, from a shop with an 80-year history. 9:00 to 21:00, last order 20:00.
  • Ebisoba Ichigen: ramen made with sweet-shrimp heads, served in its red bowls. 9:00 to 21:00, last order 20:00.

Winter: when the plan changes

Heavy snow is the one thing that beats the train. On 25 and 26 January 2026, JR Hokkaido cancelled 1,145 trains and about 7,000 people were stranded at New Chitose; for two days afterwards, the last Rapid Airport from Sapporo left at 20:04 instead of late evening. On a disrupted day, JR Hokkaido points travellers to the airport bus (about 70 to 90 minutes) or a taxi (about 60 minutes), and warns that both get very crowded and slower on bad roads.

Unused paper tickets are refunded in full if you cancel the trip, and a reserved u-seat bought online and not yet collected is cancelled automatically if its train is cancelled.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Arriving December to February? Pick a hotel close to the train or a bus stop, so a snow day doesn't leave you dragging a case across ice.
  • u-seats can be booked ahead online with JR-EAST Train Reservation (¥800 ticketless). Use an IC card or paper ticket for the gate; the e-ticket alone won't open it.
  • Peak times: the airport bus doesn't take reservations, so when it's full you wait for the next one.
  • Most hotels on booking sites show a free-cancellation date, handy when winter flights move.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Otaru: some Rapid Airport trains run straight through from the airport, 73 minutes at the fastest.
  • Noboribetsu or Hakodate: change at Minami-Chitose for limited expresses, where all seats are reserved.

One more thing: a Hakodate seafood bowl without going to Hakodate

Kikuyo Shokudo started in 1956 at Hakodate's morning market and is known there for its seafood rice bowls, especially the build-your-own "Okonomi-Don". Its New Chitose branch is on the third floor of the Domestic Terminal, open 11:00 to 20:00, so it works as a first lunch on arrival or a last one before your flight home.

Quick answers

How long does the train from New Chitose Airport to Sapporo take?

JR Rapid Airport trains take 33 minutes at the fastest (Special Rapid), 37 minutes for Rapid trains and 43 for Semi-Rapid. Between 9:00 and 16:00 there are six an hour. The fare to Sapporo is ¥1,230 and IC cards such as Suica work.

Do I need to reserve a seat on the Rapid Airport?

No. Cars 1 to 3 and 5 to 6 are non-reserved: tap your IC card and board. Car 4 is the reserved u-seat car (43 seats), which costs ¥1,000 on top of the fare, or ¥800 as a ticketless reservation online.

How much is the airport bus from New Chitose to Sapporo?

Hokuto Kotsu's airport bus to Sapporo Station or Susukino is ¥1,500 for adults and ¥750 for children. No reservation is needed, you pay when you get off (cash, contactless credit card or PayPay; Suica is not accepted), and each passenger can put one suitcase in the hold.

What happens to airport trains in a snowstorm?

They can be cancelled or cut back. After heavy snow on 25 and 26 January 2026, JR Hokkaido cancelled 1,145 trains and about 7,000 people were stranded at New Chitose. JR Hokkaido's alternatives page lists the airport bus (about 70 to 90 minutes, about ¥1,500) and taxis (about 60 minutes, from about ¥17,000).
