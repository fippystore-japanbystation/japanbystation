Asakusa, Tokyo · Where to stay

Asakusa: Should You Book Across the River in Azumabashi or Honjo?

💡 The quick answer

Price a room within 5 minutes of Kaminarimon first. On our December weeknights it cost less than the hotel across the bridge, and you can walk home from dinner in 3 minutes.

Why we say this ↓

We priced the same three nights on both banks of the Sumida River and traced the walk to Senso-ji from each hotel.

West bankBy the bridgeFarther out

On the December weeknights we checked, crossing the river did not save money. A double at SORA Kaminarimon Hotel, 190 m from Kaminarimon gate, cost ¥36,845 for three nights. A similar double at Tabist Urban Stays Asakusa, just over Azumabashi bridge, cost ¥37,350. That is ¥505 more for an 8-minute walk. Only a room farther into Sumida, near Oshiage, was clearly cheaper: ¥7,585 less over three nights, but with a shared bathroom and a 28-minute walk to the temple. So we suggest you price the west bank first and cross only if the gap on your dates is real.

Answer A · West bank, by the temple

Three minutes home from dinner

¥36,845 · 190 m

Our west-bank sample was SORA Kaminarimon Hotel (Kaminarimon 2-chome), a double rated 8.2: ¥36,845 for three nights including taxes and fees, about ¥12,300 a night. Google Maps puts it 190 m, about 3 minutes, from Kaminarimon gate. After dinner near the temple you are home almost at once, and an early Senso-ji visit before the crowds is easy. Asakusa's cheapest well-rated double on these dates was ¥35,002, so the temple side was not the expensive option.

Answer B · East bank, by the bridge

The 8-minute walk over Azumabashi

¥37,350 · 550 m

Tabist Urban Stays Asakusa sits in Azumabashi 1-chome, on the Skytree side of the river. A double with a small double bed, also rated 8.2, was ¥37,350 for the same three nights. Google Maps traces the walk to Kaminarimon at 550 m, about 8 minutes, west along Asakusa-dori and over Azumabashi bridge, with the Asahi Beer Hall and Tokyo Skytree behind you. The nearest station on your side is Honjo-azumabashi on the Toei Asakusa Line, 280 m or about 4 minutes away (our guide to the four Asakusa stations explains which line you arrive on). It's a pleasant walk to do every day, but on our dates it cost slightly more, not less.

Answer C · Farther into Sumida

Save ¥7,585, walk 28 minutes

¥29,260 · 1.9 km

The clearly cheaper rooms were farther east. OCICA Oshiage Tokyo by R Hotel (Narihira 4-chome), rated 8.7, had a double with a shared bathroom for ¥29,260 for three nights: ¥7,585 less than the room by Kaminarimon, about ¥2,500 a night. The cost is distance. Kaminarimon is 1.9 km away, about 28 minutes on foot, or about 15 minutes door to door by the Toei Asakusa Line or bus. At 9 p.m. you are not stranded: the restaurant floors of Tokyo Solamachi under Skytree (6F, 7F, 30F and 31F) stay open until 23:00, an 850 m, 12-minute walk from the hotel. A Honjo hotel with a private bathroom (unito residence, Honjo 3-chome, 18 minutes' walk from Kaminarimon) cost ¥46,564 on our dates, so "farther out" is not automatically cheaper.

Our verdict

We suggest: price a room within 5 minutes of Kaminarimon first. On our December weeknights it cost less than the hotel across the bridge, and you can walk home from dinner in 3 minutes. Book across Azumabashi bridge when it is cheaper on your dates. The 8-minute walk is easy, so we would cross for even ¥1,000 a night. Go farther into Sumida only if saving about ¥2,500 a night matters more than a private bathroom and walking to the temple. Prices move daily, so compare both banks for your exact nights.

One more thing: the shop that invented Tokyo's sakura mochi

Staying on the Sumida side puts you near Chomeiji Sakuramochi, the shop that created Tokyo-style sakura mochi in 1717 and still makes nothing else. A thin crêpe-like cake is wrapped around red bean paste and then in two or three salted cherry leaves; the shop suggests you take the leaves off and just enjoy the scent they leave behind. Have one with green tea at the counter, because they taste best fresh.

Chomeiji Sakuramochi (Yamamotoya), Mukojima 5-1-14, Sumida-ku; about 12 min from Hikifune or 15–20 min from Asakusa. About ¥250 to take away, ¥500 with tea in the shop. Closed Mondays (and some Tuesdays); takeaway only in cherry-blossom season. Sources: Serai (Shogakukan), All About, Highlighting Japan (Government of Japan).

Quick answers

Is it cheaper to stay across the river from Asakusa?

Not on the dates we checked. For Mon 14 to Thu 17 Dec 2026 (2 adults, 3 nights), a double at SORA Kaminarimon Hotel, 190 m from Kaminarimon, was ¥36,845 in total, while a double at Tabist Urban Stays Asakusa across Azumabashi bridge was ¥37,350. Only a room farther out with a shared bathroom was clearly cheaper.

How long is the walk from Azumabashi to Senso-ji?

From Tabist Urban Stays Asakusa (Azumabashi 1-chome) to Kaminarimon gate is 550 m, about 8 minutes, over Azumabashi bridge along Asakusa-dori, according to Google Maps. From there, Nakamise leads to the main hall.

What is open at 9 p.m. near Oshiage and Honjo?

The restaurant floors of Tokyo Solamachi at the foot of Tokyo Skytree (6F, 7F, 30F and 31F) are open 11:00 to 23:00, while the shops close at 21:00. From OCICA Oshiage it is an 850 m, 12-minute walk.

Should I stay in Honjo to save money in Asakusa?

Only if you accept the trade-off. On our dates OCICA Oshiage Tokyo by R Hotel was ¥29,260 for three nights, ¥7,585 less than the room by Kaminarimon, but the bathroom is shared and Kaminarimon is a 28-minute walk (about 15 minutes by subway or bus).
