Nakazakicho vs Umeda: Would You Walk for ¥3,000 Less a Night?

💡 The quick answer

Book Nakazakicho and walk if you have a small bag. With a heavy suitcase, still book it and take a taxi on arrival and departure.

Why we say this ↓

We priced the same three nights on both sides and walked the route on the map.

The walkThe taxiStation hotel

For three weeknights in December, a well-rated double near Nakazakicho Station cost about ¥3,160 a night less than a similar room by Osaka Station: ¥9,474 saved over the stay. The catch is a 12-minute, 800 m walk from Osaka Station. With a small bag we'd walk it. With a heavy suitcase, a taxi of about ¥600 to ¥800 on arrival still leaves most of the saving.

One more thing: the croquette stop

Right outside Exit 2 of Nakazakicho Station (about 86 m) is Yamatatsu, a small butcher's counter that fries its croquettes after you order, so they come out hot and crisp. It is takeaway only and cash only: cards, e-money and QR payments are not accepted. Grab one on your way out in the morning. It is the kind of breakfast no hotel buffet gives you, and it is what living in this neighbourhood instead of Umeda feels like.

Quick answers

How far is Nakazakicho from Osaka Station?

Quintessa Hotel Osaka Umeda Comic & Books, about 400 m from Nakazakicho Station, is an 800 m walk from Osaka Station: about 12 minutes by Google Maps, plus the time to get from your platform to the street. Nakazakicho Station itself is one stop from Higashi-Umeda on the Tanimachi subway line.

How much is a taxi from Osaka Station to Nakazakicho?

Osaka city taxis start at ¥600 for the first 1.2 km, then add ¥100 every 231 m (fares in force since 5 November 2025). A hotel 800 m away by foot is about a 6-minute drive, so expect roughly ¥600 to ¥800 depending on the route and traffic.

Is it cheaper to stay in Nakazakicho than Umeda?

On the dates we checked (Mon 14 to Thu 17 Dec 2026, 1 adult), a double at Quintessa near Nakazakicho was ¥24,920 for three nights, against ¥34,394 at Hotel Monterey Osaka near Osaka Station. That is about ¥3,160 a night, or ¥9,474 over the stay.

When is a hotel right by Osaka Station worth it?

When you leave early on several mornings. Hotel Granvia Osaka sits 100 m from Osaka Station, but on our dates a Comfort Single cost ¥49,243 for three nights, about ¥8,100 a night more than the Nakazakicho hotel.
