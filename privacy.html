Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. Japan by Station runs japanbystation.com and the Instagram account @japanbystation. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

On this website

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name or email on this site. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may log basic technical data such as IP addresses to keep the site secure.

When you click a booking link, the booking site may set cookies so it can tell that you came from us. That is how affiliate commissions are tracked. Those cookies belong to the booking site and are covered by its own privacy policy.

On Instagram (comments and direct messages)

Our Reels invite you to comment a keyword, such as AIRPORT, to receive the matching guide. When you do, our app "Japan by Station DM" uses the Instagram API to read comments on our own posts, check whether the comment contains one of our keywords, and send you one direct message with the link to that guide. We may also reply publicly under your comment.

To do this we process your Instagram username, the comment ID, the comment text and the time it was posted. We keep a record of which comment IDs we have already answered so you don't get the same message twice. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except Meta, which delivers the message. Records of answered comments are deleted automatically when they are no longer needed, and at the latest after 12 months.

If you reply to our message, we read your reply in our Instagram inbox to answer your question.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete any data we hold about you at any time. See how to request data deletion.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Send a direct message to @japanbystation on Instagram.
