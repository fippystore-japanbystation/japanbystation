# Japan by Station — area guide template

Every area page (e.g. Namba, Umeda, Asakusa, Gion) answers the same questions, in this order.
Each section is also a ready-made Reel topic. Every time, price and distance needs a source link and a "checked" month.

1. **In one line**: what this area is and who it's best for.
2. **The 3 famous spots**: name, why go, walking minutes from the station (exit number), entry price, best time, how long to spend.
3. **How to do it**: a walking route that links the 3 spots plus food stops (start → end, total km and time, map).
4. **Hidden / special spots nearby**: 2–4 things most tourists miss.
5. **Where to stay**: best block or exit, the price range per night, and 3 hotel picks (budget / mid / splurge) with CTAs.
6. **Who it suits**:
   - Couples: evening spots, quiet hotels, the romantic option.
   - Families with kids: room size, privacy (connecting or apartment rooms), strollers, lifts, early dinners.
   - 3–4 friends: apartment-style rooms, nightlife, group-friendly food.
   - Solo: safety, cheap eats, capsule or business hotels.
7. **Getting around**: train lines, the passes worth buying (or not), minutes and yen to the airport, Kyoto, Nara and other hubs.
8. **Costs & time**: a typical day budget (food, tickets, transport), how many days to give it, and how far ahead to book.
9. **Pros and cons, honestly.**
10. **Tickets & bookings**: where to buy each ticket (official vs Klook/KKday), whether to pre-book, and CTAs.
11. **Sources**: links for every fact, plus the date it was checked.
