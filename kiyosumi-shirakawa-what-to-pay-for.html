Kiyosumi-Shirakawa, Tokyo · Things to do

Kiyosumi-Shirakawa: Garden, MOT Museum or a Free Walk?

💡 The quick answer

On a sunny morning, Kiyosumi Gardens at 9:00 (¥150), then walk free through to Kiba Park. On a rainy afternoon, MOT from 10:00 to 18:00, but check the date.

Why we say this ↓

You want a quiet half-day in east Tokyo and don't want to pay for the wrong thing. Here is what each option costs and who it suits.

Kiyosumi GardensMOT museumFree walk

The one thing worth paying for on a sunny morning is Kiyosumi Gardens. It costs ¥150, opens at 9:00 and is 3 minutes from Exit A3. A full loop of the pond takes about 40 minutes. After it, walk free to Kiba Park. On a rainy afternoon, pick the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo (MOT), but check the date first. Its two big paid shows end on 6 and 13 December 2026. On our sample date, Wednesday 16 December, only the ¥500 MOT Collection is on, and it is free for visitors aged 65 and over because it is the third Wednesday of the month.

Answer A · Worth the ¥150

Kiyosumi Gardens, once round the pond

9:00–17:00 · last entry 16:30

Kiyosumi Gardens is a strolling garden built around a large pond, 3 minutes from Exit A3 of Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station (Toei Oedo Line and Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line). Admission is ¥150, or ¥70 for visitors aged 65 and over. Primary-school children and younger go free. The park association says a full loop takes about 40 minutes. The only uneven parts are the iso-watari, stepping stones set apart along the pond's edge. They are optional, and the path beside them carries on. The association's FAQ says half of the pond loop is a flat wheelchair route and that four wheelchairs are available to borrow. A free garden guide walk runs on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays at 11:00 and 14:00 (the language isn't stated). The garden closes from 29 December to 1 January.

Answer B · Depends on the date

Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo

10:00–18:00 · closed Mon

MOT sits inside Kiba Park, about 9 minutes from Exit B2 (Hanzomon Line) or about 13 minutes from Exit A3 (Oedo Line). It opens 10:00–18:00, and the last tickets are sold 30 minutes before closing. It is closed on Mondays and from 28 December to 1 January. What your ticket buys depends on the day. "Tada Minami―Still, shimmering light" runs until 6 December 2026 (adult ¥1,600). "Synchronic Constellation: Cross-Boundary Perspectives in Art" runs until 13 December (adult ¥2,000). Both tickets include the MOT Collection. From 14 December only "MOT Collection: Past Light, Present Sight" is listed, until 6 January 2027. It costs ¥500 for adults and ¥250 for visitors aged 65 and over, and is free for 65+ on the third Wednesday of each month. On 16 December that means a small, cheap visit rather than a major show.

Answer C · Free

River bridge, then Kiba Park

always open

For a free walk, start at Exit A3 and walk to Kiyosu Bridge over the Sumida River. GO TOKYO gives the walk as 5 to 7 minutes, depending on the page. Then turn back east past the garden entrance and continue to Kiba Park. MOT puts its building, inside the park, about 13 minutes on foot from Exit A3. The Tokyo Metropolitan Park Association lists Kiba Park as always open and about 238,711 m² in size. Its landmark is Kiba Park Bridge, a 250 m cable-stayed bridge. If the weather turns, the museum is right there.

Our verdict

We suggest: on a sunny morning, Kiyosumi Gardens at 9:00 (¥150), then walk free through to Kiba Park. On a rainy afternoon, MOT from 10:00 to 18:00, but check the date. Up to 13 December 2026, pay for Synchronic Constellation (¥2,000), which includes the Collection. From 14 December only the ¥500 Collection is on, so treat it as a short stop. Older visitors get in free on the third Wednesday (16 December). Avoid Mondays, when MOT is closed. Between 29 December and 1 January the garden and the museum are both shut, so the free walk is your only option.

One more thing: ¥400 for a whole Edo street

If you pay for one more thing here, make it the Fukagawa Edo Museum, three minutes from the station. Inside is a life-size copy of this exact neighbourhood around 1840, with shops, a fire lookout and tenement houses you can step into, take off your shoes in and photograph. Volunteer guides, some of whom speak English, explain what you're looking at. It feels like a film set, and it costs less than a coffee.

Quick answers

How much is Kiyosumi Gardens and when is it open?

General admission is ¥150, and ¥70 for visitors aged 65 and over. Primary-school children and younger are free. It opens 9:00–17:00 with last entry at 16:30, and is closed for the year-end holidays from 29 December to 1 January. It is 3 minutes from Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station, Exit A3.

What is on at the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo in December 2026?

'Tada Minami―Still, shimmering light' runs until 6 December 2026 (adult ¥1,600) and 'Synchronic Constellation' until 13 December 2026 (adult ¥2,000). Both tickets include the MOT Collection. After 13 December only 'MOT Collection: Past Light, Present Sight' is listed, running until 6 January 2027, for ¥500 for adults. MOT is closed on Mondays and from 28 December to 1 January.

Is Kiyosumi Gardens accessible for wheelchairs?

Partly. The park's FAQ says half of the loop around the pond is a flat wheelchair route, and four wheelchairs are available to borrow. The stepping-stone paths (iso-watari) along the pond's edge are stones set apart from each other, so skip them if you are unsteady on your feet.

Is there anything free to do in Kiyosumi-shirakawa?

Yes. Kiba Park, where MOT stands, is a large public park that is always open, with a 250 m cable-stayed footbridge. Kiyosu Bridge over the Sumida River is a short walk from Exit A3. MOT says the museum is about 13 minutes on foot from Exit A3.
