One Night in Hakone: What Do You Do With the Big Suitcase?

💡 The quick answer

Forward the suitcase from your Tokyo hotel straight to your Kyoto hotel (¥3,160 for a 160-size case on the route we checked, delivered the next day) and spend the Hakone night with a small bag.

Why we say this ↓

Tokyo, one night in Hakone, then Kyoto. We checked Yamato's delivery dates, Hakone's own luggage desk and the lockers at Odawara.

For a one-night stop, we suggest sending the suitcase straight from Tokyo to your Kyoto hotel and travelling through Hakone with a small overnight bag. Yamato's own search shows a case sent from Shinjuku on Tue 15 Dec arriving in central Kyoto on Wed 16 Dec, and a large case up to 160 cm costs ¥3,160. If you must have it in Hakone, hand it to the luggage desk at Hakone-Yumoto by noon (¥1,500–2,500). If you change trains at Odawara both ways, a large locker there is about ¥1,600 for the night.

Answer A · Forward it

Send it from Tokyo to Kyoto, skip Hakone entirely

¥3,160 · next day

We ran Yamato Transport's delivery-date search from Shinjuku (160-0022) to the Kyoto Station area (600-8216): sent on Tue 15 Dec 2026, it shows delivery on Wed 16 Dec, with a morning time slot available. That fits a Tokyo check-out on the 15th, one night in Hakone and a Kyoto check-in on the 16th. Most Tokyo hotel desks can arrange the pickup; Yamato says cut-off times vary by counter, so ask at check-in, not at check-out.

Prices on that route, per piece: up to 160 cm and 25 kg: ¥3,160 cash (¥3,157 cashless), up to 180 cm and 30 kg: ¥4,480, up to 200 cm: ¥5,410. Size is length + width + height. Two checks before you send: your Kyoto hotel must agree to hold a parcel that arrives before you do, and your overnight bag needs everything for the ryokan night and the next morning.

Answer B · Take it along

Carry it to Hakone-Yumoto, let Hakone carry it up

¥1,500–2,500 · by 12:00

Hakone Tozan Bus runs a Hakone Luggage Transport Service from Hakone-Yumoto Station, the gateway station. Drop the case at the counter between 8:30 and 12:00 on arrival day (book online by 11:30 the same day, or queue at the counter) and collect it at your hotel after 15:00. In between, you ride the mountain trains and boats with your hands free.

The fee is per piece, one way: ¥1,500 for a regular piece (up to 140 cm, 15 kg) and ¥2,500 for a large one (up to 200 cm, 30 kg). Two limits: it only delivers to partner facilities, so check your ryokan is on the list before you book, and in early January some hotels around Lake Ashi may not be served because of road congestion.

Answer C · Store it on the way

An L locker at Odawara, if you pass it twice

≈¥1,600 + ¥500 to reserve

Odawara is where the Tokaido Shinkansen meets the trains into Hakone, so travellers who arrive by Shinkansen and leave by Shinkansen pass it twice. JR East's Multi ecube site lists a locker bank inside the JR gates, in the Shinkansen connecting passage: L ¥800 per day (M ¥600, S ¥500), plus ¥500 if you reserve, open from the first to the last train.

The catch is in the FAQ: a locker "day" starts at 2:00 a.m., so one night counts as two days, about ¥1,600 for an L, or ¥2,100 reserved. An L is 86 cm high, 34 cm wide and 65 cm deep: measure how thick your case is. When we checked, that bank showed only 4 free L lockers, so we would reserve.

Our verdict

We suggest: forward the suitcase from your Tokyo hotel straight to your Kyoto hotel (¥3,160 for a 160-size case on the route we checked, delivered the next day) and spend the Hakone night with a small bag. It is the only option where you never touch the case between two cities. The answer changes if your Kyoto hotel will not hold parcels, or you return to Tokyo after Hakone: then use the Yumoto luggage desk (arrive by 12:00). If you change at Odawara by Shinkansen both ways and want the case for day two, reserve an L locker there.

One more thing: the bag-free last morning

The Hakone luggage service also runs downhill. Hand the case to your hotel's front desk by 10:00 on check-out day, spend the morning on the ropeway or the lake with nothing to carry, and collect it at Hakone-Yumoto Station between 13:30 and 18:30 on your way out. It can only be arranged at the hotel desk on the day (no online booking for this direction), so ask about it when you check in.

Quick answers

How long does luggage forwarding from Tokyo to Kyoto take?

Yamato Transport's delivery-date search shows a TA-Q-BIN parcel sent from Shinjuku on Tue 15 Dec 2026 arriving in the Kyoto Station area on Wed 16 Dec, with a morning time slot available. Cut-off times depend on the counter, so ask your hotel desk when the day's pickup is.

How much does it cost to send a large suitcase from Tokyo to Kyoto?

By Yamato's rate table for that route, a parcel up to 160 cm (length + width + height) and 25 kg is ¥3,160 in cash (¥3,157 cashless), up to 180 cm and 30 kg is ¥4,480, and up to 200 cm is ¥5,410.

Can I send my suitcase from Hakone-Yumoto Station to my ryokan?

Yes, if the ryokan is a partner of the Hakone Luggage Transport Service run by Hakone Tozan Bus. Drop it at Hakone-Yumoto Station between 8:30 and 12:00 and collect it at the hotel after 15:00. It costs ¥1,500 per regular piece (140 cm, 15 kg) and ¥2,500 per large piece (200 cm, 30 kg), one way.

Are there big lockers at Odawara Station?

JR East's Multi ecube site lists a locker bank inside the JR gates in the Shinkansen connecting passage: L costs ¥800 per day (¥500 extra to reserve) and measures 86 cm high, 34 cm wide and 65 cm deep. The day changes at 2:00 a.m., so one night counts as two days.
