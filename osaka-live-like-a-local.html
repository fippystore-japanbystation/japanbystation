Our pick · Tenma and Tenjinbashisuji

Tenma

1 stop from Osaka Station

Tenma sits around JR Tenma Station and Ogimachi, north of the city centre. Japan's longest covered shopping street runs straight through it, and the back alleys north of the station, known as Ura-Tenma, are packed with tiny izakaya under hundreds of lanterns. At night it is lively, but it is local-lively: office workers and neighbours, not tour groups.

For food lovers · Tsuruhashi

Tsuruhashi

direct to Namba and Nara

This is Osaka's Korean neighbourhood, and it's famous for yakiniku. The market lanes start right outside the station, and Miyuki-dori Koreatown is a 10 to 15 minute walk south. Three lines meet here, which makes it a smart base if Nara is on your list.

One more thing: the free ferries locals commute on

Osaka still runs eight free city ferries across its rivers, and people use them to get to work, school and the shops. Ride one, for example the Senbonmatsu ferry in Taisho, which leaves about every 15 minutes, and you are standing among bikes and commuters, not tourists. The ride takes a few minutes, costs nothing, and shows you the water-city side of Osaka that most visitors never see.

Osaka City ferries: 8 routes, free for pedestrians and bicycles; most run from early morning to about 19:00–21:30, no service on 1 January, and they stop in bad weather. Sources: Osaka City Construction Bureau, Taisho Ward Office.

Quick answers

What is the most local area to stay in Osaka?

Tenma, around JR Tenma and Ogimachi stations. You get Japan's longest covered shopping street, Tenjinbashisuji (about 2.6 km and 600 shops), and the Ura-Tenma izakaya alleys about 5 minutes on foot, and Osaka Station is one stop away on the JR Loop Line.

Is Tenma far from the sights?

No. JR Tenma is one stop from Osaka Station on the Loop Line, for Kyoto trains and the airport express. Going south, the Sakaisuji Line from Ogimachi or Minamimorimachi reaches Nipponbashi, on the Namba side near Dotonbori and Kuromon, in about 10 minutes.

Is Tsuruhashi a good base?

Yes, if you love food or plan a day in Nara. Three lines meet there: the Kintetsu Nara Line stops on its way from Osaka-Namba, and the Sennichimae Line runs direct to Namba. Expect it busier and smokier around the station at dinner time, when the yakiniku grills are going.

Should I stay in a local area on my first trip to Osaka?

For a first trip of two or three nights we still suggest Namba or Umeda, because they save you time. Tenma or Tsuruhashi suit a second trip, food lovers, anyone who wants quieter mornings, or stays of four nights or more.
