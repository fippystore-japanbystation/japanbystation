Kyoto · Where to stay

First Trip to Kyoto: Kyoto Station, Kawaramachi/Gion or Karasuma?

Kyoto's sights are spread out and its buses fill up. Pick a base by the train lines you'll use, not by the postcard view.

Kyoto StationKarasumaKawaramachi/Gion

For most first trips we'd stay around Shijo-Karasuma: the subway reaches Kyoto Station in 3 minutes (¥220), Kawaramachi and Nishiki Market are one stop or a short walk away, and Hankyu runs to Arashiyama in about 27 minutes. Choose Kyoto Station if you'll do JR day trips or arrive late with luggage. Choose Kawaramachi/Gion if evening walks in old Kyoto are the reason you came.

About a 4-minute read · facts checked 23 November 2026

Before you choose: buses or trains?

A city bus is a flat ¥230, but Kyoto's own tourism site warns that routes like the 205 (to Kinkaku-ji) and 206 (towards Kiyomizu-dera) get very crowded in season, and it recommends trains and the subway. So we'd base yourself near the lines you'll actually use. A Subway 1-Day Pass is ¥800; the Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass is ¥1,100 and covers only the calendar day you start it, not 24 hours.

Rail-first suits

  • Short stays with a fixed list of sights
  • Early starts before the crowds
  • Travellers with luggage or kids

Bus-first only if

  • You travel outside cherry blossom and autumn leaf season
  • Your hotel is on a direct route to where you're going
  • You don't mind standing
Option A · Kyoto Station

Day trips, late arrivals, big bags

Inari in 6 min

JR from here reaches Fushimi Inari (Inari Station) in 6 minutes for ¥150 and Saga-Arashiyama in 17 minutes for ¥240. The tourism office notes plenty of lockers and luggage services, and that large hotels here are easier to find in peak season. Downside: old Kyoto is a ride away every evening. For an early bowl, Honke Daiichi Asahi, about 5 minutes' walk away, has served soy-sauce ramen since 1947, opens at 6:00 (closed Thursdays), and people queue before it opens.

Option B · Shijo-Karasuma

Two train lines, both directions

Our pick

Karasuma is Kyoto's business district, which is exactly why it works as a base: the Karasuma subway line north–south and the Hankyu line east–west cross here. Kyoto Station is 3 minutes away, Kawaramachi one Hankyu stop, and Arashiyama about 27 minutes with a change at Katsura (¥240). Downside: the streets are office blocks rather than wooden houses, so the atmosphere is a short walk east.

Option C · Kawaramachi / Gion

Walk out at dusk into old Kyoto

Inari in 8 min

From Shijo-Kawaramachi you can walk to Yasaka-jinja and Gion, and Nishiki Market is next door. Keihan trains from Gion-Shijo reach Fushimi-Inari in 8 minutes (¥240). From Kyoto Station it's 13 minutes (¥390, subway then Hankyu), or a ¥230 bus if it isn't full. Downside: the tourism office describes the lodging here as long-standing ryokan and luxury hotels, so there are fewer budget rooms.

Nishiki tips: the market association says many shops close on Wednesdays, and asks visitors to eat in front of the shop rather than while walking.

Our verdict

We suggest: Shijo-Karasuma for a 3–4 night first trip. Two rail lines, Kyoto Station in 3 minutes and Kawaramachi one stop away. It changes if most days are JR day trips (Nara, Osaka, Himeji) or you arrive late: then sleep at Kyoto Station. And if this is a special-occasion trip and evenings in old Kyoto matter more than transport, splurge on Gion. Arashiyama is the tourism office's pick for an early morning with fewer people, but as a base for one night, not a first trip.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Kamo River: the tourism office suggests a morning walk on the banks near Shijo-Kawaramachi.
  • Osaka: if you're splitting the trip, see where to stay in Osaka; Tenjinbashisuji 6-chome even has through Hankyu trains to Kyoto (Tenma guide).

One more thing: breakfast at 7 in a 1940 coffee house

Staying in Karasuma? Walk about 10 minutes from Karasuma-Oike Station to Inoda Coffee's main shop on Sakaimachi-dori, open every day from 7:00. It has been serving its Arabian Pearl blend since it opened in 1940, traditionally served with milk and sugar (black if you ask), in a machiya townhouse. Cards are accepted.

Quick answers

Where should I stay in Kyoto for a first trip?

We suggest Shijo-Karasuma: it is 3 minutes from Kyoto Station on the subway (¥220), one Hankyu stop from Kawaramachi, and Hankyu runs to Arashiyama in about 27 minutes. Stay at Kyoto Station if you'll take JR day trips or arrive late; stay in Kawaramachi or Gion if evening walks in old Kyoto matter most.

Is it better to use buses or the subway in Kyoto?

Trains and the subway where you can. Buses are a flat ¥230 but routes such as the 205 and 206 get very crowded in busy seasons, and Kyoto's own tourism site recommends trains and subways. A Subway 1-Day Pass is ¥800; the Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass is ¥1,100 and works only on the day you start it.

How much is Kyoto's hotel tax in 2026?

Since 1 March 2026 it is per person per night: ¥200 under ¥6,000, ¥400 for ¥6,000–19,999, ¥1,000 for ¥20,000–49,999, ¥4,000 for ¥50,000–99,999 and ¥10,000 from ¥100,000.

How do I get from Kyoto Station to Fushimi Inari?

JR Nara Line to Inari Station: 6 minutes, ¥150. From Gion, Keihan trains reach Fushimi-Inari Station in 8 minutes (¥240).
