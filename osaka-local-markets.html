Local Markets in Osaka Beyond Kuromon

💡 The quick answer

If you have already walked Kuromon, or you'd rather skip the crowds, we suggest one of these three.

Kuromon is fun but packed. These are the markets where Osaka shops for its own dinner.

KizuTsuruhashiTenjinbashisuji

If you have already walked Kuromon, or you'd rather skip the crowds, we suggest one of these three. Each one is a short subway or loop-line ride from Namba or Umeda, and each has a different feel: a chef's wholesale market, a post-war maze of Korean food stalls, and a 2.6 km covered street that locals use every day.

Hours and access checked 23 November 2026 against osaka-info.jp and the markets' own sites

Market 1 · For a seafood breakfast

Kizu Wholesale Market

3 min from Daikokucho

A private wholesale market with more than 300 years of history, where restaurant chefs buy fish, vegetables and dried goods. Visitors can walk in and buy too. It is quieter and less polished than Kuromon, which is exactly the point.

Walk

3 min

From Daikokucho Exit 1

Osaka Metro Midosuji or Yotsubashi Line. One stop south of Namba.

Best time

8:30–10:30

Go early

Stalls open around dawn for chefs and many close by late morning.

Closed

Sun + hols

Check the calendar

Also some Wednesdays and 31 Dec to 4 Jan. The calendar is on kizu-ichiba.com.

Our suggestion: skip hotel breakfast once. Take the subway one stop from Namba to Daikokucho and eat a seafood bowl at the market, then walk back toward Namba.
Market 2 · For grilled meat and kimchi

Tsuruhashi Market

right outside the station

A maze of narrow covered lanes that grew out of a post-war street market around Tsuruhashi Station. Today it is six markets and shopping streets full of yakiniku restaurants, Korean grocers, fish stalls and clothing shops. Walk 10 to 15 minutes south and you reach Miyuki-dori, Japan's largest Koreatown.

Station

0 min

Tsuruhashi Station

JR Osaka Loop Line, Kintetsu and the Osaka Metro Sennichimae Line all stop here.

From Namba

1 line

Direct from Namba

The Sennichimae Line runs straight from Namba to Tsuruhashi. Kintetsu trains from Osaka-Namba stop here too.

On foot

10–15 min

Miyuki-dori Koreatown

About 150 Korean grocers, restaurants and shops along one street.

Our suggestion: come hungry in the evening for yakiniku, or late morning to walk the food lanes. Many small shops prefer cash.
Market 3 · For an everyday Osaka walk

Tenjinbashisuji Shopping Street

2.6 km, covered

Said to be the longest shopping street in Japan, running about 2.6 km north from Tenjinbashi. Around 600 shops, from fish and pickles to croquettes and old coffee shops. It grew up as the approach to Osaka Tenmangu Shrine and takes about 40 minutes to walk end to end.

Length

2.6 km

Covered all the way

A good rainy-day walk. Start at Minamimorimachi and head north.

Stations

4

Easy to join anywhere

Minamimorimachi, Ogimachi and Tenjinbashisuji 6-chome (Osaka Metro), and JR Tenma.

Shrine

2-chome

Osaka Tenmangu

The shrine the street grew around. Home of the Tenjin Festival every 24–25 July.

Bonus · For the curious

Osaka Central Wholesale Market

≈10 min from JR Noda

Osaka's main city market, the second-largest market complex in Japan by trading volume after Tokyo's Toyosu. It is not a shopping market and you can't join the auctions, but the restaurants inside are open to everyone and guided tours run on weekday mornings if you book by phone.

Market food tours

One more thing: sushi inside the wholesale market

For one more market, go to Endo, a sushi counter inside Osaka's Central Wholesale Market in Fukushima, open since 1907. It serves "tsukami-zushi": warm, loosely pressed rice under fish bought in the market that morning, five pieces to a plate, and you brush on your own soy sauce. It was named one of Tabelog's top 100 sushi restaurants in west Japan, and it opens early, so going for breakfast beats the lunch queue.

Chuo Ichiba Endo, inside Osaka Central Wholesale Market (Noda 1-1-86, Fukushima-ku); about 620 m from Tamagawa Station. Opens early morning; credit cards not accepted. Sources: Tabelog (Sushi WEST Hyakumeiten 2021), Retty, GIGAZINE.

Quick answers

Is Kizu Market open to tourists?

Yes. It is a working wholesale market for chefs, but anyone can walk in and buy. Go between about 8:30 and 10:30 for the best mix of open stalls and space to move.

When is Kizu Market closed?

Sundays, national holidays, 31 December to 4 January, and some Wednesdays (it follows the Central Wholesale Market calendar). Check kizu-ichiba.com before you go.

Which market is least touristy?

Tsuruhashi and the Tenjinbashisuji shopping street feel the most local. Kizu is quieter than Kuromon but has become popular with visitors too.

Can I visit the Osaka Central Wholesale Market?

It is not a retail market, so you can't join the auctions. You can eat at the restaurants inside, and guided tours run on weekday mornings by phone booking.
