Tokyo to Kawaguchiko With a Big Suitcase: Bus or Train?

We compared boarding, where the case goes and what happens if you are late, using the December 2026 timetables.

Highway busDirect trainChange at Otsuki

With one big suitcase each, we suggest the highway bus from Busta Shinjuku: ¥2,000 online, the case rides free in the hold, and buses run from 6:45 to 22:25. Take the Fuji Excursion train (¥4,200) when you must arrive by a set time, such as a weekend, a holiday or a booked tour, because trains do not sit in traffic. If its seats are gone, or you leave after 10:30, the Kaiji to Otsuki plus a Fujikyu local runs all day with one change.

Answer A · Highway bus

Busta Shinjuku: the case goes under the bus

¥2,000 · about 1 h 45 min

Fujikyu Bus's rule is simple: large suitcases go free in the luggage compartment, one piece per person. You hand it over at the kerb and never lift it onto a rack. The bus leaves from the 4th floor of Busta Shinjuku, which Fujikyu puts about 2 minutes from JR Shinjuku's New South Gate, with a lift and escalators up. It stops right in front of Kawaguchiko Station. Fares are ¥2,000 booked online on Fujiyama Connect or ¥2,200 at the counter.

The risk is the road. Fujikyu gives the trip as about 1 hour 45 minutes and says times vary with traffic. Book a fixed seat (tickets are not sold on board), and note that changes and refunds stop at the scheduled departure, so a late arrival at Busta loses the ticket. Answer B · Direct train

Fuji Excursion: no traffic, few departures

¥4,200 · 1 h 53 min–2 h

The Fuji Excursion runs straight from Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko with no change. On December 2026 weekdays it leaves at 7:30, 8:30, 9:30 and 10:30, arriving 9:28, 10:26, 11:28 and 12:24, plus a seasonal 11:34 on weekdays until 29 December. The fare is ¥4,200 (¥2,580 fare + ¥1,620 limited express). It is on a railway, so road traffic does not affect it, which is why we pick it for a fixed arrival time.

Seats are the catch. Every seat is reserved, tickets go on sale one month ahead, and JR East's booking partner warns they often sell out at weekends and in peak seasons. Ekitan notes a luggage storage area on board; a shelf space is not reserved with your seat, so board early. As far as Otsuki the train runs coupled to a Kaiji or Azusa: check that your car number is a Fuji Excursion car. It leaves Shinjuku from platforms 9–10.

Answer C · Connecting trains

Kaiji to Otsuki, then a Fujikyu local

One change · runs until late

The Kaiji limited express leaves Shinjuku at about :30 past each hour on weekdays and reaches Otsuki about an hour later (12:30 arrives 13:28). At Otsuki you do not go outside: you walk through the connecting passage and transfer gate to the Fujikyu platform. Fujikyu trains for Kawaguchiko leave one to three times an hour (weekday afternoon locals 13:01, 13:55, 14:25, 15:03…), so allow for a wait of up to about 50 minutes; the 12:30 Kaiji, for example, meets the 13:55 local. The Japan Bullet Train guide puts this route about 20–30 minutes slower than the direct train.

It is the flexible option: the last weekday connection is the 21:59 Kaiji, reaching Otsuki at 23:02, for the 23:10 last train to Kawaguchiko. The Otsuki–Kawaguchiko fare is ¥1,170, and the locals need no seat ticket. The cost is a transfer with your case: the passage is signposted, but we could not confirm a step-free route at Otsuki, so travel light or allow extra time.

Our verdict

We suggest: on a weekday, with one big case each, book the highway bus from Busta Shinjuku: it is the cheapest (¥2,000), the case goes in the hold and you choose from departures across the day. Switch to the Fuji Excursion when the arrival time matters more than the fare (weekends, holidays, a booked dinner or tour), and book it the day sales open. Use the Kaiji via Otsuki when the direct train is full or you leave after 10:30. The answer changes if anyone has more than one big case: the bus takes one per person, so then take the train.

One more thing: a ticket without a seat number

If the Fuji Excursion shows no reserved seats, look again. Fujikyu Railway says you can buy either a reserved-seat limited express ticket or an unreserved-seat ticket, which fixes only the date and section, not the train or seat. The same page notes that between Fujisan and Kawaguchiko stations you can ride the limited express with a normal fare ticket and take any free seat.

Quick answers

Can I take a large suitcase on the highway bus from Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko?

Yes. Fujikyu Bus says large suitcases go free in the luggage compartment under the bus, one piece per person. Buses leave from the 4th floor of Busta Shinjuku, about 2 minutes from JR Shinjuku's New South Gate.

How much is the bus from Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko?

¥2,000 for an adult booked online on Fujiyama Connect, or ¥2,200 at the counter. The ride is about 1 hour 45 minutes, depending on traffic. Tickets are not sold on board, and changes or refunds are not possible after the scheduled departure time.

What time does the Fuji Excursion leave Shinjuku?

On weekdays in December 2026: 7:30, 8:30, 9:30 and 10:30, arriving at Kawaguchiko at 9:28, 10:26, 11:28 and 12:24, plus a seasonal 11:34 on weekdays until 29 December. The fare from Shinjuku is ¥4,200 (¥2,580 fare + ¥1,620 limited express). After that, take the Kaiji to Otsuki and change.

What is the last train connection from Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko?

On a December 2026 weekday, the 21:59 Kaiji from Shinjuku reaches Otsuki at 23:02, and the last Fujikyu train for Kawaguchiko leaves Otsuki at 23:10. The last highway bus leaves Busta Shinjuku at 22:25.
