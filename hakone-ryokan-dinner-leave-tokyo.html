Ryokan Dinner in Hakone: What Time Do You Leave Tokyo?

Your room includes dinner, and the ryokan wants you there by six. We worked back from that deadline through every train, and timed a stop on the way.

For a ryokan in Gora whose check-in closes at 18:00, we suggest the 15:00 Romancecar from Shinjuku: it reaches Gora at 17:03 with one change and leaves about an hour to spare. Want a stop first? Take the 10:00 Romancecar, spend the afternoon at the Hakone Open-Air Museum and leave it by 16:50. Can't leave Tokyo until evening? Skip the dinner plan: book a room-only hotel by Hakone-Yumoto Station and eat at a grill 2 minutes away, last orders 21:30.

Answer A · Go straight there

The 15:00 Romancecar, and the ryokan collects you

Gora 17:03 · ¥2,871

Our sample ryokan is Ryokan Kiritani Hakoneso in Gora: a Japanese-style room with breakfast and dinner was ¥34,000 for two (+¥300 tax) on Wed 16 Dec on Booking.com, and its check-in runs 15:30–18:00. Working back from 18:00 on that date: the 15:00 Romancecar Hakone 13 leaves Shinjuku, reaches Hakone-Yumoto at 16:25, and the 16:27 Hakone Tozan train climbs to Gora by 17:03. Total ¥2,871 by IC card, including the ¥1,200 seat.

The last stretch is where luggage hurts: the ryokan is about 850 m from Gora Station on Booking's map. Its Jalan listing gives two easier ways: call from Gora Station after 15:30 and they pick you up in about 5 minutes, or ride the cable car up to Koen-kami station and walk about 5 minutes. The fallback if you miss the 15:00 is the 15:21 rapid express with two changes (Gora 17:48, ¥1,671): it gets you there, but with 12 minutes to spare and no seat reservation.

Answer B · Add one stop

The Open-Air Museum, one stop before Gora

Leave the museum by 16:50

The stop that fits best is the Hakone Open-Air Museum: it is a 2-minute walk from Chokoku-no-Mori Station, one stop before Gora, so the detour costs you no extra transport. Take the 10:00 Romancecar Hakone 3 (Yumoto 11:27, Chokoku-no-Mori 12:16). The museum opens 9:00–17:00 every day (last entry 16:30); adults pay ¥2,000 at the door or ¥1,800 online. Free lockers (a ¥100 coin you get back) are at the entrance; for a big case, ask the museum or use the paid lockers at the station.

Trains from Chokoku-no-Mori to Gora on our sample date: 16:31, 16:47, 17:01, then nothing until 17:31. The firm rule: leave the museum by 16:50 for the 17:01, which reaches Gora at 17:03 and gives you the same hour of margin as Answer A. The 17:31 still works on paper, but only if the pickup is waiting.

Answer C · Leave Tokyo late

Room only by Yumoto Station, dinner on foot

¥16,800 + dinner by 21:30

If work or sightseeing keeps you in Tokyo until evening, a dinner-included ryokan is the wrong booking: you'd pay for a meal you can't reach. Book room-only next to Hakone-Yumoto Station instead. Yumoto Station Hotel MIRAHAKONE, 150 m from the station, showed an economy double with a private shower at ¥16,800 for 16 Dec (a 30% deal on a listed ¥24,000), and check-in runs until 23:30.

The 18:00 Homeway 5 from Shinjuku runs straight to Hakone-Yumoto by 19:40 (¥2,461 by IC card). Kinosuke, about 2 minutes from the station, grills local fish and Sagami meat over charcoal, with dinner 17:30–22:00 and last orders at 21:30; it closes on Mondays (Hakone Navi). Even the 19:00 departure from Shinjuku (Yumoto 20:38, one change) leaves time to order.

Our verdict

We suggest: for a Gora ryokan with an 18:00 check-in, be on the 15:00 Romancecar from Shinjuku, and call for the pickup from Gora Station. With a free morning, take the 10:00 instead and add the Open-Air Museum, leaving by 16:50. The answer changes if you can't leave Tokyo before about 18:00: then don't pay for a ryokan dinner, book room-only by Hakone-Yumoto Station and eat by 21:30 (not on a Monday). Your own ryokan's deadline may differ, so check it on the booking and move these times by the same amount.

One more thing: an onsen for room-only nights

Room-only hotels by the station rarely have a hot-spring bath. Hakone Yuryo, a day-onsen with open-air baths and private bath rooms, is a free shuttle ride of about 3 minutes from Hakone-Yumoto Station. The catch for late arrivals: on weekdays it closes at 20:00, with last entry at 19:00 (weekends and holidays 21:00, last entry 20:00), so it fits a train that reaches Yumoto by about 18:30, not the 19:40. Weekday adult entry is about ¥1,700–1,800.

Quick answers

What time should I leave Shinjuku for a Hakone ryokan dinner?

For a ryokan in Gora with an 18:00 check-in deadline, we suggest the 15:00 Romancecar Hakone 13 from Shinjuku on a weekday like Wed 16 Dec 2026: Hakone-Yumoto 16:25, then the 16:27 Hakone Tozan train to Gora at 17:03. That leaves about an hour for the pickup or walk. The 15:21 rapid express reaches Gora at 17:48, with no margin.

How long does it take from Shinjuku to Gora?

About two hours: 85 minutes by Romancecar to Hakone-Yumoto, then 36 minutes on the Hakone Tozan train. On our sample date the fare was ¥2,871 by IC card including the ¥1,200 Romancecar seat.

Can I visit the Hakone Open-Air Museum on the way to my ryokan?

Yes. It is a 2-minute walk from Chokoku-no-Mori Station, one stop before Gora, open 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30), adults ¥2,000 at the door or ¥1,800 online. Free lockers (¥100, returned) are at the entrance; ask the museum about bags that don't fit. Leave by 16:50 for the 17:01 train to Gora.

Where can I eat dinner near Hakone-Yumoto Station late?

Kinosuke, about 2 minutes on foot from the station, serves charcoal-grilled dishes for dinner 17:30–22:00 with last orders at 21:30, and is closed on Mondays (Hakone Navi).
