Coin Lockers at Sapporo Station: Prices, Where They Are, and Plan B When They're Full

💡 The quick answer

Snow boots, a suitcase and a city to see. Here's where the case can wait, and where to go when every door is locked.

Sapporo Station's lockers fill up early. On weekends with events, the station's own tourist information centre says there's often no free locker by about noon, and JR Sapporo has no staffed baggage room. So arrive in the morning, or book one of the 36 Multi E-Cube lockers by the 1F East gate (¥400–700, plus ¥500 to reserve). A large locker costs about ¥600–700 a day. If they're all full, Yamato's office a few blocks away holds a bag for ¥500 if you collect it the same day.

Paying: the older key lockers want ¥100 coins, and there are change machines nearby. The touch-panel lockers speak English, Chinese and Korean, and take a card or cash. Multi E-Cube is cashless only: IC cards such as Suica or Kitaca, QR payment or a credit card. You can leave a bag for up to three days, and the lockers can be opened from 5:15 to midnight. Open a locked door and you pay again, so pack everything at once.

Where the lockers are, by where you're going

  • Coming back for a JR train, or to Otaru? Use the lockers inside the ticket gates: you collect the case on your way to the platform.
  • Heading out by the West Concourse? There are lockers on the North exit side of the West Concourse, near the tourist information centre.
  • Want one you can book? The 36 Multi E-Cube lockers are on 1F, just outside the East gate, with more lockers in the East gate concourse.
  • Going underground toward Odori? Go down to the APIA underground mall: there are lockers inside it and by the Daimaru Sapporo basement entrance.
  • Taking the subway? The Namboku Line and Toho Line gates at the subway's Sapporo Station each have lockers nearby.

The station is under renovation until about 2028, and JR Hokkaido is rearranging the lockers and benches outside the gates. If a bank has moved, the tourist information centre in the West Concourse keeps a current list.

All full? Plan B, C and D

  1. Hand over Yamato Transport, Sapporo-ekimae office. Kita 3 Nishi 1, Sun Memoria Building 1F, a few blocks south of the station. ¥500 if you collect the bag the same day, ¥1,000 if you collect it later. Yamato runs it with Jotetsu Bus for travellers heading out to Jozankei, so check Yamato's baggage page for hours and who it serves before you walk over.
  2. Book ecbo cloak or Bounce. Book a spot online in a shop or office near the station (ecbo's partners include 7-Eleven and Yamato branches) and show the QR code when you arrive. Large suitcases are fine. Prices and hours vary by spot.
  3. Send it Same-day delivery. In winter 2026 the West Concourse centre sent bags to hotels in central Sapporo, Jozankei and New Chitose Airport from ¥1,000 (book ahead). Between the airport and city hotels, Sapporo's tourism site lists delivery from ¥1,870.
  4. Ask Your hotel. The tourist information centre's own advice: lock up early, or leave bags at your hotel before or after check-in.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Snow Festival 2027 (4–11 February): expect the lockers to go even faster. In 2026 a temporary luggage desk opened in the West Concourse from 20 January to 28 February (¥1,000 per item); the 2027 desk hasn't been announced yet, and we'll add it here when it is.
  • Booking window: only the Multi E-Cube lockers can be reserved, and you need a free Multi E-Cube account first.
  • Renovation: locker banks outside the gates are being moved until about 2028, so a map from last year may be wrong.

One more thing: lockers that open for free in a disaster

Since 1 July 2025, JR Sapporo has had "disaster-response" coin lockers, which its maker says were Japan's first. In a disaster they can be used free of charge, so people who have to leave the station quickly can lock their bags away and evacuate with their hands free. If the station ever has to be cleared, those are the lockers to look for.

Quick answers

How much are coin lockers at Sapporo Station?

Sapporo's official tourism site lists about ¥300–400 for a small locker, ¥400–500 for a medium one and ¥600–700 for a large one, per use or per day, for up to three days. The 36 Multi E-Cube lockers by the 1F East gate cost ¥400, ¥500 and ¥700, cashless. Checked 27 November 2026.

Can I reserve a coin locker at Sapporo Station?

Yes, but only the 36 Multi E-Cube lockers outside the 1F East gate. Book on the Multi E-Cube website (free membership) for ¥500 on top of the locker fee; they take IC cards, QR payment and credit cards.

Is there staffed luggage storage at Sapporo Station?

Not all year. The station's tourist information centre says JR Sapporo has no baggage room and it doesn't hold bags itself; in winter 2026 it ran a temporary desk (20 Jan–28 Feb, ¥1,000 per item). Nearby, Yamato's Sapporo-ekimae office holds bags for ¥500 if you collect them the same day.

What if all the lockers at Sapporo Station are full?

Try the lockers in the APIA underground mall, by Daimaru's basement entrance and at the subway's Namboku and Toho line gates. Or book storage with ecbo cloak or Bounce, leave it at Yamato's office near the station, or send the case to your hotel.
