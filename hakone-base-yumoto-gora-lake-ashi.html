One Night in Hakone Without a Car: Yumoto, Gora or Lake Ashi?

Three bases, the same Wednesday night, all on Booking.com. We checked the walk with luggage, where you can still eat, and the last bus.

YumotoGoraLake Ashi

For one car-free night we suggest Hakone-Yumoto if you arrive after about 17:00 or leave early: the Romancecar ends there, our sample hotel is 150 m from the platform (¥16,800 for two) and dinner runs to a 21:30 last order. Choose Gora (¥18,540 with breakfast, 100 m from the station) if your day is the Open-Air Museum or the ropeway side. Choose Lake Ashi (¥18,225 with breakfast and a lake view) only if you'll be there by mid-afternoon: the last weekday bus up from Yumoto is 20:48 and lakeside kitchens close early.

Answer A · Hakone-Yumoto

Off the train and into the room in minutes

¥16,800 · 150 m

Arrival with luggage: Yumoto is where the Romancecar from Shinjuku terminates, so there is no change with bags. Yumoto Station Hotel MIRAHAKONE is 150 m from the station; for Wed 16 Dec it showed an economy double with a private shower at ¥16,800 room only (a 30% deal on ¥24,000), and check-in runs until 23:30.

Dinner: this is the only base where a late arrival still has choice on foot. Kinosuke, 2 minutes from the station, serves charcoal-grilled dishes 17:30–22:00, last orders 21:30, closed Mondays. Next morning: the Hakone Tozan train climbs to Gora in about 36 minutes, and buses to Moto-Hakone on Lake Ashi take about 32 minutes from stop 2. The cost of the convenience is that every sight is uphill from here.

Answer B · Gora

Halfway up, next to the museum and the cable car

¥18,540 with breakfast

Access to what you came for: the Hakone Open-Air Museum is one stop down the line (2 minutes by train, then a 2-minute walk), and the cable car to Sounzan and the ropeway to Owakudani start in Gora. Note the works: Odakyu Hakone replaces the Owakudani–Sounzan ropeway section with buses from 7 to 24 December 2026 (about every 15 minutes), and the Togendai–Owakudani section from 12 January to 26 February 2027.

The final walk: pick a hotel near the station, because Gora is built on a slope. Sundance Resort Hakone Gora is 100 m from Gora Station; on 16 Dec a Japanese-style room with breakfast (shared bathroom) was ¥18,540 + ¥300 tax at Booking's members-only price, or ¥32,040 with dinner too. Check-in runs 15:00–20:00 and the hotel wants your arrival time. Further up the hill, ryokan such as Kiritani Hakoneso send a car if you phone from the station.

Answer C · Lake Ashi

The lake in the morning, if you arrive early

¥18,225 · lake view

The value: you wake up where day-trippers end their loop. RoheN ASHINOKO, 300 m from Moto-Hakone, showed a lake-view double with a private bathroom and breakfast for ¥18,225 on 16 Dec (members-only price; standard ¥22,500), almost the Gora price with the lake outside. Hakone Shrine, with its torii in the water, is about a 10-minute walk from the Hakone-jinja-iriguchi bus stop.

The catch is the evening. Weekday buses from Hakone-Yumoto (stop 2) after 17:00 leave at 17:28, 17:58, 18:28, 18:58, 19:48 and a last 20:48, which reaches Moto-Hakone-ko at 21:20 (¥1,210). Food closes earlier: La Terrazza Ashinoko takes last orders at 19:00 (closed 2nd and 4th Tuesdays), and most lakeside lunch places shut between 16:00 and 18:00. Arriving after dark, book a hotel with its own restaurant (RoheN's listing has one) or eat in Yumoto first.

Our verdict

We suggest: Hakone-Yumoto for most one-night, car-free trips with luggage. It had the lowest room price of our three on this date (room only), steps from the Romancecar, and the only base with dinner on foot until 21:30. Switch to Gora if your day is built around the Open-Air Museum or the ropeway and you'll check in by about 18:00. Switch to Lake Ashi only if you can be there by mid-afternoon: after about 19:00 the lake has no dinner and only two buses left. In December 2026 the ropeway's upper section runs as a bus from 7 to 24 Dec, so allow extra time if you cross from Gora to the lake.

One more thing: breakfast with your feet in the onsen

Staying by the lake? Bakery & Table Hakone at Moto-Hakone opens at 9:00 every day, and its terrace has a foot bath fed by Lake Ashi's hot spring. Buy bread downstairs, sit on the edge with your feet in the warm water and look across the lake; the upstairs café also looks over Lake Ashi (last orders 16:30). Hakone Navi suggests its curry doughnut made with rice flour.

Quick answers

Is Hakone-Yumoto a good base for one night in Hakone?

For a car-free night with luggage, yes: the Romancecar stops there, hotels such as Yumoto Station Hotel MIRAHAKONE are 150 m from the station, and a dinner spot like Kinosuke takes last orders at 21:30, 2 minutes from the station. The trade-off is that the sights are 30–40 minutes uphill.

How do I get from Hakone-Yumoto to Lake Ashi in the evening?

By Hakone Tozan Bus from stop 2 at Hakone-Yumoto Station. On weekdays after 17:00 buses leave at 17:28, 17:58, 18:28, 18:58, 19:48 and a last one at 20:48, which reaches Moto-Hakone-ko at 21:20 (¥1,210).

Is the Hakone Ropeway running in December 2026?

Partly. Odakyu Hakone suspends the Owakudani–Sounzan section from 7 to 24 December 2026 and the Togendai–Ubako–Owakudani section from 12 January to 26 February 2027 for maintenance. Replacement buses run about every 15 minutes for ticket holders.

Where can I eat dinner at Lake Ashi?

Choices are thin in the evening. At Moto-Hakone, La Terrazza Ashinoko serves pizza until 20:00 with last orders at 19:00 (closed 2nd and 4th Tuesdays); most lakeside lunch places close between 16:00 and 18:00. Arriving later, book a hotel with its own restaurant.
