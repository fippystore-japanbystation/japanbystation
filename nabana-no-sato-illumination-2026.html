Nabana no Sato Illumination 2026: Dates, Tickets and the Trip from Nagoya or Osaka

💡 The quick answer

Nabana no Sato's winter lights run from 17 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, so any evening this winter works except 6–7 January.

A 200 m tunnel of light, a warm flower greenhouse to wait in, and a ticket that pays you back ¥1,000. Official 2026 prices and the easiest way there.

Dates & pricesGetting thereOne evening

Nabana no Sato's winter lights run from 17 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, so any evening this winter works except 6–7 January. In December a ticket is ¥3,000 in advance (¥3,200 at the gate), and ¥1,000 of that comes back as a voucher for food and shopping inside. From Nagoya it is a 30–35 minute direct bus; from Osaka it's a long but doable evening with Kintetsu's set ticket. If you can, go on a weekday and skip 23–25 December, when prices rise to ¥5,500.

One adult price applies from elementary-school age; preschool children go free with an adult. The park opens at 10:00 and the lights come on around sunset (the time shifts daily). In November it closed at 21:00 on weekdays and 22:00 on weekends and holidays, with last entry 30 minutes before; check the official calendar for your date. Closed 6–7 January 2027.

What makes it special

  • Hana Kairo, the tunnel: about 200 m of light overhead, the picture everyone comes for.
  • The big theme area: this season's show, "Wild Paradise" according to Mie's tourism board, runs about 10 and a half minutes with light and sound.
  • River of Light and the Twin Tree: lights over water, and a tree that lights up when the chapel bell rings.
  • Autumn leaves are lit from late November to late December, so a December visit gets both.

The ¥1,000 voucher, and what it won't pay for

Every ticket includes a voucher (¥1,000, or ¥2,000 at Christmas) for food and shopping inside the park, which turns dinner into part of the ticket price. It gives no change and has an expiry date. It doesn't work at the Island Fuji observation tower (¥800 for adults, cash only, a 7-minute ride up and round 45 m), the flower market or vending machines. Island Fuji stops in bad weather.

Getting there

From Nagoya, bus: Meitetsu's direct bus leaves from Meitetsu Bus Center, 4F, stop 22 (tickets on 3F), about 30–35 minutes, ¥1,200 one way or ¥2,400 return until 13 December 2026; fares rise from 14 December. No reservations, IC cards accepted, seated passengers only, so the bus can fill up.

From Nagoya, train: Kintetsu to Kintetsu-Nagashima (about 17 minutes by express, which stops there on weekends and holidays from 31 October to 11 January and on 23–25 December), then the Sanco bus, about 10 minutes, ¥250. Kintetsu's Nabana set ticket bundles both ways by train and bus plus admission and the voucher: from Kintetsu-Nagoya ¥3,670 (dates up to 20 November and from 1 March) or ¥4,170 (21 November to 28 February).

From Osaka: the same set ticket from Osaka-Namba costs ¥7,430 or ¥7,930, valid two days, limited express surcharge extra. It isn't sold for 23–25 December.

Driving? Parking is free (about 5,700 spaces), but the roads home jam badly. The park suggests arriving before 16:00 on weekdays (15:00 at weekends) and leaving after 19:00 (20:00 at weekends).

Which visit suits you

  • Families: arrive mid-afternoon, Begonia Garden first, an early dinner on the voucher, lights at dusk, home before the late buses fill.
  • Couples: a weekday in December, after the first rush at lighting time; the Christmas nights are the busiest of the year.
  • Short on time in Nagoya: the direct bus, two to three hours inside (the park's own estimate), back for a late dinner in the city.

One evening at Nabana no Sato

  1. 15:00 Bus from Meitetsu Bus Center (or Kintetsu to Nagashima).
  2. 15:40 Begonia Garden while it's light: about 12,000 begonias in a heated greenhouse.
  3. 16:15 Early dinner on your voucher. Restaurants get busy and may stop seating early around lighting time.
  4. 17:00 Lights on around sunset: let the first crowd pass, then walk the Hana Kairo tunnel.
  5. 18:00 The theme-area show, then the River of Light.
  6. 19:00 Bus back to Nagoya.

🚌 Rather not juggle buses and tickets? A bus tour from Nagoya to the outlet and the illumination takes care of the ride there and back.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Christmas (23–25 Dec): date-designated advance tickets only, ¥5,500; gate sales only if any remain. Discount tickets don't apply.
  • Advance tickets from FamilyMart and 7-Eleven (and Asoview e-tickets) are ¥200 cheaper than the gate and go straight through without exchange. Printing at the park can take 30+ minutes.
  • No refunds or changes on advance tickets, and hotel guests are told not to buy them separately.
  • Closed 6–7 January 2027.

Where to stay

Nagoya Station (Meieki) is the easy base: the Meitetsu Bus Center and Kintetsu are both there, and you're on the Shinkansen next morning. Come back from the lights to a room by the Shinkansen →

Prices can differ between sites, so also check hotels in Nagoya on Klook.

One more thing: wait for the lights in the greenhouse

The park's own advice is to spend the hour before lighting inside the Begonia Garden, which is heated to about 24–26°C and included in your ticket. Crowds peak at the moment the lights come on, so the calm move is to warm up among the flowers, eat early, and walk into the tunnel 20–30 minutes after lighting.

Quick answers

When is the Nabana no Sato illumination 2026?

From 17 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, its 23rd season. The park is closed on 6 and 7 January 2027. It opens at 10:00 and the lights come on around sunset; in November it closed at 21:00 on weekdays and 22:00 on weekends and holidays, with last entry 30 minutes before.

How much is Nabana no Sato in December 2026?

From 21 November to 22 December: ¥3,000 with an advance ticket or ¥3,200 at the gate, including a ¥1,000 voucher for food and shopping inside and entry to the Begonia Garden. From 23 to 25 December it is ¥5,500 with date-designated advance tickets and ¥2,000 in vouchers. Preschool children go free with an adult.

How do I get to Nabana no Sato from Nagoya?

The easiest is the direct Meitetsu bus from Meitetsu Bus Center (4F, stop 22): about 30–35 minutes, ¥1,200 one way until 13 December 2026 (new fares from 14 December), no reservations. Or take Kintetsu to Kintetsu-Nagashima and the Sanco bus (about 10 minutes, ¥250); the Kintetsu set ticket includes trains, buses and admission.

Can I go to Nabana no Sato from Osaka?

Yes, as a long evening trip. Kintetsu sells a set ticket from Osaka-Namba with round-trip trains to Kintetsu-Nagashima, the bus and admission for ¥7,430 or ¥7,930 depending on the date (limited express surcharge extra). It is not sold for 23–25 December.
