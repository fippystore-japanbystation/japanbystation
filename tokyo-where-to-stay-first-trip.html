First Trip to Tokyo: Which Area Should You Stay In?

💡 The quick answer

Shinjuku for a mixed first trip, because nearly every line and the Narita Express are at the door and well-rated rooms started around ¥12,500 for two.

Why we say this ↓

First time in Tokyo and not sure where to sleep? There are three good bases. Here's what each cost for two on the same night, and the train times that decide it.

Shinjuku / ShibuyaTokyo Station / GinzaAsakusa / Ueno

For most first trips, we'd pick Shinjuku. It has the most train lines, the Narita Express and food until late, and a well-rated double for two started around ¥12,500 on our sample weeknight. Taking the Shinkansen two or more times? Stay near Tokyo Station. Keeping it under about ¥16,000 a night for two, or landing at Narita and riding the Skyliner? Pick Asakusa.

Answer A · For nightlife, shopping and the most trains

Shinjuku (or Shibuya)

middle ≈¥18,000 for two

Why Shinjuku? Because most of the city is one ride away. JR, Keio, Odakyu, Tokyo Metro and Toei all meet here, and Guinness World Records counts it as the world's busiest station (about 2.7 million passengers a day in 2022). Tokyo Station is 14 minutes on the JR Chuo Rapid Line (¥253 by IC card), and the Narita Express stops here too. On our date, the first 20 well-rated hotels started at about ¥12,500 and the middle price was about ¥18,100. Two examples: Rosenheim Tokyo Shinjuku (rated 9.2) at ¥15,145 and Villa Fontaine Tokyo Shinjuku (8.5) at ¥18,434. Shibuya cost more on the same night.

Answer B · For Shinkansen day trips and quieter evenings

Tokyo Station / Ginza / Nihonbashi

middle ≈¥22,000 for two

Planning day trips by bullet train? Then this is your base. Six Shinkansen lines start at Tokyo Station (Tokaido toward Kyoto and Osaka, Tohoku, Hokkaido, Akita, Yamagata, Joetsu and Hokuriku), so a 7 a.m. train to Kyoto is a short walk, not a ride across town. The fastest Narita Express gets here in 53 minutes, and Ueno is 8 minutes away on JR. The catch? Price. Within 1 km of the station the middle price was about ¥22,000, and about ¥25,000 in Ginza. The better deals were a little further out, like Tosei Hotel Cocone Kanda (rated 8.5, 900 m) at about ¥14,000 with tax.

Answer C · For lower prices and the Skyliner

Asakusa (or Ueno)

middle ≈¥15,800 in Asakusa

Watching the budget? Asakusa was the cheapest of the three on our date. The middle price was about ¥15,800, with Tosei Hotel Cocone Asakusa Kuramae (rated 8.6) at ¥13,860 and The Kanzashi Tokyo Asakusa (8.8) at ¥15,048. Ueno wasn't cheaper than Shinjuku that night (middle about ¥18,400 within 1 km). But it's where the Keisei Skyliner from Narita ends, about 41–57 minutes from the airport. From Ueno, Asakusa is 5 minutes on the Ginza Line, and from Asakusa, Shibuya is 34 minutes direct.

Our verdict

We suggest: Shinjuku for a mixed first trip, because nearly every line and the Narita Express are at the door and well-rated rooms started around ¥12,500 for two. Switch to Tokyo Station as soon as you have two or more Shinkansen day trips, since you save a cross-town ride each early morning. Switch to Asakusa when your limit for two is under about ¥16,000 a night on your dates, or when you arrive at Narita and want the Skyliner to Ueno.

One more thing: Tokyo's kitchen street

If you stay on the Asakusa/Ueno side, spend an hour on Kappabashi, an 800 m street of about 170 shops that supply Tokyo's restaurants. You'll find chefs' knives, lacquerware, noren curtains and the plastic food samples you see in every restaurant window. People come for the range rather than low prices. Go on a weekday: more than half the shops close on Sundays.

Quick answers

Where should I stay in Tokyo for the first time?

For most first trips we suggest Shinjuku: it is 14 minutes from Tokyo Station on the JR Chuo Rapid Line, has the Narita Express, and on our sample weeknight a well-rated double started around ¥12,500 for two. Choose Tokyo Station if you plan two or more Shinkansen day trips, and Asakusa if keeping the room under about ¥16,000 matters most.

Is Ueno or Asakusa cheaper than Shinjuku?

Asakusa was, Ueno was not. On Wed 16 Dec 2026 the middle price of the first 20 hotels rated 7+ for two adults was about ¥15,800 in Asakusa, ¥18,100 in Shinjuku and ¥18,400 within 1 km of Ueno Station.

Is it worth staying near Tokyo Station?

Yes if you will use the Shinkansen often. Six Shinkansen lines start there, Ueno is 8 minutes away and Shinjuku 14 minutes. You pay for it: the middle price within 1 km was about ¥22,000 for two on our date, and evenings are quieter.

How long is the train from Asakusa to Shinjuku?

About 26–28 minutes with one change, for example the Toei Asakusa Line and Shinjuku Line (about ¥272 by IC card). Shibuya is 34 minutes direct on the Ginza Line.
