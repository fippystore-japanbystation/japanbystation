One Hotel Night Near Tokyo Disney: Before or After?

💡 The quick answer

Match the night to the hotel type. A Disney hotel: the night before, because Happy Entry never works on the check-in day.

Why we say this ↓

It depends on which kind of hotel you book. We read the official Happy Entry rules and priced the same nights at Maihama and in central Tokyo.

Night beforeNight afterStay in Tokyo

If the room is at one of the six Disney hotels, we suggest the night before: their early entry, Happy Entry, works on every day of your stay except the day you check in. If it is an Official Hotel such as the Hilton or Sheraton, there is no early entry either way, so we'd spend the night after, when the park closes at 21:00 and a tired child is one monorail ride and a free shuttle from bed. And if your Tokyo hotel is near the Keiyo Line, keeping it can cost about the same.

Answer A · The night before

Sleep there first, if it's a Disney hotel

Happy Entry: 15 min early

Tokyo Disney Resort's own rule is the whole answer here. Guests at a Disney hotel can enter Tokyo Disneyland 15 minutes before other guests through a special entrance, and the benefit applies on every day of the stay, including check-out day, except the day of check-in. The six hotels listed for Tokyo Disneyland include the Tokyo Disney Resort Toy Story Hotel and the Tokyo Disney Celebration Hotel. Each guest aged 4 or over gets a pass.

Two honest limits from the same page: it "does not guarantee" a full 15 minutes if the hotel entrance is busy, and a few dates are excluded for some hotels (at Tokyo Disneyland, 12 January 2027 for MiraCosta, Fantasy Chateau and Celebration Hotel guests). The time you save on the commute is real too: Tokyo Disney Resort gives about 20 minutes from Tokyo Station and 40 minutes from Shinjuku to the park.

Answer B · The night after

Official Hotel: use it for the 21:00 exit

Monorail + free shuttle

Tokyo Disney Resort's list of Official Hotel benefits has a free shuttle, ticket help and baggage services, but no early entry. So the morning is the same whether you slept at Maihama or not, and the hotel is worth more at night. After the 21:00 closing you ride the Disney Resort Line to Bayside Station (the monorail loops its four stations in about 13 minutes; ¥300 adult, ¥150 child, last train 23:30) and take the free Disney Resort Cruiser bus to the hotel.

Prices for 2 adults and a 6-year-old on Thu 17 Dec 2026 on Booking.com: Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay ¥40,000 (Ocean Dream Room, 3 beds), Hilton Tokyo Bay ¥43,505 (Twin Hilton Room Park), Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay ¥40,117 (Deluxe). A Disney hotel the night after works too, but you pay Disney hotel rates for proximity only: no Happy Entry on the check-in day.

Answer C · Both nights in Tokyo

Keep one room on the Keiyo Line

Hatchobori ≈ same price

Besides Tokyo Station, consider Hatchobori, the first Keiyo Line stop after Tokyo on the way to Maihama. You board one stop closer than Tokyo Station, and you never repack: the suitcases stay in one room for the whole trip. Tokyo Disney Resort puts Maihama about 15 minutes by train from Tokyo Station, so the ride from Hatchobori is a little shorter.

What surprised us: on these dates it was not much cheaper. ICI Hotel Tokyo Hatchobori was ¥39,600 on Wed 16 Dec and ¥37,950 on Thu 17 Dec, against ¥43,000 and ¥40,000 at the Sheraton. So the case for C is no move with the bags, not the money. One exception: Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay had a Black Friday deal at ¥27,594 for Wed 16 Dec; deals like that change the maths, so check your own dates.

Our verdict

We suggest: match the night to the hotel type. A Disney hotel: the night before, because Happy Entry never works on the check-in day. An Official Hotel: the night after, because it has no early entry and its value is the short ride home after the 21:00 closing. The answer changes if your central hotel is on the Keiyo Line and within a few thousand yen of Maihama (then keep it and skip the move), or if a deal makes a Maihama room much cheaper on one of the two nights.

One more thing: check out without the suitcase

If you sleep at an Official Hotel the night before, you do not have to carry your bags around the park or back to the station. Tokyo Disney Resort's Station Delivery Service (fee required) lets you leave them at the hotel when you check out, between 7:00 and 12:30, and pick them up at the counter on the street level of the Tokyo Disney Resort Welcome Center, next to JR Maihama Station, when you leave. It works in reverse on arrival: a counter on the station level sends your bags to the hotel.

Pick-up time depends on when you dropped the bags off. Source: Tokyo Disney Resort, Guest Benefits at Hotels Affiliated with Tokyo Disney Resort, checked 23 November 2026.

Quick answers

Do Disney hotel guests get early entry on the day they check in?

No. Tokyo Disney Resort says Happy Entry can be used on any day of the stay, including check-out day, except the day of check-in. So to use it on your park day, sleep at the Disney hotel the night before.

Do Tokyo Disney Resort Official Hotels like the Hilton or Sheraton get early entry?

The official list of Official Hotel benefits does not include early entry. It lists a free shuttle bus to Bayside Station on the Disney Resort Line, help buying park tickets, and baggage services. Happy Entry is for the six Disney hotels.

How long does it take from Tokyo Station to Tokyo Disneyland?

Tokyo Disney Resort gives about 15 minutes by JR from Tokyo Station to Maihama Station, and about 20 minutes in total to Tokyo Disneyland. From Shinjuku it gives about 40 minutes.

Is a hotel at Maihama much more expensive than central Tokyo?

Not on the dates we checked. For 2 adults and a 6-year-old on Wed 16 Dec 2026, Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay was ¥43,000 and ICI Hotel Tokyo Hatchobori ¥39,600 on Booking.com. On Thu 17 Dec it was ¥40,000 vs ¥37,950.
