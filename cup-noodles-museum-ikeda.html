Cup Noodles Museum Ikeda: Design a Cup, Make Noodles, or Just Explore?

Three ways to do the museum where instant ramen was born, and which one suits your visit.

Design a cupMake noodlesJust explore

The museum itself is free and about 20 minutes from Umeda. What costs money, and sometimes planning, are the two hands-on experiences. For a short visit we suggest designing your own cup (¥500, no booking). Book the Chicken Ramen workshop only if you want 70 minutes of real cooking.

Hours, prices and booking rules checked 23 November 2026 against the museum's official site and osaka-info.jp

Answer A · For a short visit

My CUPNOODLES Factory

¥500 per cup

Draw on your own cup, then pick one of four soups and four of twelve toppings, and the staff seal it for you to take home. No reservation: numbered tickets are handed out on the day, so arrive earlier on weekends and holidays.

Answer B · For hands-on cooking

Chicken Ramen Factory

70 min · book ahead

You knead, roll, steam and season the dough, then flash-fry it into instant noodles yourself. Sessions run five times a day (9:40, 10:50, 12:00, 14:00 and 15:10 at the time of checking) and must be reserved in advance on the museum's booking site. Age limits apply; check them when you book.

Answer C · If both are full

The free museum

no ticket needed

Walk through the Instant Noodles Tunnel lined with about 800 packages, see the reproduction of the backyard shed where Chicken Ramen was invented in 1958, and watch the CUPNOODLES Drama Theater. The museum shop sells limited items. Some listings note cards are not accepted, so bring cash.

Our verdict

We suggest: for most visitors, the ¥500 cup is the right pick. It needs no booking and fits a half day. Book the Chicken Ramen Factory only if you can commit to a session time. Avoid Tuesdays, when the museum is closed.

Quick answers

Do I need to book the Cup Noodles Museum in Ikeda?

Entry is free and needs no booking. The Chicken Ramen Factory workshop must be reserved in advance online. My CUPNOODLES Factory needs no reservation: you collect a numbered ticket on the day.

How much does it cost to make your own Cup Noodles?

My CUPNOODLES Factory costs ¥500 per cup. The Chicken Ramen Factory costs ¥1,200 for junior high students and older and ¥600 for elementary school students.

When is the Cup Noodles Museum Osaka Ikeda closed?

It opens 9:30 to 16:30 with last admission at 15:30, and closes on Tuesdays (the next day if Tuesday is a holiday) and over New Year.

How do I get to the Cup Noodles Museum from Umeda?

Take the Hankyu Takarazuka Line to Ikeda Station, about 20 minutes by express from Osaka-umeda, then walk about 5 minutes from the Masumi-cho exit.
