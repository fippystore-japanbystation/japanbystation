Two of the best meals at Shibuya Station are upstairs, not out on the crossing. Hikarie 8F has set meals from all 47 prefectures, and Mark City 4F has sit-down sushi, both connected to the station. For something older, walk three minutes from the Hachiko exit to Nonbei Yokocho, where Torifuku has grilled yakitori since 1932. And at 4am, Bura Bura on Dogenzaka is still serving. All eight picks had 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked, and the hours come from their own listings.

Who this list suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Rainy days: two picks are inside the station buildings
  • Solo travellers: noodle counters, a yakitori counter, a coffee bar
  • Night owls: one pick serves until 7:00

Think twice

  • Halal or vegetarian diets: none of these lists it officially
  • Card-only travellers: three picks take cash only
  • Wednesday lunch: Kiraku is closed, and d47 lists no Wednesday hours
We suggest: a regional set meal at d47 Shokudo for lunch, yakitori at Torifuku for an early dinner, and Hatou for coffee before the last train. Looking for the best view of the crossing first? See where to watch the Shibuya Scramble.

A slow coffee in a park before Shibuya wakes up (from 8:00)

  • Blue Bottle Coffee Shibuya Cafe (ブルーボトルコーヒー 渋谷カフェ), Kitaya Park: a two-floor café inside a small public park in Jinnan, about 8 minutes north of the Hachiko exit (500 m). Open every day 8:00–20:00, with 10 seats downstairs, 33 upstairs and benches outside.
  • Up very early? Bura Bura on Dogenzaka is still open until 7:00 (last order 6:30). See late night.

A lunch from somewhere else in Japan, without leaving the station

  • d47 Shokudo (d47食堂), Shibuya Hikarie 8F: set meals built on local dishes from all 47 prefectures, such as aji-fry from Matsuura port (¥1,300) or an oyster-rice set from Oku (¥1,050). Lunch 11:30–16:00 (last order 15:00), dinner 18:00–21:00 (last order 20:00). The listing shows no Wednesday hours, so don't plan on Wednesday. Cards, IC cards and QR payments accepted.
  • Sushi no Midori (梅丘寿司の美登利), Mark City East Mall 4F: sit-down sushi in the building above the Keio Inokashira Line. The chef's seasonal omakase nigiri is ¥4,180–4,400. Weekdays 11:00–15:00 and 17:00–21:00, weekends 11:00–21:00 straight through; last order 20:30. Reservations are taken, mostly for tables.
  • Chukamenten Kiraku (中華麺店 喜楽), Dogenzaka: a two-floor noodle shop about 3 minutes from the station, serving chuka-men, a soy-based ramen, for about ¥1,000. Open 11:30–20:30, closed Wednesdays. Cash only.

Yakitori in a 1950s alley, then seafood until 23:00

  • Torifuku (鳥福), Nonbei Yokocho: a yakitori counter that started as a Shibuya food stall in 1932 and moved into Nonbei Yokocho, the old drinking alley beside the tracks, in 1950. There are 13 seats over two floors, so leave big luggage at the hotel. Mon–Fri 16:00–21:00; Saturday 16:00–20:00 by reservation only, in two sittings; closed Sundays, holidays and the third Saturday. About ¥6,000–8,000 a head, cash only.
  • Uoshin (魚真), Dogenzaka: a seafood izakaya serving Toyosu market fish as sashimi, sushi, grilled, simmered and tempura. Its signature is "nokke" sushi, topped with sea urchin and crab. Open every night 17:00–23:00, about 6 minutes from the Hachiko exit. About ¥5,000–6,000; cards and QR payments accepted.

Late-night coffee, and noodles when the clubs let out

  • Chatei Hatou (茶亭 羽當), East exit side: a hand-poured coffee house about 3 minutes from the station, open 11:00–23:00 (last order 22:00). The house blend is ¥900. Cash only, no reservations.
  • Bura Bura (油そば専門店ぶらぶら), Dogenzaka: abura soba, noodles with sauce and oil instead of soup, 2 minutes from the station. Open 11:00 to 7:00 the next morning (last order 6:30). Eight seats.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Torifuku on Saturday is reservation only, and it's closed on Sundays, holidays and 29 December–5 January.
  • Wednesday: Kiraku is closed, and d47 Shokudo lists no Wednesday hours.
  • Cash: keep yen for Kiraku, Torifuku and Hatou; the other five take cards.

One more thing: the bowl that's still served when the first trains run

Long after Shibuya's last trains have gone, Bura Bura on Dogenzaka keeps cooking, until 7:00 in the morning. Abura soba has no soup: you mix the sauce and oil through the noodles, then add vinegar and garlic to taste. Regular, large and extra-large bowls all cost the same, so order big if you've been dancing.

Quick answers

Where can I eat breakfast near Shibuya Station?

Blue Bottle Coffee's Shibuya Cafe in Kitaya Park opens at 8:00 every day, about 8 minutes on foot from the Hachiko exit. If you're up before that, Bura Bura on Dogenzaka serves abura soba until 7:00 (last order 6:30). Checked 26 November 2026.

Where can I eat late at night near Shibuya Station?

Bura Bura, 2 minutes from the station on Dogenzaka, is open 11:00 to 7:00 the next morning. Uoshin's seafood izakaya and Chatei Hatou's coffee house both stay open until 23:00 (Hatou's last order is 22:00).

Is there good food inside Shibuya Station's buildings?

Yes. d47 Shokudo on Shibuya Hikarie 8F serves set meals from every prefecture of Japan, and Sushi no Midori on Mark City East Mall 4F serves sit-down sushi from 11:00. Both are connected to the station, so you don't have to go outside.

Is there halal or vegetarian food near Shibuya Station?

None of the eight picks on this page lists halal or vegetarian dishes on its own pages, so we don't tag them. Check a current halal guide or ask the tourist information centre in the station.
