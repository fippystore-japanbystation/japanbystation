The Munich Christmas Market

Sapporo and Munich are sister cities, and the market began in 2002 as part of that friendship. In 2026 it fills Odori 1-chome, with a live-wood symbol tree (the "Richterbaum") ringed by four wooden arches, Santa greetings, workshops and a stage, and Odori 2-chome, the food and gift block, with a new "Twinkle Lounge" in the middle where you can sit with something warm.

  • What to drink: Glühwein, including from a stall that sells nothing else, plus hot chocolate, and beer from the Sapporo Beer Garden stall.
  • What to eat: sausages, pretzels, German stews and roasted almonds from a stall that traces its recipe back to 1930.
  • What to take home: German tin and wood crafts, candles, snow globes, and the yearly mug, which changes design every year.
  • Paying: every stall takes cashless payment (cards, e-money, QR).

The White Illumination, and what's new

The organisers call it Japan's first illumination: it began in 1981 with about 1,000 bulbs in Odori 2-chome. Today Odori gets the big light sculptures, while the trees along Ekimae-dori, Akapla (the plaza in front of the Red Brick Office) and Minami 1-jo are wrapped in lights. Minami 1-jo alone has 46 ginkgo trees and about 70,000 LEDs along roughly 300 m.

Two venues join in 2026. The Red Brick Office, the former Hokkaido Government building, is bathed in blue light. And from 9 to 28 January, Nakajima Park hosts "SNOWFISH" by the art group ENESS: big balloon creatures with large eyes whose light and sound react to people, some of which you can walk inside.

One evening light walk (until 25 December)

  1. 16:30 Start at Sapporo Station's South Plaza as the lights come on.
  2. 16:50 Akapla and the blue Red Brick Office, a few minutes' walk away.
  3. 17:30 Walk down Ekimae-dori under the trees, or take the Chi-Ka-Ho underground walk (about 10 minutes from the station) if it's snowing hard.
  4. 18:00 Odori 2-chome for dinner at the market: Glühwein first, then sausages. Last orders 20:45.
  5. 20:00 Odori's light sculptures, then Minami 1-jo's ginkgo tunnel on the way to Susukino.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • The market and the Odori lights both end on 25 December. Arriving later? The street venues stay lit into February and March, and Nakajima Park opens 9 January.
  • 23–25 December: Odori stays lit until midnight (per Weathernews), the busiest nights of the season.
  • Dress for snow: it's an outdoor walk. Shoes with good grip help on packed snow, and the underground passages are your shortcut between station and park.
  • Hotels: most Sapporo listings offer free cancellation, so holding a room near Odori costs nothing if plans change.

One more thing: eat first, light-walk last

The market's last order is 20:45, but the Odori lights stay on until 22:00, and until midnight on 23–25 December. So do it in that order: dinner at the huts while they're open, then walk the park after the stalls close, when the crowds thin and the sculptures have the snow to themselves.

Quick answers

When is the Sapporo White Illumination 2026?

The Odori Park venue runs from 20 November to 25 December 2026. Other venues stay lit longer: Ekimae-dori until 11 February 2027, the Red Brick Office until 28 February, and Sapporo Station South Plaza, Akapla and Minami 1-jo until 14 March 2027.

What are the hours of the Sapporo Christmas Market 2026?

The Munich Christmas Market in Sapporo is open 11:00–21:00 daily (last order 20:45) from 20 November to 25 December 2026, in Odori Park 1-chome and 2-chome.

What time do the Odori lights switch on?

Weathernews lists the Odori venue as lit 16:30–22:00, and until midnight on 23, 24 and 25 December. Check the official site for any change.

Can I pay by card at the Sapporo Christmas Market?

Yes. The organisers say cashless payment (credit cards, e-money and QR codes) is accepted at all venues; brands may change.
