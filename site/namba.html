Osaka · Namba

Things to Do Around Namba

Everything worth a stop within a 15-minute walk of Namba Station, with the walking time to each.

The canalEatShop

Namba is the easiest part of Osaka to explore on foot. Leave Namba Station by Exit 14 and the canal, the food streets and the shopping arcades are all within a few minutes. Here is how it fits together.

Walking times from Osaka Metro Namba Station · checked 22 November 2026

Part 1 · The canal

Dotonbori

3–5 min on foot

The canal is the heart of Namba. Giant 3D signs hang over the restaurants, the Glico running man glows above Ebisu Bridge, and the whole strip gets brighter after dark.

Part 2 · Eat

Street food and markets

Osaka calls itself Japan's kitchen, and Namba is where you taste it. Eat standing up, try small portions, and move on to the next stall.

≈8 minon foot

Kuromon Market

Covered market with about 150 shops. Grilled seafood, wagyu skewers, fruit. Best before noon.

3–5 minon foot

Takoyaki

Osaka's octopus balls, cooked to order all along Dotonbori. Let them cool a minute.

5–6 minby subway

Kushikatsu in Shinsekai

For deep-fried skewers, ride two stops south. One rule: don't double dip.

One more thing: the lion that swallows bad luck

Ten minutes west of the Namba crowds, in a quiet residential street, is Namba Yasaka Shrine, the local guardian shrine of Namba. Its stage is a 12 m lion's head with a gaping mouth, and the lion is said to swallow evil spirits and bring luck, which is why students and shop owners come to pray here. It's free, and early morning is the quiet time to see it.

Namba Yasaka Shrine, 2-9-19 Motomachi, Naniwa-ku; about 6–10 min from Namba Station. Open daily from early morning to about 17:00, free. Sources: JR West (New Golden Route), GLTJP (supervised by the shrine), Japan Cheapo.
