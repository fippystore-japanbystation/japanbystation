Food Near Osaka Station (Umeda): Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and a Late Supermarket

You don't have to leave the station to eat well here: coffee from 7:00 in an alley under the tracks, curry until 23:00.

BreakfastLunchEvening

Hanshin's basement can sell 10,000 squid pancakes in a single day, a two-minute walk from JR Osaka's gates. Umeda is full of food like that if you know which building to go into: the alley under the tracks, the department-store basement and the four old Ekimae Buildings. Here are seven places, grouped by meal, each rated 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked, with hours and prices from their own listings.

Who this list suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Anyone staying around Osaka Station, Umeda or Kitashinchi
  • Day-trippers with an hour before a train to Kyoto, Kobe or the airport
  • Rainy days: every pick is underground or under a roof

Think twice

  • Sunday visitors to the Ekimae Buildings: many shops close
  • Card-only travellers: YC and Madura take cash only
  • A long, slow night out: for that, go one stop to Tenma (below)
We suggest: coffee and a katsu sando at YC for breakfast, ikayaki at Hanshin for a snack, a weekday lunch in the Ekimae basements and spice curry at LUCUA in the evening. Confused by the names? Our Osaka Station vs Umeda guide explains which building is where.

A coffee-shop breakfast under the tracks (from 7:00)

  • Umeda Coffee-kan YC (梅田珈琲館YC), Shin-Umeda Shokudogai, 2 min: a coffee shop opened in 1968 by Yamamoto Coffee, the "YC" in the name. Order the katsu sando made with Hatcho miso and the blend coffee (about ¥380). Open 07:00–23:00 every day of the year, food until about 22:00. Cash only, non-smoking.
  • Madura (マヅラ喫茶店), Ekimae Dai-1 Building B1, 7 min: founded in 1947, with a space-age interior that has barely changed in about fifty years. Monday to Saturday 09:00–23:00; closed Sundays, holidays and over New Year. Cash only.

A lunch you can eat in ten minutes, or linger over

  • Hanshin Meibutsu Ikayaki, Hanshin Umeda B1 Snack Park, 2 min: squid pressed flat into a thin, chewy batter pancake with sauce, under ¥200 for a plain one; the deraban adds an egg. 10:00–22:00 (last order 21:30), standing counters, cards and IC cards accepted. The Snack Park opened in 1978.
  • Udon Bou (うどん棒), Ekimae Dai-3 Building B2: about 12 minutes from JR Osaka, 5 from Kitashinchi. Big bowls of udon, around ¥1,000–2,000; tempura udon and chanpon udon are the orders you'll see most in reviews. Tuesday to Friday 11:00–15:00 and 17:30–20:00, weekends and holidays lunch only; closed Mondays, and it shuts when the noodles run out.
  • Iwamura (いわむら), Ekimae Dai-2 Building B2: a small French-style kitchen in an office basement. Weekday lunch sets around ¥1,200–1,300, 11:30–14:00 (last order 13:30); dinner Monday to Saturday 17:30–21:00. Closed Sundays and holidays. No cards at lunch.

A spicy dinner one minute from the gates, and a late supermarket

  • Kyu Yamu Tetsudo (旧ヤム鐵道), LUCUA B2 Balcica, 1 min from the Central Exit: Osaka-style spice curry. LUCUA's own menu page features the all-gake curry, every sauce on one plate, at ¥1,738. 11:00–23:00, cards accepted. The easiest late dinner before a night train or after a day trip.
  • BIO-RAL Eki Marché Osaka, by the Eki Marché gate: the supermarket closest to the JR gates, 09:00–22:00 every day, cards and QR payments accepted. It's a natural-foods format, so not the cheapest, but ideal for ready meals and fruit for the hotel room. Its sister store BIO-RAL Umekita, in Grand Green's south building, is open 10:00–22:00.

Besides Umeda, consider Tenma for a proper night out: it's one stop away on the Loop Line, and its standing bars fill up after six.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • New Year at Hanshin: on 31 December the store closes at 18:00 (Snack Park 10:00–18:00); it is closed 1–2 January; on 3 January the Snack Park runs 9:30–20:00.
  • Sundays: Madura and Iwamura are closed, and Udon Bou serves lunch only. Plan Ekimae meals for weekdays.
  • Year-end: Iwamura closes 28 December to 4 January.
  • Smoking: some Ekimae basement bars still allow smoking, and under-20s can't enter those. Check the sign on the door.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Shin-Osaka or Osaka Station for your bullet train.
  • Osaka's local markets beyond Kuromon.

One more thing: the alley the railway workers built

Shin-Umeda Shokudogai, the low, noisy food alley under the tracks between JR Osaka and Hankyu, opened in 1950. It began as a livelihood scheme for former Japanese National Railways employees, and today about 200 tiny shops are packed into two maze-like levels. YC is in here. Walk it once slowly: it's the closest thing Umeda has to an old Osaka street, and it's a two-minute detour from the gates.
