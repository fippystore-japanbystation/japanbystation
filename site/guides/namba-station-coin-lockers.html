Coin Lockers at Namba Station: Where the Extra-Large Ones Are, Prices, and Plan B for Airport Cases

Hundreds of lockers, very few that swallow a big airport suitcase. Here's where those few are.

Sizes and pricesWhere the big ones areIf they're full

Namba's two biggest train stations list just 16 extra-large coin lockers between them: 8 at Nankai Namba, where the airport trains arrive, and 8 at the Kintetsu/Hanshin East gate. A regular large locker (about ¥600–800) fits most checked suitcases. If yours is bigger, or everything is full, the n·e·s·t counter on Nankai's 2nd floor stores it for ¥800 a day, or sends it ahead to Kansai Airport for your flight.

Paying: this is where Namba catches people out. Most Nankai banks are cash only. The Nankai B1 bank by the stairs takes IC cards, Suica and PASMO included. Kintetsu's East gate lockers take e-money, ¥100 coins or a tap of your credit card. Its West gate lockers take ¥100 coins only, with no change machine. Listings say the Midosuji subway lockers take cash or PiTaPa, not Suica.

Where the big lockers are, by where you're going

  • Just off the airport train at Nankai Namba? The 2nd-floor Central gate has the bank with the most large lockers (44), and a second bank by the Japan Post ATM has 2 extra-large ones. Going down to the 1st floor, the north exit bank next to Sumitomo Mitsui Banking is the biggest of all: 100 small, 36 medium, 20 large and 6 extra-large.
  • Want IC-card payment? The Nankai B1 bank by the stairs has 124 small, 52 medium and 23 large lockers. A travel guide notes it's off the main flow, so it's often the one with space.
  • Taking Kintetsu to Nara or Hanshin to Kobe? Leave the East gate, walk straight and turn right at the FamilyMart, near Exit 21: 8 extra-large, 69 large, 59 medium and 72 small lockers. Up the stairs at Exit 21 there are 36 more large ones on the landing.
  • At Kintetsu's West gate? Only a handful of lockers, coins only. Don't count on them for a big case.

All full? Plan B, C and D

  1. Hand over n·e·s·t, Nankai Namba 2F. 9:00–21:00, every day. ¥800 per item per day for bags up to 220 cm (three sides added) and 30 kg, for up to 14 days. English-speaking staff; cards, e-money and QR codes accepted.
  2. Fly light Send it to Kansai Airport. n·e·s·t delivers between its Namba and Kansai Airport counters for ¥2,500 a piece, and you can pre-order on its website. Spend your last day without the case and collect it at the airport.
  3. Send it Same-day hotel delivery. Kintetsu's delivery lockers at the West gate send a locker's contents to an Osaka City hotel the same day: ¥1,900 small, ¥2,300 medium, ¥2,800 large, whatever fits inside.
  4. Book Reserve a locker. Kintetsu's blue East gate lockers can be booked up to 24 hours ahead (see below).

📅 Good to know before you go

  • n·e·s·t limits: no food or perishable items (there's no fridge), 14 days at most, and the counter may close in a typhoon.
  • Airport delivery: n·e·s·t only delivers between Namba and Kansai Airport, not to hotels.
  • Coins: there are no change machines by Kintetsu's lockers, so break a note at the FamilyMart near either gate.
  • Shinkansen next? If you go on to Shin-Osaka, a bag over 160 cm in total size needs a seat with an oversized baggage area.

Nearby, once the bag is gone

  • Food near Namba Station, from cheesecake at 9:30 to skewers at 23:30.
  • Kansai Airport to Osaka: Nankai or JR, by hotel area.
  • Coin lockers at Osaka Station, if you're heading north to Umeda.
  • Nara as a day trip, about 40 minutes on Kintetsu.

One more thing: book a locker before you land

At Kintetsu/Hanshin Osaka-Namba, the blue lockers at the East gate can be reserved up to 24 hours ahead in the SPACER app. Book on the train from the airport and you walk to a locker that's already yours instead of hunting with a suitcase. They're the only lockers at that station you can reserve; all the others are first come, first served.
