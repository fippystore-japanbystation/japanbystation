Coin Lockers at Osaka Station: Prices, the Quieter Side, and What to Do When They're Full

Umeda has more than 3,000 coin lockers. The trick is knowing which ones are free before you drag the case around.

Sizes and pricesWhere the big ones areIf they're full

A large, suitcase-size locker at JR Osaka Station costs about ¥700 a day, and you can tap any Japanese IC card to pay. Most people queue for the Central Gate lockers. The Sakurabashi side, at the south-west corner, has more than a hundred large lockers too and far fewer people. If every locker is taken, check JR West's live locker map, or hand the case to Crosta Osaka for ¥1,000.

Paying: most JR banks have one touch screen for the whole row, in English, Chinese and Korean. Put the bag in, close the door, choose your locker on the screen, then pay with cash or tap an IC card. Suica, PASMO, ICOCA and the other big cards all work. Pay with the card and the same card opens the door later. Pay cash and you get a receipt with a code, so keep it safe.

Timing: on many banks a new day starts at 2:00, and the JR lockers can be used from about 4:40 to 24:40. A few rows are limited to 6 hours, and the panel says so.

Where the big lockers are, by where you're going

  • Arriving through the Central Gate? The biggest bank is the Central locker room behind the ticket office: about 157 small, 180 medium and 162 large lockers. It's also the busiest.
  • Staying at Hotel Granvia, or just want a free locker? Use the Sakurabashi exit. Four banks there add up to about 126 large lockers, and the passage towards the Central Gate holds a bank that's mostly medium and large. Travellers' guides call this side the quiet one.
  • Going to the South Gate, Daimaru or the city buses? The South Exit bank has about 47 large lockers.
  • Changing to the Midosuji subway or going into LUCUA? Three banks by the Midosuji North Exit hold about 72 large lockers between them.
  • Catching a JR highway bus? A small bank by the JR Expressway Bus Terminal has 9 large lockers.

Coming in on HARUKA from the airport? The new underground platforms use the West Gate. From there, our Kansai Airport to Osaka guide and the Osaka Station hub show the walk to the main concourse.

All full? Plan B, C and D

  1. Look ICOCA Locker Search. JR West's live map shows which lockers are free, by size, on big screens and on your phone. The screens stand by the Central locker room and the Midosuji North and South exits.
  2. Hand over Crosta Osaka, 1F. By the Central Gate, next to the travel agency counters, 9:00–20:00. ¥1,000 per piece per day, card payment. Nothing over 2 m or 30 kg, no valuables, no liquids. Weekends get busy, and you can book ahead on its site.
  3. Send it Delivery to your hotel. Crosta Osaka carries bags to hotels in Osaka City for ¥2,000 a piece, so you can walk to lunch empty-handed.
  4. Ask TiS Osaka and TiS Umeda. The JR travel counters by the Central and Sakurabashi gates also store bags for ¥1,000, from 10:00.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Late nights: JR's lockers close at about 24:40. Hankyu's ground-floor lockers in front of Osaka-umeda station are open 24 hours.
  • Weekends: Crosta Osaka says it can get crowded on weekends and holidays, so book ahead on its site if your timing is tight.
  • Shinkansen: a bag over 160 cm in total size needs a seat booked with an oversized baggage area.
  • Coins: Hankyu's and the Osaka Metro's lockers take cash (Hankyu also PiTaPa), not Suica, so keep some ¥100 coins.

One more thing: Hankyu's animal lockers

Across the road, the coin lockers on the ground floor of Hankyu's Osaka-umeda station are marked with animals: a rabbit and a turtle by the taxi stand, an elephant and an owl at the bookshop's two entrances, a sheep by the Sanbangai entrance and a monkey in front of the Hankyu Terminal Building. Remember your animal and you'll find your bag again. They're open 24 hours, cost about ¥400–700, and Hankyu has a vacancy search and a free locker concierge too. Pay with coins or a PiTaPa card.
