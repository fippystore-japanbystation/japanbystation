Three Nights on ¥30,000: Namba or Tennoji?

You fly home from Kansai Airport and want a few evenings around Dotonbori. We priced both bases on the same nights, with every train included.

TennojiNambaFull totals

For three evenings around Dotonbori, we suggest Namba. On the weeknights we checked, a well-rated private room near Tennoji was ¥1,403 cheaper. Once we added the airport trains and the subway to Dotonbori and back each evening, Namba came out ¥217 cheaper: ¥16,681 against ¥16,898. You also walk home after dinner instead of watching the clock for a 00:17 last train. Both bases leave more than ¥13,000 of a ¥30,000 budget. Tennoji wins when your evenings are not in Dotonbori, or when it is clearly cheaper on your dates.

Answer A · Built around the airport

Tennoji: the cheapest room, a direct JR line

¥13,338 · 3 nights

The cheapest private room we found within 1 km of Tennoji Station, rated 8 or higher, was an economy double at Smile Hotel Osaka Tennoji: ¥13,338 for all three nights, taxes included. It is 0.6 km from the station and rated 8.0 from 613 reviews. From Tennoji, the JR Kansai Airport Rapid runs straight to the airport for ¥1,060. Our sample journey took about 48 minutes. On Thursday 17 December, the first direct train leaves at 05:25 and reaches Kansai Airport at 06:10. Dotonbori is a 6-minute Midosuji Line ride away (¥240). The last train back from Namba on a weekday in December leaves at 00:17.

Answer B · Evenings around Dotonbori

Namba: walk home, cheapest airport train

¥14,741 · 3 nights

Using the same filters near Osaka Namba Station, the cheapest room was an economy single at Toho Hotel Namba Motomachi. It costs ¥12,184 plus ¥2,557 in taxes and fees, so ¥14,741 for three nights. It is 0.6 km from the station and rated 8.4 from 1,816 reviews. If you want a double bed, APA Hotel Namba Shinsaibashi Higashi was ¥17,918 (0.9 km, 8.3). Nankai's Airport Express from Namba costs ¥970 and needs no change. Our sample journey took about 43 minutes. On 17 December the first train reaches the airport at 05:58. The Rapi:t limited express is faster, from 34 minutes, and adds a ¥700 seat ticket (¥550 if you buy it through Nankai's online ticketless service).

Our verdict

We suggest: base yourself in Namba for an itinerary with evenings around Dotonbori. On our dates it cost ¥217 less, the airport train is cheaper with no change, and you walk home after dinner. Choose Tennoji if your evenings are around Tennoji, Abeno or Shinsekai. Choose it too if, on your dates, its room is more than about ¥1,620 cheaper than Namba's: that is what six subway rides and the extra airport fare add up to. If you will be out in Dotonbori after 00:17, stay in Namba either way.

One more thing: Abeno Harukas' free sky garden

If you stay in Tennoji, you don't need the paid observation deck for a view. Take the shuttle lift to the 16th floor of Abeno Harukas, where the open-air garden, about 80 m up, is free. Office workers eat lunch there, and it is lit up at night.

Abeno Harukas 16F garden, next to JR Tennoji and Kintetsu Osaka-Abenobashi stations; the 16th floor is a free zone, while the 58–60F HARUKAS 300 deck is paid. Sources: Rurubu, Walkerplus (quoting Abeno Harukas PR).
