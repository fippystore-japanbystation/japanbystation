Tokyo Christmas Markets 2026: Which One Is for You?

Two paid markets with collectable mugs, two you can just walk into, and the big one by Yokohama's harbour. Dates, hours and entry, checked against the official pages.

Quick tableWhich oneOne evening

Tokyo has five Christmas markets worth your evening in 2026, and they split neatly in two. Roppongi Hills and Skytree Town cost nothing to walk into and fit an hour between other plans. The Tokyo Christmas Market (Shin-Toyosu and, from 4 December, Shiba Park) and Yokohama Red Brick charge entry but feel like a proper night out. Short on time, we'd pick Roppongi Hills; for the full German-market evening, Shiba Park.

Tokyo Christmas Market: two venues, two moods

The market that started in Hibiya Park in 2015 has moved again. In 2026 it runs at Shin-Toyosu (theme: the sea, on the waterfront) from 20 November and at Shiba Park (theme: the forest, under the trees by Onarimon) from 4 December, both until 25 December. Expect the Christmas pyramid, Glühwein, German food, nutcrackers and ornaments, stage music and workshops for kids.

  • Hours: Shin-Toyosu 11:00–21:30 (last entry 20:30); Shiba Park 11:00–22:00 (last entry 21:00). Both open at 16:00 on their first day.
  • Entry: adults ¥1,000 on weekdays and ¥1,500 at weekends until 23 December; ¥2,000 on 24 and 25 December. Children at elementary school pay half; preschoolers are free.
  • Getting there: Shiba Park is 1 minute from Onarimon Station Exit A6 (the organisers point out it is not Shibakoen Station), or about 12 minutes on foot from JR Hamamatsucho's North Exit. For Shin-Toyosu, the Yurikamome to Shin-toyosu is the closest; from Shimbashi, the Tokyo BRT bus takes about 10 minutes to Toyosu Shijo-mae, then a 2-minute walk.

Not drinking? Non-alcoholic Glühwein, hot tea and cocoa are on the menu too.

Roppongi Hills: free, compact, and the 20th edition

Roppongi Hills' market at O-YANE Plaza turns 20 this year. It recreates a corner of Stuttgart's Christmas market with about ten stalls, led by Käthe Wohlfahrt's German ornaments, plus Glühwein, sausages, stollen and fresh pretzels. Entry is free, and it runs 21 November–25 December, 11:00–21:00. The anniversary brings limited items, such as a 2026 Santa-hat smoker figure at Käthe Wohlfahrt.

Come after 17:00 and walk down Keyakizaka: about 400 m of trees in the "Snow & Blue" lights, on until 22:00. Roppongi Station Exit 1C on the Hibiya Line is a minute away.

Skytree Town: the picture-book one

On the 4th-floor Sky Arena, Skytree Town's market runs 5 November–25 December, 11:00–22:00 (last orders 21:00), with 15 wooden huts and a fairy-tale theme. The drinks come in boot-shaped mugs, five new Grimm-inspired designs this year, and the food leans cute: Santa marshmallows, snowman milk tea, sliced stollen. No admission ticket is listed; you pay for what you eat and drink. It suits families, and you can pair it with the tower's gold "Star Light" illumination.

Yokohama Red Brick: the big harbour night

The Red Brick Warehouse market runs 20 November–25 December and is the most "event" of the five: an 11 m live tree, a 30 m light roof, a tunnel of lights, German huts and a yearly mug, with Yokohama's harbour and skyline behind it. Entry starts at ¥500 and depends on the day and ticket type; part of the site is free. Hours are 11:00–21:00 until 4 December, then 11:00–22:00. Check the price for your date on the official site before you go.

Which one for you

Our picks

  • Short on time: Roppongi Hills. Free, one minute from the station, and Keyakizaka's lights next door.
  • Families: Skytree Town (no ticket, cute food) or Shin-Toyosu (workshops, preschoolers free).
  • Couples: Yokohama Red Brick at dusk, or Shiba Park under the trees.
  • Collectors: the Tokyo Christmas Market's advance-ticket mugs (see below).

Think twice

  • 24–25 December: the paid markets cost ¥2,000 and are at their busiest.
  • Weekend evenings at the Tokyo Christmas Market: same-day entry may be limited when crowded.
  • Big shopping plans: Roppongi's market is small, about ten stalls.

One evening, two markets (from 4 December)

  1. 16:30 Shiba Park market from Onarimon Exit A6. Glühwein while it's still light.
  2. 18:00 Subway to Roppongi (Toei Oedo Line from nearby Daimon to Roppongi; check your route on the day).
  3. 18:30 Roppongi Hills market, a pretzel and an ornament.
  4. 19:30 Walk Keyakizaka under the lights (on until 22:00).

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Every market ends on 25 December. If you land on the 26th, these are gone until next November.
  • 24–25 December cost more: ¥2,000 per adult at both Tokyo Christmas Market venues, against ¥1,000 on a weekday.
  • Advance tickets can't be changed: once bought, the official site says they can't be cancelled or moved to another date. Tickets are sold on the official site's ticket links and at FamilyMart.
  • Hotels: December weekends fill early near Roppongi; most listings offer free cancellation, so you can hold a room now and decide later.

One more thing: the two mugs that stack

Only advance adult tickets for the Tokyo Christmas Market come with a mug, and each venue has its own colour: ultramarine at Shin-Toyosu, deep red at Shiba Park. Put the two together and they stack into a little Christmas pyramid. Bring yours back to the stalls and you pay only for the drink. Same-day tickets come without one; you can still keep the mug you drank from by giving up the deposit.
