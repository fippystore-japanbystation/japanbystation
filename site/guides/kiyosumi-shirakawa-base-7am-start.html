Kiyosumi-Shirakawa: Quiet Mornings, but Breakfast at Seven?

Tokyo's coffee neighbourhood is calm, green and close to the river. It also wakes up late. Here is what is open when you need to leave at 7:00.

Breakfast by 7Slow morningFixed departure

Yes, it works as a base for 7 a.m. starts, if you pick the right hotel. The famous coffee does not open early: Blue Bottle opens at 8:00. For a fixed 7:00 departure we suggest a hotel above the station with breakfast from 6:30, then the 07:05 train, which starts at Kiyosumi-Shirakawa and reaches Shibuya at 07:28. Want a café breakfast at 7:00? The riverside CLANN opens then, but it is a 10-minute walk from the station.

Answer A · A named place serving at seven

CLANN BY THE RIVER

Opens 7:00 · 10 min from Exit A3

The restaurant on the 2nd floor of LYURO Tokyo Kiyosumi (it used to be PITMANS) lists 7:00–22:00 every day and serves a soup breakfast with bread from chigaya, the Kuramae bakery, and coffee from LEAVES COFFEE ROASTERS beans. It sits right on the Sumida River with a wooden terrace. It is cashless only: card, Suica or Pasmo, or QR payment.

The catch is the clock. It is about 10 minutes on foot from Kiyosumi-Shirakawa Exit A3, so breakfast at 7:00 means a train closer to 7:30. That is a perfect slow start if you sleep at LYURO itself, but not if you must be out at 7:00. The restaurant also asks you to contact it for details, so check before an important morning.

Answer B · Stay for a slow morning

Blue Bottle at 8, the garden at 9

8:00 · 9:00 · ¥150

The neighbourhood's reputation is built on later mornings. Blue Bottle's Kiyosumi-Shirakawa Flagship Cafe opens 8:00–19:00 every day, with 47 seats. Kiyosumi Gardens opens at 9:00 (last entry 16:30) for ¥150, and is closed only from 29 Dec to 1 Jan.

So the people who love this base are the ones who are not rushing: a coffee at 8, the pond garden at 9, then the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo or the river walk. Our Kiyosumi-Shirakawa guide covers what is worth paying for there.

Answer C · A fixed 7:00 departure

Hotel breakfast above the station

From 6:30 · 0 min from Exit B1

Comfort Hotel Tokyo Kiyosumi Shirakawa sits at Exit B1 (the hotel says 0 minutes on foot) and serves a free buffet breakfast from 6:30 to 9:30 on the 2nd floor. On Booking.com a double for 1 adult with breakfast was about ¥16,000 for Wed 16 Dec 2026 (¥16,072) before it sold out later the same day.

The route out: on our Wednesday the 07:05 Hanzomon Line train started at Kiyosumi-Shirakawa, so it leaves from here rather than arriving full, and reached Shibuya at 07:28 (¥209) with no change, or Tokyo Station at 07:18 via Otemachi (¥178). The 07:08 Oedo Line also started here and reached Shinjuku at 07:40 (¥272). Breakfast at 6:30, at the platform by 7:05.

Our verdict

We suggest: Kiyosumi-Shirakawa is a good base for quiet mornings even with 7:00 starts, if you sleep by the station and eat the hotel breakfast from 6:30. Trains starting here put you in Shibuya by 07:28 or Shinjuku by 07:40. The answer changes when your departure moves to about 7:30: then walk to CLANN for its 7:00 riverside breakfast. And keep one morning with no alarm for Blue Bottle at 8:00 and the garden at 9:00, which is why people stay here.

One more thing: the bridge five minutes from your bed

On your first evening, walk five minutes from the station to Kiyosubashi Bridge. It is a 1928 suspension bridge modelled on one in Cologne, now a National Important Cultural Property, and it is lit up from just after sunset until 23:00. From the middle you see the Sumida River with Skytree in the distance, and the riverside terrace below is almost empty at night.

Kiyosubashi, Kiyosumi 1, Koto-ku; about 5 min from Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station. Lit from about 15 min after sunset to 23:00. Free. Sources: GO TOKYO, Tokyo Metropolitan Government Location Box, nightscape.tokyo.
