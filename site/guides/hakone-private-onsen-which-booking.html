Hakone "Private Onsen": Which Booking Actually Gets You One?

In-room bath, reservable private bath or a two-hour private bath room. Same words on the booking page, three very different evenings.

The three optionsTimingWhere to stay

If what you want is hot-spring water with nobody else in it, don't assume the "room with open-air bath" is the answer. At one Gora ryokan we checked, that room costs ¥12,600 more for two and its bath is heated tap water, while the inn's real onsen private bath is ¥3,600 for 50 minutes. Our pick for most couples: a standard ryokan room plus a booked private bath, or a two-hour private bath room in Yumoto if the bath matters more than the room.

Who this suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Couples or families who want to bathe together (shared baths are split by gender)
  • Anyone with tattoos who wants a bath without asking permission
  • First-timers nervous about bathing with strangers
  • Travellers who care whether the water is really from a hot spring

Think twice

  • Solo travellers happy in a shared bath: you'd pay for privacy you don't need
  • Late arrivals: private-bath slots at small inns go to whoever checks in first
  • Anyone who needs to pay by card at the inn: some ryokan take cash only on site

The three ways to get a private bath

A · In-room bath

+¥12,600 for two

Read the room description, not the photo

On 16 December 2026, Kiritani Hakoneso in Gora sold its standard two-meal plan at ¥27,000 for two on Ikyu and its Annex room with open-air bath at ¥39,600. The plan page says plainly that the in-room baths "are not onsen water", with a reheating function instead. Pay for this when you want a bath any time of night, not when you want the hot spring.

B · Reservable private bath

¥3,600 / 50 min

The real onsen, by the slot

Kiritani's private open-air bath uses the inn's milky sulphur water from Owakudani. It's ¥3,600 for two for 50 minutes, limited to five groups a day. You request it when you book, and time slots are given out in check-in order, so it is not guaranteed. The inn also lists free family baths.

C · Day-use private bath

¥10,400 / 120 min

A guaranteed booking, any hotel

Hakone Yuryo, a 3-minute free shuttle from Hakone-Yumoto Station, has 19 private open-air bath rooms. The smallest (18 m², up to two people) cost ¥10,400 for 120 minutes on weekdays, booked online with a card. Pair it with a simple room-only stay and the bath is the highlight, booked to the minute.

Our verdict: for a couple staying one night, book a standard ryokan room and add its private bath (B), and check in early. Choose C if your hotel has no private onsen or you arrive after 17:00 and don't want to gamble on a slot. Choose A only when the room page itself says hot spring, onsen or gensen, or when an in-room tub at midnight is the whole point.

Getting there

Odakyu trains run from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto; for Gora, change to the Hakone Tozan Railway. Hakone Yuryo's free shuttle bus leaves from near Hakone-Yumoto Station and takes about 3 minutes. Kiritani Hakoneso is on the hill in Gora and offers a free pick-up; ask when you book, especially if you have big bags or it's raining. Timing that makes the difference

  1. Booking Add the private-bath request in the notes when you reserve (option B). For Yuryo (option C), booking opens one month before your date and closes at 15:00 the day before.
  2. 15:30 Kiritani's check-in opens. The earlier you check in, the better your choice of private-bath slots.
  3. 18:00 Last check-in at Kiritani, and last entry to Yuryo's private baths on weekdays (19:00 at weekends and on holidays).
  4. After dinner Kiritani's baths run until 09:30, so a late soak or an early-morning one both work.

Eat around your bath

  • Irori Saryo Hachiri (inside Hakone Yuryo): fish and irori-hearth dishes after your bath, weekdays 11:30 to 20:00 (last order 19:00), weekends and holidays to 21:00 (last order 20:00). It saves a second trip when you've booked a late private room.
  • Ryokan dinner: Kiritani serves dinner and breakfast in private dining rooms in its main building, about a 2-minute walk outdoors with stairs and slopes from the Annex. Worth knowing if anyone in your group has trouble with steps.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Cancellation: Kiritani's Ikyu plan for 16 December was free to cancel until 9 December; after that 30% from six days before, 50% the day before and 100% on the day. Payment at the inn is cash only.
  • Yuryo closes for maintenance 1 to 3 December 2026, and charges holiday rates from 26 December 2026 to 3 January 2027.
  • Want the big baths too? Yuryo's public baths cost extra on top of a private room: ¥1,000 on weekdays, ¥1,300 at weekends.
  • Running late? Yuryo cannot move your slot if you arrive late. Extensions are ¥2,600 per 30 minutes, if the room is free.

One more thing: the private bath is a race to check-in

At small ryokan with one private bath, slots are often handed out at the front desk. Kiritani says so plainly: five groups a day, allocated in check-in order. Arrive at 15:30, the moment check-in opens, and you choose your time; arrive at 17:45 and you take what's left. If an early check-in isn't possible, book Yuryo's private room for the afternoon instead and treat the inn's bath as a bonus.
