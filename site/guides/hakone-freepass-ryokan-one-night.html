Hakone Freepass for a Ryokan Night: Worth It or Not?

One night, a good bath and dinner at the inn. We added up the single fares for three real plans, so you only pay for the pass when it pays you back.

Full loopRyokan firstVia Odawara

If your night in Hakone is really about the ryokan, skip the Freepass: Shinjuku to Gora and back, plus a short hop to the Open-Air Museum, costs ¥3,662 in single fares against ¥7,100 for the pass. Buy it the moment the ropeway and the Lake Ashi boat are in your two days: that loop costs ¥8,272 in singles, so the pass saves about ¥1,170 and gets you into Gora Park free. Arriving by Shinkansen? Buy the cheaper Odawara version, or none at all.

Who the pass suits, and who it doesn't

Buy the Freepass

  • You'll ride the ropeway and the pirate ship on day two
  • You like hopping on and off without buying tickets each time
  • You want Hakone Gora Park (free with the pass) and other discounts

Pay as you go

  • Ryokan, onsen, dinner, maybe one museum: that's the trip
  • Your inn is in Hakone-Yumoto and you won't go uphill
  • You continue to Kyoto from Odawara (the Shinjuku pass includes a return ride you won't use)
Answer A · Full sightseeing loop

Ropeway and boat in the plan? The pass wins

¥8,272 vs ¥7,100

The classic loop from Shinjuku with a night in Gora, in single IC fares: Odakyu to Gora ¥1,671, cable car to Sounzan ¥430, ropeway to Togendai ¥2,000, boat to Moto-Hakone ¥1,700, bus down to Hakone-Yumoto ¥1,210, Odakyu back to Shinjuku ¥1,261. Total ¥8,272. The 2-day Freepass from Shinjuku is ¥7,100 and covers all of it, so you save about ¥1,170 before any discounts.

Answer B · Ryokan-focused stay

Inn, bath, one short outing: skip it

¥3,662 vs ¥7,100

Shinjuku to Gora and back is ¥1,671 each way. Add a two-minute ride to Chokoku-no-Mori for the Open-Air Museum and back (¥160 each way) and you're at ¥3,662: about ¥3,400 less than the pass. If your inn is in Hakone-Yumoto, where the Romancecar terminates, it's just ¥1,261 each way. Tap an IC card (Suica, PASMO) and you don't need to buy a single ticket.

Answer C · Mixed route via Odawara

Shinkansen in, Kyoto next? Start at Odawara

¥6,000 pass

The Shinjuku Freepass includes one Odakyu round trip to Odawara. If you arrive by Shinkansen or carry on to Kyoto from Odawara, that part is wasted: buy the Odawara version (¥6,000). The same loop from Odawara and back costs ¥6,470 in singles, so the pass saves only about ¥470. A ryokan night in Gora with one short ride is about ¥1,860 in singles. Extra charges to remember: the Romancecar fee, and anything outside the pass area (the Shinkansen, Izu-Hakone buses).

In between: the Hakone Tozan Train 1-Day Pass (¥1,580) covers Odawara–Gora trains for one day and gets you into Gora Park free. It pays off if you hop off at two or three stations on the way up.

Our verdict

We suggest: no pass for a ryokan-first night. Tap your IC card and spend the difference on dinner. The answer changes the moment you add both the ropeway and the boat: then buy the 2-day Freepass from Shinjuku (¥7,100), or from Odawara (¥6,000) if the Shinkansen is part of your route. In December 2026 the ropeway's Owakudani–Sounzan section runs as a replacement bus from 7 to 24 Dec (Freepass valid), so allow extra time on that day.

Eat like a local in Gora

Tamura Ginkatsutei, two minutes from Gora Station, created Gora's signature dish in 1995: tofu katsuni, pressed tofu filled with minced pork, fried, then simmered with egg in a clay pot. Lunch runs to a 14:30 last order, dinner 17:00–19:00 last order. It's closed on Wednesdays and Tuesday evenings, and takes cards. Lunch is up to about ¥2,000. Room-only night in Yumoto instead? 📅 Good to know before you book

  • Ropeway works: Owakudani–Sounzan runs as a bus from 7 to 24 Dec 2026; Togendai–Ubako–Owakudani from 12 Jan to 26 Feb 2027. Buses run about every 15 minutes and can be crowded.
  • Freepass refunds: if a service stops for weather, Odakyu refunds unused parts at your last station. Keep the pass until the end; it's collected there.
  • Buying ahead: stations sell the pass from a month before; vending machines only sell same-day passes. Romancecar seats need their own ticket.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Owakudani: the steaming volcanic valley at the top of the ropeway. On the replacement-bus days, Odakyu suggests reaching it on the section that is running.
  • Hakone Open-Air Museum: two minutes from Chokoku-no-Mori, one stop below Gora. See our ryokan dinner timing guide.

One more thing: one Gora Park ticket, two visits

Hakone Gora Park, between Koen-shimo and Koen-kami on the cable car, lets you back in the same day and the next day on one ticket (¥650, or free with the Freepass or the Tozan Train 1-Day Pass). Staying in Gora, walk in before the 16:30 last entry on arrival day, then go back at 9:00 the next morning, when the park opens, before you check out.
