Japanese Christmas Chicken 2026: Nagoya Wings, Jidori Roasts and Where to Order

Twenty-five peppery Nagoya wings in a barrel, a roast of Nagoya Cochin, a leg of Tokushima jidori. Japan's own Christmas chicken, with this year's prices and order dates.

Twenty-five wings for ¥3,300: that's Sekai no Yamachan's Teba Barrel, Nagoya's peppery tebasaki packed for Christmas and collected on 20–25 December. Japan eats chicken at Christmas, and the most interesting versions are regional: a whole roast of Nagoya Cochin, a leg of Tokushima's Awaodori jidori from FamilyMart, and roasts of named brand chickens in Tokyo and Osaka food halls. We suggest the Teba Barrel if you're anywhere near a Yamachan branch, and Hankyu Umeda's whole roast if you can't book ahead.

Who this suits, and who it doesn't

Order one if

  • You're in Japan on 20–25 December with a room to eat in.
  • You'd rather taste a regional chicken than a global chain's bucket.
  • You can be in one place on the pick-up day.

Skip it if

  • You're moving cities every day: almost everything is collected in person.
  • You want a sit-down Christmas dinner: that's a restaurant course, booked separately.

Prices include tax. "Jidori" are Japan's local chicken breeds, such as Nagoya Cochin and Awaodori; "brand chickens" are named breeds from one region or farm. Both are sold by name, the way beef is.

Nagoya wings by the barrel: Sekai no Yamachan

Sekai no Yamachan is the Nagoya izakaya chain known for its maboroshi no tebasaki, "phantom" chicken wings fried crisp and dusted with black pepper. For Christmas it packs 25 of them into a Teba Barrel for ¥3,300, and you choose the pepper: none, light, normal or extra. For mixed groups there's also a Chicken Barrel (¥2,450): three drumsticks and three thighs of fried chicken with its "phantom pepper" (or without), plus waffle fries.

  • Booking: online 9 November–17 December; by phone or in store until 25 December.
  • Pick-up: 20–25 December, takeaway only, at most Sekai no Yamachan and Ichibanken + Sekai no Yamachan branches.
  • Not taking part: Nagoya Dome, Maruhachi Bettei, Susukino Minami-Sanjo (Sapporo), Hachioji, Sanjo-Kiyamachi and Kawaramachi (Kyoto) and Yokkaichi.
  • Extras: the first 2,000 buyers get a plate like the ones used in the restaurants, and everyone gets a Christmas sticker.

Our tip: 25 wings is a lot for two; we'd order one barrel for about four people, and the Chicken Barrel alongside if anyone wants a less peppery option.

A whole Nagoya Cochin roast: Nagoya Tokyu Hotel

Nagoya Cochin is Aichi Prefecture's famous jidori breed. Nagoya Tokyu Hotel in Sakae roasts it for Christmas as a party set for four to five: the Nagoya Cochin roast chicken with mushroom-and-potato gratin, pickles, fruit and the hotel's own bread, ¥12,000, limited to 50 sets. Its regular family roast chicken set (with warm vegetables and bread) is ¥6,800.

  • Book by 18 December: on the hotel's website, by fax, at the 1F Montmartre restaurant, or by phone (052-251-3795).
  • Collect 22–25 December, 12:00–19:00, at the bakery counter by Montmartre on the ground floor.
  • Getting there: about 5 minutes on foot from Sakae Station (Higashiyama and Meijo lines), Exit 12.

A jidori leg from the convenience store: FamilyMart's Awaodori

FamilyMart's most Japanese Christmas chicken is a roast leg of Awaodori, the jidori breed from Tokushima on Shikoku, ¥1,890, sized for one or two. You heat it in the microwave, so it works in a hotel room with a microwave or a shared kitchen.

  • For 24–25 December: order by 9:00 on 20 December.
  • For 20–23 December: order by 9:00 on 16 December.
  • Order in store or through FamilyMart's online reservation; a few stores don't take Christmas orders.

Brand-chicken roasts from the food halls

Tokyo, Takashimaya (Nihonbashi and Shinjuku stores): two chicken specialists make roasts for store pick-up on 24–25 December. Umeya's Suigo Akadori roast chicken (Suigo Akadori is a red-feathered brand chicken; the roast serves two to four) is ¥4,320, and Matsuoka's Arita-dori BBQ grill, two legs, is ¥2,979. Online orders run until 10:00 on 20 December, though some items close earlier.

Osaka, Hankyu Umeda: on the B1 deli floor, Chicken Deli TORIYOSHI sells a whole roast of Satsuma herb Yuzen-dori, a brand chicken from Kagoshima, for ¥3,980, on sale 20–25 December. It's the easiest whole bird to get in Osaka without planning weeks ahead. More of Umeda's Christmas food halls: Christmas food in Osaka 2026.

Which one for you, and what if you didn't book

  • A group of four in Nagoya, Tokyo or Osaka: the Teba Barrel. Phone or walk in to a branch if online booking has closed.
  • A splurge dinner in Nagoya: the Nagoya Cochin roast set, booked by 18 December.
  • Two people, a microwave, a quiet night: FamilyMart's Awaodori leg.
  • No booking at all: Hankyu Umeda's B1 roast (20–25 December), or the Yamachan takeaway counter at JR Nagoya Station (see below).

📅 Good to know before you book

  • 16 December, 9:00: FamilyMart's Awaodori leg orders close for 20–23 December pick-up (20 December for 24–25).
  • 17 December: online booking for the Teba Barrel ends; phone and in-store orders run to 25 December.
  • 18 December: Nagoya Tokyu Hotel stops taking roast chicken orders, and its Nagoya Cochin set is limited to 50.
  • 20 December, 10:00: Takashimaya's online Christmas orders close (some items sooner).

One more thing: the barrel you don't have to book

Sekai no Yamachan's takeaway shop inside JR Nagoya Takashimaya, the department store on top of Nagoya Station, sells only the Teba Barrel for Christmas and takes no reservations: you pay at the counter when you collect. If you're changing trains at Nagoya between 20 and 25 December, that's 25 wings for the next leg of the trip.
