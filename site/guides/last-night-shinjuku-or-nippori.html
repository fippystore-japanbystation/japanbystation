Last Night in Tokyo: Move from Shinjuku to Nippori Before a Narita Flight?

We timed the same airport morning three ways for a traveller who wants to be at Narita Terminal 1 by 08:30.

Stay putMove onceSleep at Narita

For an 08:30 arrival at Narita, stay in Shinjuku: leave at 07:13, change once at Nippori, and Skyliner 15 has you at Terminal 1 at 08:27. Moving to Nippori lets you board that same train at 07:45, only 32 minutes more sleep, and the move costs about that much the evening before. Sleep at the airport only when you need to be there before the first Skyliner arrives at 06:03.

Answer A · Stay put in Shinjuku

Out of the hotel by about 7:00

07:13 → 08:27 · ¥2,840

The fastest route on our sample morning: JR Yamanote Line from Shinjuku at 07:13 to Nippori at 07:34, then Skyliner 15 at 07:45, arriving at Narita Terminal 1 at 08:27. ¥2,840 in total, with one change and a 10-minute transfer allowance at Nippori.

Prefer no change with your bags? Narita Express 3 left Shinjuku at 06:34 and reached Terminal 1 at 07:58, ¥3,330. You start 39 minutes earlier, but you sit down once and stay seated.

Answer B · Move once, to Nippori

32 minutes saved, paid for the night before

07:45 → 08:27 · ¥2,580

The morning: from Nippori you board the same Skyliner 15 at 07:45 and reach Terminal 1 at 08:27 for ¥2,580. You leave 32 minutes later than from Shinjuku and skip the change.

The evening before: check out of Shinjuku, ride the Yamanote Line 21 minutes to Nippori (our sample: 18:02 to 18:23, ¥260), walk to the hotel and check in again. APA Hotel TKP Nippori Ekimae, 200 m from the station, was ¥14,742 for 2 adults on Wed 16 Dec; the middle of the first 20 Shinjuku results that night was about ¥18,800. So the move roughly breaks even on time, and can save money if your Shinjuku nights are cancellable.

Answer C · Sleep at the airport for a very early flight

9h nine hours Narita Airport

From ¥7,000 per person

The first Skyliner on our sample morning left Nippori at 05:25 and reached Terminal 1 at 06:03; from Shinjuku you would need the 04:44 Yamanote Line to catch it. If you need to be at the airport earlier than that, trains cannot help.

The capsule hotel 9h nine hours Narita Airport is on the landside of Terminal 2 and directly connected to it. Check-in runs from 14:00 to 05:00 (tell them if you arrive after midnight), check-out is by 10:00. On 16 Dec a capsule was from ¥7,000 for men and ¥7,700 for women, per person, including taxes. Flying from Terminal 1? The free terminal shuttle bus takes about 10 minutes from Terminal 2.

Our verdict

We suggest: keep your Shinjuku hotel and take the 07:13 Yamanote Line plus Skyliner (or the 06:34 Narita Express for no change) whenever arriving at Narita around 08:00–08:30 is enough. Move to Nippori only if you carry big bags, your last evening is on that side of town, or the room there is clearly cheaper. The answer changes when you need to be at the airport before 06:00: then sleep at Narita, because the first Skyliner does not arrive until 06:03.

One more thing: dango that Soseki wrote about

Two minutes from Nippori Station is Habutae Dango, open since 1819 and famous enough to appear in Natsume Soseki's novel I Am a Cat. The dumplings are silky, made only from rice flour, and come two ways: grilled with a soy glaze that isn't sweet, or covered in smooth red bean. Eat them in the shop with green tea; they don't keep well, so they are not one for the suitcase.

Habutae Dango main shop, Higashi-Nippori 5-54-3, about 2–5 min from Nippori Station; there is also a café by the station. About ¥263 a skewer. Sources: Tabelog, Time Out Tokyo (Japanese), Retty.
