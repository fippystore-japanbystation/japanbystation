Shin-Osaka Station: Where to Eat and Where to Leave Your Bags

About 200 suitcase lockers, only 4 extra-large ones, and a staffed counter that sends your case to the hotel. Plus three places to eat by the gates.

LockersIf they're fullWhere to eat

Shin-Osaka has just 4 extra-large lockers, all on the south side of the JR East exit, out of about 200 large ones in 9 banks. One normal suitcase fits a large locker (about ¥700 a day). Two cases, or one very big one, belong at Crosta Shin-Osaka: ¥1,000 a day, or ¥2,000 to have it waiting at your Osaka hotel tonight. Hungry too? An old-style coffee shop opens downstairs at 7:20, and a beef katsu sandwich waits inside the Shinkansen gates.

Who this page suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Arriving by Shinkansen hours before check-in
  • A few hours in Osaka between two bullet trains
  • Early trains: breakfast from 7:20

Think twice

  • Two big cases each: plan on Crosta or delivery, not lockers
  • Late arrivals after 20:00: the staffed counters are shut
  • A proper food crawl: Shin-Osaka is for a quick meal, the city is for dinner
We suggest: one suitcase, use a large locker on the 3F concourse; anything bigger, book Crosta and send it to your hotel. Not sure Shin-Osaka is the station you need? Read Shin-Osaka vs Osaka Station first.

  • Getting off the Shinkansen? The 3F concourse has the most lockers, with five banks within about 3 minutes of the Shinkansen south gate. They're also the first to fill.
  • Have a very big case? Go to the JR East exit, south side: 39 large lockers and the station's only 4 extra-large ones.
  • Changing to the Midosuji subway? Use the 2F lockers (by the Arde mall and the front exit). They're further from the gates, so they're often freer.

Lockers full? Hand it over or send it ahead

  1. 9:00–20:00 Crosta Shin-Osaka, 1F by the front exit: ¥1,000 per bag a day, or ¥2,000 to deliver it to your Osaka hotel the same day. Book online in advance. Nothing over 2 m or 30 kg. Cash or card.
  2. 11:00–19:00 TiS Shin-Osaka (Nippon Travel): ¥1,000 per bag, card only.
  3. Morning Go early: a local travel site says the counters, like the lockers, can be full by the afternoon.

Riding on with it? On the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen, a case of 161–250 cm in total needs a reserved seat with an oversized baggage area.

Three places to eat, from a 7:20 breakfast to a late fish dinner

  • Breakfast in an old coffee shop: Kissa Sancho (喫茶サンチョ), 1F Aji no Koji. An old-style kissaten on the station's ground floor, open 7:20–21:00, under ¥1,000. It takes PayPay but no cards, so bring cash.
  • A sandwich for the train: Restaurant Yokoo (レストラン ヨコオ), inside the Shinkansen gates at Osaka Noren Meguri. The thick-cut Kansai-style beef katsu sandwich is the one to take on board; there's omurice if you sit down. Just 5 seats. 10:00–21:30 (last order 21:10), every day.
  • Fish until 23:00: Uoya Stand Fuji (魚屋スタンドふじ), at the Midosuji Line north gate. A fish dealer's own counter in the Shin-Naniwa Daishokudo food hall. "Stand" means standing-bar prices, but every one of the 50 seats has a chair. Lunch under ¥1,000, dinner about ¥2,000–3,000. 11:30–23:00, no fixed days off. Use stairs A or B from the subway.

For a sit-down bowl of udon, walk 10 minutes north to Higashi-Mikuni. See our Shin-Osaka station guide.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Crosta needs a booking on busy weekends and holidays; the official listing warns of queues.
  • Same-day delivery only works if you drop the case while the counter is open (9:00–20:00).
  • Kissa Sancho takes no cards: PayPay or cash.

One more thing: the quiet lockers are downstairs

Most people grab the first locker they see on the 3F concourse. Go down instead. The 1st-floor lockers by the lift, the toilets and the front exit are the furthest from the gates, so they're usually the emptiest. They sit next to a waiting area with room to open your suitcase and repack. The lift is right there, and the taxis and buses leave from this floor.
