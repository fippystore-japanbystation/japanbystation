Namba Station: Which Namba, Which Exit for Your Hotel

Four stations share one name. Which one your train uses, the exit for where you are going, the fastest way to the airport, and one hotel for each kind of trip.

Which NambaWhich exitWhere to stay

"Namba Station" is really four stations: Osaka Metro Namba (the subway), Nankai Namba (trains to Kansai Airport), Osaka-Namba (Kintetsu to Nara and Hanshin to Kobe) and JR Namba. They are linked on foot, mostly underground, so pick by your train line, then by the exit for where you are going. For your hotel, match it to how you will reach the airport: rapi:t people sleep near Nankai, bus people near JR Namba.

Who a base at Namba Station suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Travellers flying in and out of Kansai Airport: direct trains and a direct bus start here
  • Food-first trips: Dotonbori, Hozenji and Kuromon Market are a walk away
  • Day trips to Nara or Kobe: Kintetsu and Hanshin trains leave from Osaka-Namba
  • Night owls: you can walk back to the hotel after the last train

Think twice

  • JR Pass holders doing Kyoto day trips: Osaka Station in Umeda is easier (see Osaka Station vs Umeda)
  • Light sleepers who want quiet streets: the Dotonbori side stays loud late
  • Anyone hoping to catch the shinkansen in a hurry: Shin-Osaka is 16 minutes away on the Midosuji Line

Which Namba station do you need?

Look at the line on your ticket or map app, then use this list.

  • Going to Kansai Airport by train, or to Koyasan? Nankai Namba. Its platforms are on the 3rd floor of the Nankai building, under the Swissotel.
  • Going to Nara or Kobe? Osaka-Namba, one station shared by Kintetsu and Hanshin, three levels underground. Platforms 1 and 2 are for Nara, platform 3 for Kobe-Sannomiya. Until 2009 it was called Kintetsu Namba, so older maps differ.
  • Going to Umeda, Shin-Osaka or Tennoji? Osaka Metro Namba on the Midosuji Line (M20). The same station also has the Yotsubashi (Y15) and Sennichimae (S16) lines.
  • Going east on JR towards Tennoji, Nara or Horyuji? JR Namba, the end of the Yamatoji Line, directly connected to the OCAT building. It sits a little west of the others; the hotel above it lists the Nankai trains as about 7 minutes' walk.
Our suggestion: type the operator into your map app ("Nankai Namba", "Kintetsu Osaka-Namba"), not just "Namba". The four stations are a few minutes apart, and the wrong one costs you a walk with your bags.

Which exit? Start from where you are going

Exit numbers below are on the Osaka Metro Namba station map, the one most signs follow.

  • Going to Dotonbori, Hozenji Temple or the Shinsaibashisuji arcade? Use Exit 14, on the east side of Midosuji. Exit 25 also reaches Dotonbori from the west side.
  • Going to Namba Parks, Takashimaya or the Nankai airport trains? Use Exit 4. Namba Parks is directly connected to Nankai Namba.
  • Going to Namba HIPS? Take Exit 15-B, a covered walkway straight into the building.
  • Going to Kintetsu or Hanshin trains? Follow signs for Exit 27 and Namba Walk; the Osaka-Namba gates are along that passage.
  • Going to the airport bus at OCAT or to JR Namba? From the Yotsubashi Line, take the connecting passage at Exit 30. The airport buses leave from the 2nd floor of the OCAT bus terminal.
  • Going to Kuromon Market or Nippombashi? Stay underground and walk east along Namba Walk, a 715 m shopping street that ends at Nippombashi station; the market is right outside.

Namba to Kansai Airport: rapi:t, Airport Express or bus?

  • rapi:t (Nankai Namba): 34 minutes at the fastest, every seat reserved. Since 1 April 2026 the seat surcharge is ¥700 at the counter or ¥550 with Nankai's ticketless app, on top of the ¥970 fare. Many guides still quote the old, lower surcharge.
  • Airport Express (Nankai Namba): about 45 minutes, no surcharge, so just the ¥970 fare. Good if you travel light outside rush hour.
  • Limousine bus (OCAT, by JR Namba): about 48 minutes to Terminal 1 and 11 more to Terminal 2, ¥1,400 adult (¥700 child), departures from 06:10 to 22:40, no reservation. Best with big suitcases: you load them once and sit down.

Eat around Namba Station: the famous plate and the local one

What most visitors go for: Jiyuken, open since 1910, about 3 minutes from the station opposite Bic Camera. Order the meibutsu (signature) curry, listed at ¥650 on Hot Pepper Gourmet. Cash only, closed Mondays (Tuesday when Monday is a holiday).

Dig a little deeper: Marufuku Coffee Sennichimae Honten, a coffee house roasting its own beans since 1934, about 5 minutes from Metro Namba. Regulars come for the thick hotcakes cooked on a copper griddle and the homemade coffee jelly; Retty and Rurubu put a set at about ¥1,300 to ¥1,450. It opens 8:00 to 23:00 (last order 22:30), so it works for an early breakfast before the airport train.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • "Namba" in a hotel name can mean anywhere from Nippombashi to Sakuragawa. Check the minutes to the station you will use (Nankai for the rapi:t, OCAT for the bus) before you book.
  • The last airport bus from OCAT leaves at 22:40 and the bus takes no reservations, so plan a late departure around the train instead.
  • Free cancellation: many Namba listings offer it on Booking.com (check each one), so you can hold a room while you compare sides.

One more thing: drop your bags on the 2nd floor, not in a locker

On your last day, the coin lockers at Namba fill up and the Kintetsu ones take only ¥100 coins. Nankai Namba has a staffed n·e·s·t counter on the 2nd floor, in front of the Central Ticket Gate and outside the gates, open 9:00 to 21:00. It keeps a bag for ¥800 a day, or sends it to Kansai Airport for ¥2,500 a piece, so you can spend the day in Namba with empty hands and pick it up at the airport.
