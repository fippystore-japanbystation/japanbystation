Hakone Ropeway Closed: Is the Day Still Worth It?

Usually yes. The lake is still 20 minutes away by bus. Here's how to rebuild the day, and when to move it instead.

Plan B optionsLunchRefunds

If the Hakone Ropeway is suspended, keep the day: from Gora Station, two short Tozan bus rides get you to Moto-Hakone on Lake Ashi in about 20 minutes for ¥910, all covered by the Hakone Freepass. If the wind has stopped the lake cruise too, build the day around Gora instead: the Open-Air Museum is open every day. Only if the views from the gondola and boat were the reason you came do we suggest moving the date, and the pass and Romancecar refund rules make that cheap.

Who should still go, and who should move the day

Still go

  • You came for the lake, the shrine and an onsen, not the gondola itself
  • You have a Freepass: buses are included and unused parts can be refunded
  • Your ryokan is booked for tonight: Hakone in the rain still has hot water

Move the day if you can

  • Owakudani's steam vents and the Fuji view from the gondola were the whole point
  • You're travelling with kids who were promised the "pirate ship"
  • It's a one-day visit from Tokyo and tomorrow's forecast is clearer

First, check what's actually closed

Hakone Navi's Transportation Info page shows live status for every Freepass line. On the morning we checked (24 November 2026), the Ropeway and the Lake Ashi sightseeing cruise were both suspended, while the Tozan Train, Cable Car and buses ran normally. That mix is common on windy days, so check it before you leave Tokyo, not at Sounzan.

Planned closures are different: Owakudani–Sounzan is closed 7 to 24 December 2026, and Togendai–Ubako–Owakudani 12 January to 26 February 2027. On those dates replacement buses run about every 15 minutes, but only for people holding a valid ropeway ticket such as the Freepass, and Odakyu warns they get very crowded.

Three ways to rescue the day

A · Keep the lake

20 min · ¥910

Gora to Moto-Hakone by bus

Take the "S" sightseeing-facility bus from Gora to Kowakien (4 minutes, ¥250), then the H-line bus to Moto-Hakone-ko (14 minutes, ¥660); on our sample search for a December weekday the whole trip was 11:00 to 11:20. Coming straight from Hakone-Yumoto instead, the direct bus from stop 2 takes about 30 minutes for ¥1,210 and runs about every 15 minutes in the daytime. Need Togendai? Gora to Togendai by bus is about 55 minutes, ¥880.

B · Stay around Gora

9:00–17:00 daily

Art, lunch, onsen

The Hakone Open-Air Museum is two stops below Gora and open every day (adults ¥2,000, or ¥1,800 online), with indoor galleries for when the rain gets heavy. The Pola Museum of Art (¥2,200) has a free shuttle from Gora, 8 minutes, but the whole museum is closed on 1 December 2026. Add lunch in Gora and a bath, and it's a full, slower day.

C · Change the day

¥100 Romancecar refund

When the views were the point

An e-Romancecar booked online can be moved to another train the same day as often as you like, and to another date once, before departure. Refunds before departure cost ¥100 a seat. An unused digital Freepass refunds for a ¥220 fee before your trip starts.

Our verdict: if only the ropeway is down, take option A and you lose almost nothing; on our sample search the Gora–Togendai bus (about 55 minutes) was even quicker than the cable car and ropeway combination (65 minutes). If the cruise is suspended too, pick B and save the lake for another day. Move the whole trip (C) only when Owakudani or the gondola view was the reason you booked, or when you can come back within the next day or two.

Eat like a local: lunch on either plan

  • Bakery & Table Hakone (Moto-Hakone): right by the Moto-Hakone-ko bus stop, open every day, with a bakery downstairs and a café with lake views upstairs (last café order 16:30). GOOD LUCK TRIP lists the rice-flour curry doughnut as the best-seller. Cards accepted.
  • Tamura Ginkatsutei (Gora): about 2 minutes from Gora Station, and where tofu katsuni, a tofu cutlet simmered with egg, originated. Lunch last order 14:30; closed Wednesdays and Tuesday evenings. Cards accepted.
  • Hakone Gyoza Center (Gora): small and popular for gyoza sets at lunch, last order 14:30. Closed Thursdays and Wednesday afternoons, cash only. A reviewer on its listing suggests writing your name in the notebook by the door.

A ropeway-free route from Gora

  1. 9:30 Arrive Gora on the Tozan Train and check the status page once more.
  2. 10:00 Cruise running? Bus to Kowakien, change to the H line, and you're at Moto-Hakone around 10:20.
  3. 10:30 Walk about 10 minutes to Hakone Shrine (free) and its lakeside torii.
  4. 12:00 Lunch and a lake view at Bakery & Table, by the bus stop.
  5. 13:30 H-line bus back towards Hakone-Yumoto (about 30 minutes), or back up to Kowakien for Gora.
  6. 15:00 A bath in Yumoto before the Romancecar home.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Fixed closures: part of the ropeway is shut 7–24 December 2026 and 12 January–26 February 2027. If the gondola matters, book outside those dates.
  • Freepass refunds: if a line is suspended and you didn't ride it, Odakyu refunds that part at your last station. On the digital pass the ropeway part is ¥1,640 (2-day) and the cruise ¥1,180.
  • Romancecar: if Odakyu cancels the train or it runs an hour or more late, the limited-express fare is refunded in full.
  • Most Hakone hotels on booking sites show a free-cancellation date: useful when the forecast turns.

One more thing: claim the ropeway back from your pass

Most people with a Freepass shrug and ride the bus. But if the ropeway was suspended and you never rode it, the unused part is refundable at your last station: on the digital Freepass that's ¥1,640 for an adult 2-day pass, ¥820 if only one section was down, and another ¥1,180 if the cruise was suspended too. Ask at the station where you finish before you go through the gate home.
