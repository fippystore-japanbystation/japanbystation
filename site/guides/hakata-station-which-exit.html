Hakata Station: Which Exit? Hakata vs Chikushi

Two sides, one huge building. Most of what you're after is out the Hakata exit, the airport is 5 minutes away on the subway underneath you, and you can drop your bags and eat without ever going far.

Which exitLuggageWhere to stay

Hakata Station has two faces. The Hakata exit (west) is the city side: Hakata Bus Terminal a minute away, Canal City about 10 minutes on foot, the department stores, and the road towards Tenjin. The Chikushi exit (east) is the other side, with Yodobashi and a row of business hotels. For the airport you need neither: the subway is under the station, and Fukuoka Airport is two stops and about 5 minutes away, for ¥260.

Which exit for where you're going

  • Going to Fukuoka Airport or Tenjin? Don't leave the building: follow the subway signs to the Airport Line. The airport is 2 stops (about 5 min, ¥260); Tenjin is about 5 minutes (¥210).
  • Catching a highway bus, or the bus to Dazaifu? Use the Hakata exit. Hakata Bus Terminal is about a minute away and connected to the station: city buses leave from 1F, highway buses from 3F, and the Dazaifu Liner "Tabito" from 1F bay 11 (about 40 min, ¥800).
  • Going to Canal City? Use the Hakata exit and walk about 10 minutes, or take the subway Nanakuma Line one stop to Kushida-jinja-mae (Exit 1, then 3 minutes).
  • Staying on the east side, or heading to Yodobashi? Use the Chikushi exit. Yodobashi Hakata is right outside, next to subway Exit 15.
  • Going to Hakata Port or Bayside Place? Use the Hakata exit for the Nishitetsu bus stop "F" in front of Nishi-Nippon City Bank, about 20 minutes to the port.
We suggest: for the airport, Tenjin or Canal City, think "subway" before you think "exit". The Airport Line and the Nanakuma Line both start under the station, and the lift down to the JR concourse is at the subway's Chuo exit.

Lockers and same-day luggage delivery

  • JR Kyushu coin lockers: ¥400 (smallest), ¥500 (S), ¥800 (M), ¥1,000 (L) and ¥1,200 (XL) a day; the day rolls over at midnight. Some can be reserved through SPACER.
  • JR Hakata City lockers: on several floors; the AMU B1F ones (5:30–24:00, IC card) and the DEITOS Annex 1F ones (24 hours, IC card) are the easiest late at night. The Kuten 9F–10F lockers are cashless only.
  • Multi E-Cube (Hakata-exit side, in the passage to Hakata Hankyu): 5:00–24:30, cashless, S ¥500 / L ¥1,000. Book by 9:30, drop the bag by 11:00, and it's delivered to your Fukuoka hotel the same day (¥1,800–2,000).

Free building Wi-Fi ("JR HAKATA CITY") runs 4:30–2:00 if you need to check locker availability on the spot.

Eat around the station, two layers

What most visitors go for: Hakata Men Kaido, the ramen street on DEITOS 2F, right by the Shinkansen gates, open as late as 23:30. Shops include Ikkosha, Shin-Shin, Ganso Hakata Daruma and Nagahama No.1. For more choice, City Dining Kuten on the 9th and 10th floors of JR Hakata City is open 11:00–22:00 (some places until midnight). For mentaiko to take home, Hakata Hankyu B1 has Yamaya, Fukusaya, Fukutaro, Chikae and Shimamoto side by side (10:00–20:00).

Dig a little deeper: about 6 minutes' walk on the east side (574 m by Tabelog's count), Hakata Issou's Hakata-eki-higashi main shop is one of Tabelog's Ramen WEST "Hyakumeiten" every year from 2020 to 2025, with a 3.72 score and bowls under ¥1,000. It opens 11:00–24:00 with irregular days off and takes cash only. Tabelog reviewers report hour-long Sunday lunch queues, so a weekday mid-afternoon bowl is the calmer plan.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Same-day luggage delivery from the Multi E-Cube lockers needs a booking by 9:30 and the bag in by 11:00.
  • The ticket office (Midori no Madoguchi) is open 7:00–22:00; machines for collecting online-booked tickets run 4:45–23:20.
  • Hakata Hankyu is closed on 1 January, so buy mentaiko gifts before New Year's Day.
  • Most hotels offer free cancellation, so booking near the station early costs nothing if plans change.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Canal City Hakata: about 10 minutes on foot from the Hakata exit.
  • Dazaifu: the Tabito bus from the bus terminal, about 40 minutes, ¥800 (¥20 off with an IC or credit card).
  • Fukuoka Subway 1 Day Pass: ¥640 for adults (¥320 for children).

One more thing: a railway shrine on the roof

Take the lift to the top of JR Hakata City and you reach Tsubame no Mori Hiroba, a rooftop garden about 60 m up, open 10:00–22:00 with no entry fee listed. It has a small railway shrine where people pray for safe journeys and a terrace over the tracks, a calm ten minutes before a long train ride. The little rooftop train has stopped running for good; the garden may close in bad weather.
