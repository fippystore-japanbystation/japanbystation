Itami Sky Park: Can You Watch Planes Without a Car?

You want takeoffs and landings up close, starting from Umeda, with no rental car. Here are the three ways in, with fares, waits and the trip back.

Train + busTrain + taxiAirport deck

Yes, and it's cheap. Take the Hankyu line from Osaka-umeda to Itami (21 minutes, ¥240), then Itami City Bus route 25 for 8 minutes (¥250) to the "Itami Sky Park / Kamisuko" stop. The park is free and open 9:00–21:00 in winter. We suggest a loop, though. Go out via Hotarugaike and the monorail to the free observation deck at Osaka Airport. Then ride the same route 25 bus 8 minutes from the airport to Sky Park, and head back to Umeda through Hankyu Itami. You see planes from both sides for ¥1,180 in total, with no backtracking.

Answer A · Cheapest

Hankyu to Itami, then city bus 25

¥490 each way

On Hankyu's route search for Wednesday 16 December, the 12:31 from Osaka-umeda reached Itami at 12:52, with one change at Tsukaguchi (¥240). Itami City Bus route 25 ("via Kamitsu, for Osaka Airport") leaves from stop 6 outside the station. On the weekday timetable it ran at 13:18 and 13:48, roughly every 25–30 minutes at midday. Allow for up to half an hour's wait. The 13:18 reaches the Itami Sky Park / Kamisuko stop at 13:26. The fare is ¥250, paid when you board, so have coins ready. Routes 22/23 (Iwaya loop) from the same stop also serve the park. Coming back, route 25 leaves the Sky Park stop for Hankyu Itami and JR Itami twice an hour, with the last bus at 22:03. The park itself is 1.2 km long and runs beside the runway. It has North, Central and South entrances, six toilets, and a take-out café at the Central Entrance.

Answer B · Fastest out

Hankyu to Itami, taxi to the park, bus back

about ¥1,100 taxi

A taxi from Hankyu Itami skips the bus wait. On an OpenStreetMap route estimate, the drive to the park's address is about 2.1 km. Itami falls in the Kobe taxi-fare area. The upper-limit fare there is ¥700 for the first 1.3 km and ¥100 for every 225 m after that, so expect about ¥1,100 on the meter. Fares vary slightly by company. The return trip is the weak point. Neither the park nor the city lists a taxi rank at Sky Park, so plan to come back on route 25 (¥250 to Hankyu Itami). That makes about ¥1,830 for the round trip from Umeda, against ¥980 by bus both ways.

Answer C · Easiest

Osaka Airport observation deck

free · ¥440 from Umeda

If you mainly want to watch takeoffs and landings, the airport's own deck is the easiest choice. Take a Hankyu express from Osaka-umeda to Hotarugaike (13 minutes, ¥240). Then ride the Osaka Monorail one stop to Osaka Airport (¥200, 1.4 km). The deck is on the 4th floor of the terminal. Entry is free and it is open every day. At the time of checking, the airport listed hours of 6:00–21:30, with last entry at 21:00. The airport describes views of aircraft up close and of the runways at night. It may close without notice for weather or security. The terminal's shops and cafés are right below you. Flights at Itami run from 7:00 to 21:00.

Our verdict

We suggest: a loop. Go Osaka-umeda → Hotarugaike → monorail to the airport deck. Then take city bus 25 from Osaka Airport stop 7 to Sky Park (8 minutes), and finally bus 25 to Hankyu Itami and the train back to Umeda. That's ¥1,180, and you never ride the same leg twice. The answer changes with time and weather. With only an hour or two, or in rain, stay on the deck (¥440 each way). With three or four people and a missed bus, take the taxi from Hankyu Itami for the way out only. In winter Sky Park opens at 9:00. From April to October it opens at 7:00 on weekends and holidays. Either way, leave the park by about 22:00 to catch the 22:03 last bus.
