Four Friends in Osaka: One Apartment or Two Hotel Rooms?

Same city, same budget, very different trips. Here's how we'd decide.

CompareIs it legal?Where

Four people in Osaka usually choose between three options: a private apartment, an apartment hotel, or two twin rooms in a regular hotel. None is always cheapest. What decides it is how many nights you're staying, how many bathrooms you need and how much you care about having someone at a front desk.

Checked 23 November 2026

The quick comparison

🏠 Private apartment

Good: one shared living space, a kitchen, a washing machine.
Watch: often one bathroom for four, cleaning fees, self check-in, and some have a minimum stay.

🏨 Apartment hotel

Good: real beds for four, a kitchenette, a laundry machine, and staff if something goes wrong.
Watch: popular with groups, so they book out early.

🛏️ Two twin rooms

Good: two bathrooms, daily cleaning, easy cancellation, often right by the station.
Watch: rooms are compact and there's no common space to hang out.

Our suggestion: for 2–3 nights, two twin rooms near the station are usually the easiest. For 4 nights or more, an apartment hotel often wins, because laundry and a living room start to matter.
Before you book an apartment

How to check a rental is legal

Short-term rentals in Japan need a registration or a special-zone certification, and legal hosts must record your passport at check-in. Osaka City stopped taking new special-zone applications on 29 May 2026 and has been inspecting rentals more closely, so unlicensed listings are more likely to be cancelled at short notice.

One more thing: a takoyaki party in your apartment

If you take the apartment, do what Osaka families do at home and throw a takoyaki party. A three-minute walk from Namba, Sennichimae Doguyasuji is a 150 m covered street of shops that supply Osaka's restaurants, and several sell takoyaki pans in every size. Buy one there, pick up octopus and batter mix at a supermarket, and four people turning dough balls with picks is a better night than any restaurant.

Sennichimae Doguyasuji Shotengai, Namba-Sennichimae; about 3 min from Namba Station. Hours vary by shop (many roughly 9:00–18:30). Some shops also run food-sample-making workshops. Check that your apartment has a hob the pan works on. Sources: Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau (osaka-info.jp), Mapple.
