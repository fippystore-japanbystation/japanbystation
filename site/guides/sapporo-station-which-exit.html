Sapporo Station: Which Exit? South vs North

Which side for Odori and Susukino, how to stay out of the snow all the way there, the airport train, where the buses went, and where to drop your bags.

Which exitLuggageWhere to stay

Most travellers want the South exit: it faces the city, Daimaru and Stellar Place, and leads down to Chi-Ka-Ho, the underground walkway that takes you to Odori in about 10 minutes without stepping into the snow. The North exit is for Hokkaido University and the tour buses. One thing has changed for good: the station bus terminal closed in 2023, so buses now leave from street stops around the South exit.

Which exit for where you're going

  • Going to Odori, the TV Tower area or Susukino? Use the South exit side and go down to Chi-Ka-Ho: about 520 m underground, open 5:45–24:30, about 10 minutes to Odori. Susukino is about 1.5 km from the station, roughly 20 minutes on foot, or ride the subway.
  • Going to the Clock Tower? South exit, then about 10 minutes on foot. It's open 8:45–17:10 and costs ¥350.
  • Shopping at Daimaru? It's about 3 minutes from the West gate. The East gate is the one for JR Tower and Stellar Place.
  • Going to Hokkaido University? Use the West gates, then the North exit: about 7 minutes to the Main Gate.
  • Joining a bus tour? Tour buses board at the JR North exit bus stop; the Chuo Bus tour counter is in the West Concourse.
We suggest: in winter, think "underground first". Chi-Ka-Ho and the station's underground malls get you to Odori dry, which matters with a suitcase on icy pavements.

The airport train, and where the buses went

Rapid Airport to New Chitose: ¥1,230. The Rapid takes about 37 minutes and the fastest Special Rapid about 33; between 9:00 and 16:00 there are six trains an hour. For a reserved seat, Car 4 is the u-seat car: ¥1,000 extra, or ¥800 if booked ticketless. Look for the "U seat" sign on the platform. Our New Chitose to Sapporo guide compares the train with the bus and a taxi.

Buses: the Sapporo Station Bus Terminal under the old ESTA building closed on 30 September 2023. Since then, buses use temporary stops around the South exit, which the city expects to stay until about FY2028. Highway buses stop around Kita 3-jo Plaza (Akapla), stops 1–7, and the airport buses use stops in the Kita 4 Nishi 1 / Kita 5 area. If you read an older guide that says "the bus terminal under ESTA", that's out of date.

The same redevelopment closed ESTA and its 10th-floor Sapporo Ramen Republic in August 2023. The Hokkaido Shinkansen is now expected to reach Sapporo around the end of FY2038.

Lockers and luggage

  • JR coin lockers: 818 of them since July 2025, in the West Concourse, the East Concourse and inside the gates. Small ¥400, medium ¥500, large ¥700–800 a day, up to 3 days. There are five lockers sized for skis or snowboards.
  • Multi E-Cube: 36 cashless lockers outside the 1F East gate, S ¥400 / M ¥500 / L ¥700, reservable for ¥500 extra, from the first train to the last.
  • Staffed luggage desk: seasonal. Last winter it ran 3 December–23 February at ¥1,000 per item; we couldn't confirm the 2026–27 dates yet.

Eat around the station, two layers

What most visitors go for: the department-store floors. Daimaru Sapporo B1 (10:00–20:00) has "Hoppe Town", a Hokkaido grocery street of about 80 brands, with much of it sold by weight, and restaurants on 8F (11:00–22:00). Stellar Place's restaurant floors are open 11:00–23:00. For jingisukan, Matsuo Jingisukan Sapporo Ekimae sits in the Nippon Life Building basement, connected to the subway station: 11:00–15:00 and 16:30–22:30, open daily except 31 Dec–2 Jan, with smoke-free roasters and Matsuo's sweet fruit-and-vegetable sauce.

Dig a little deeper: on the north side, about 330 m from the station, Yoshitsune (Kita 7 Nishi 5) is a small jingisukan place listed on Tabelog at about ¥4,000–4,999 a head. It opens 17:00–22:30, closes Wednesdays and Sundays, takes cash only and online reservations only, and smoking is allowed inside. Book before you fly if you want a seat.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • No bus terminal: until about FY2028, buses leave from street stops around the South exit, so allow a few extra minutes to find yours.
  • Yoshitsune takes online reservations only and closes on Wednesdays and Sundays.
  • Lockers fill up in the snow-festival and ski season; the reservable Multi E-Cube lockers are the safer bet.
  • Most hotels offer free cancellation, so holding a station-area room for a February trip early costs nothing if plans change.

Nearby, if you have more time

  • Sapporo in December 2026: the White Illumination and the Christmas market at Odori, ten minutes away along Chi-Ka-Ho.
  • Otaru from Sapporo: the canal town on the same JR line as the airport train.
  • Tourist Information Center: West Concourse (north side), 8:30–20:00, with a JR desk for foreign visitors.

One more thing: the night view above the platforms

T38, the observation deck on the 38th floor of JR Tower, stays open until 22:00 (last entry 21:30), so you can see Sapporo's lights and the snowy grid of streets on your first night without leaving the station. Adults pay ¥740. The entrance is easy to miss: take Stellar Place's Center Elevator II up to the East 6F entrance.
