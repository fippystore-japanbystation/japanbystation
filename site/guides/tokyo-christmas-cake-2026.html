Tokyo Christmas Cake 2026: Department-Store Deadlines, Standout Cakes and No-Booking Options

Takashimaya, Ginza Mitsukoshi and Isetan Shinjuku: when orders close, where you collect, the cakes worth the trip, and what to do if you're too late.

DeadlinesStandout cakesNo booking

The most common mistake with a Tokyo Christmas cake is leaving it until you land: the department stores close their orders between 12 and 15 December, and you collect on 24 or 25 December. If you're in Tokyo for Christmas Eve, order a 12 cm cake from Takashimaya or Ginza Mitsukoshi now; if you're too late, Lawson's mini Christmas cakes go on sale with no booking from 15 December. We suggest a 12 cm Takashimaya cake for two to four people, collected on the 24th.

Who this suits, and who it doesn't

Order one if

  • You'll be in Tokyo on 24 or 25 December and can get to the store.
  • You want the Japanese ritual: a cream-and-strawberry cake on Christmas Eve.
  • Your room has a fridge, or you'll eat it the same evening.

Skip the pre-order if

  • You arrive after mid-December: go for a mini cake or a slice instead.
  • You're on a train on the 24th with no fridge at the other end.

Takashimaya: chef cakes you collect at Nihonbashi or Shinjuku

Takashimaya's Kanto stores share one list of chef-made cakes, many sold only there. Order at the store's cake counter (Nihonbashi until 12 December) or online until 10:00 on 13 December, then collect on 24 or 25 December at the store you chose. Five that stand out:

  • Les Temps Plus, Noël Pistache (15 cm, ¥6,480): pistachio mousse and cream with raspberry jelly. Takashimaya's buyer says it sold out early last year.
  • Asterisque, Flocon (15 cm, ¥6,501): a snowflake-shaped tart of fromage-blanc mousse, strawberry cream and toasted meringue.
  • Mont St. Clair, Noël Rouge (12 cm, ¥5,801): pink and strawberry through and through, with griotte cherry and yoghurt. A good size for two to four.
  • Bonne Qualité, Noël Ichigo Milk (12 cm, ¥5,801): strawberry-milk mousse from a Nakameguro shop that opened in 2025, its first time at Takashimaya.
  • Calva, Éclat d'Abricot (15 cm, ¥6,912): apricot and caramel chocolate from the Ofuna shop near Kamakura, new to Takashimaya this year.

Ginza Mitsukoshi: eight pastry chefs, counted cakes

Ginza Mitsukoshi's eight Christmas cakes are made in fixed numbers for each pick-up day. Orders are online (Mitsukoshi Isetan online store) until 20:00 on 15 December; pick-up is 24–25 December, 11:00–19:00, at a special counter in Ginza Terrace on the Annex 9th floor.

  • Takeo, Chantilly Noël ~Kaori~ (¥7,344): a shortcake with Tochigi's Tochiaika strawberries, rice-flour sponge and Hokkaido cream. 50 on the 24th, 30 on the 25th.
  • Pâtissière MAYO, freshly made Christmas shortcake (¥9,180): Tottori's sweet "Tottoki" strawberries, raspberry jelly and strawberry macarons.
  • Mont St. Clair, Coffret Noël (¥9,001): Tsujiguchi Hironobu's shortcake with a sponge sweetened with Ishigaki Island brown sugar. The largest batch: 150 on the 24th.
  • Anténor Ginza, Ravisson Fraise (¥5,940): cocoa sponge, strawberries and white-chocolate holly, the lowest price of the eight.

Isetan Shinjuku: the showpieces, mostly on Christmas Eve

Isetan Shinjuku's 2026 theme is "Aurore" (dawn), and many of its own cakes are for pick-up on 24 December only, Isetan Shinjuku only. Look at Seist's First Light (orange, hazelnut and vanilla, ¥6,750), L'Atelier Todani's Gâteau Basque Point du Jour (a fresh-cake take on the Basque bake, ¥5,350) and Sébastien Bouillet's Fleur de l'Aube (raspberry, cassis and yuzu, ¥6,480). Orders opened on 2 October; check each cake's page for its last order day before you plan around it.

Small cakes, convenience stores and no booking

  • No booking needed: from 15 December, Lawson sells 3-gō mini Christmas cakes for one or two, strawberry ¥896, chocolate ¥950, on the shelf.
  • Small and pre-ordered: 7-Eleven's 3-gō cakes are ¥1,620 each, including a brûlée chocolate cake by Toshi Yoroizuka's Sept Rêves. Its classic "Christmas Kamakura" is ¥3,240; order on the 7NOW app by 23:59 on 30 November for 10% off. Pick-up 19–25 December.
  • Slices on the day: depachika cake counters and cake shops sell slices and small cakes through Christmas; the whole Christmas cakes are the pre-order part.

Staying in a hotel? That's fine: department-store and convenience-store cakes are collected in person, so all you need is to be in Tokyo on the pick-up day. Cream cakes need a fridge, so check your room has one, or plan to eat it that evening. New to food halls? How depachika work.

🚇 Collecting from Nihonbashi or Ginza and heading out again? The Tokyo Subway Ticket covers Tokyo Metro and Toei subway rides for a day or more, so a cake detour doesn't need another ticket.

📅 Good to know before you order

  • 30 November, 23:59: 7-Eleven's 10% early discount on the 7NOW app ends.
  • 12–13 December: Takashimaya's Christmas cake orders close (Nihonbashi store 12 Dec; online 13 Dec, 10:00).
  • 15 December, 20:00: Ginza Mitsukoshi's online orders close; each cake has a daily limit, so popular ones sell out sooner.
  • Already sold out: Mori Yoshida's Takashimaya cake when we checked on 26 November.

One more thing: a hotel shortcake from a department store

Takashimaya Nihonbashi's list includes a Christmas shortcake from the Royal Park Hotel Nihonbashi, made with a moist sponge (¥5,940), collected at the store on 24–25 December. It's the classic Japanese Christmas cake from a hotel pastry kitchen, without a trip to the hotel or a hotel-restaurant booking.
