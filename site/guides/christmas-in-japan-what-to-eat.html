Christmas in Japan 2026: What People Eat and How to Join In

Fried chicken from a bucket, a white cake with strawberries, and hot wine at a market. Here's how each one works this year, with the official dates and prices.

KFCChristmas cakeChristmas Eve plan

Christmas dinner in Japan is fried chicken and a strawberry shortcake, and the big night is Christmas Eve. KFC Japan takes reservations for its Christmas barrels and packs for pick-up on 19–25 December, and convenience stores and department stores pre-sell whole cakes the same way. As a visitor, we suggest a KFC pack reserved online, a small cake you can buy on the day, and a Christmas market for the Glühwein.

Who this suits, and who it doesn't

Join in if

  • You're in Japan on 23–25 December and want to eat what locals eat.
  • You have a hotel room or rental flat to take the bucket and cake back to.
  • You like a food tradition with a story (an advert from 1974, in this case).

Skip it if

  • You hate queues: 24 December is KFC Japan's busiest day of the year.
  • You want a restaurant Christmas dinner on the night: special courses are sold by reservation.
  • You're travelling between cities on the 24th with no fridge for a cream cake.

KFC Christmas: how a 1974 advert became dinner

KFC Japan ran its first Christmas campaign in 1974, a bucket of chicken with a bottle of wine, under the line "Kentucky for Christmas". According to KFC, the idea came from a foreign customer who said he couldn't find turkey in Japan. Today 24 December is KFC Japan's busiest day, about ten times busier than an average day.

What's on the 2026 menu

  • Party Barrel Original: 8 pieces of Original Recipe chicken, plus this year's new mac-and-cheese gratin (heat it at home) and a chocolate mousse cake about 18 cm across. ¥5,990, or ¥5,490 if you reserve by 13 December.
  • Party Barrel Variety: 4 pieces of chicken with tenders (or the new 11 Spice Chicken in some stores), 2 biscuits, the gratin and the cake. Same prices.
  • Christmas Packs: Pair ¥3,090, S ¥3,550, B ¥4,290 and A ¥5,790, mixing chicken, nuggets and barbecue chicken; BBQ Chicken Combo ¥2,890.
  • Premium: a garlic-and-herb roast leg of Gokoku-mitori chicken, ¥1,890 (in some stores).

New in 2026: the menu differs by store (one group sells tenders and the roast leg, another the 11 Spice Chicken and a spicy roast leg), so check your store's list on the KFC site. There's no picture-plate set this year.

How to order as a visitor

  1. Now Reserve on KFC Net Order (24 hours, on the website or app) or at the counter of the store you'll collect from. Reservations opened on 11 November; some items stop when they sell out.
  2. By 13 Dec Reserve a Party Barrel to get the ¥500-off early price. KFC expects in-store queues on 12–13 December and suggests ordering online.
  3. 19–25 Dec Collect at the store and time you chose. In-store orders need the reservation-card stub you're given when you pay; wrong day or wrong store, and you can't collect.

Smart timing: online orders made by 18 December for pick-up on 19–23 December (any time) or on 24–25 December before 13:00 or after 20:00 get a free burger voucher, KFC's "off-peak" bonus. Those are also the slots with the shortest wait.

No reservation? Convenience stores sell Christmas chicken too: 7-Eleven's charcoal-grilled roast chicken leg is ¥650.16 and FamilyMart's Awaodori roast leg ¥1,890 (FamilyMart's chicken sets are also sold by pre-order).

The Christmas cake: strawberries, cream, order ahead

The Japanese Christmas cake is a light sponge with whipped cream and strawberries, the classic "shortcake". Cake sizes are in gō: 3-gō feeds one or two, 4-gō (about 12–13 cm) two to four, 5-gō (about 15 cm) four to six. Most whole cakes are sold by pre-order and collected around 19–25 December.

Department stores: the showpiece cakes

Depachika cakes are the ones people queue to pick up. Isetan Shinjuku opened its 2026 Christmas cake orders on 2 October, and several of its own cakes are for 24 December pick-up only. If you want one, order soon and plan your evening around the store pick-up. On the day, depachika and cake shops still sell slices and small cakes, which is all most travellers need. Market food: Glühwein, sausages and stollen

The third Christmas menu is German, and it lives at the Christmas markets. What to look for this year:

  • Glühwein in three colours (red, rosé, white) at the Tokyo Christmas Market, and white Glühwein at about 20 stalls in Yokohama. In Osaka, Tenshiba's stalls pour it from ¥800, ¥100 less if you buy the ¥1,000 mug.
  • Stollen: Skytree Town's market slices "Santa's stollen", and Roppongi Hills sells stollen next to its pretzels.
  • Something hot and filling: stew in a bread bowl, a 67 cm spiral sausage at Skytree Town, goulash in Osaka (¥1,400).
  • Not drinking: Kinderpunsch and hot chocolate are at every market.

A Christmas Eve plan that needs no restaurant booking

Restaurants sell their Christmas courses by reservation (Umeda Sky Building's 39th-floor restaurants, for example, run them on 23–25 December from ¥17,000 a person, for two or more). Here's an evening that works without one:

  1. Now Reserve a KFC Christmas Pack online for the 24th, before 13:00 (or on the 23rd), and note the store.
  2. Afternoon Christmas market for Glühwein and a sausage while it's light; most are free to enter.
  3. Early evening Collect the chicken and a Lawson mini cake on the way back.
  4. Night Eat in your room, then go out for the illuminations, which run late.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • 13 December: last day for KFC's ¥5,490 Party Barrel price; 18 December for the off-peak burger voucher.
  • 30 Nov–1 Dec: the convenience-store early discounts end (7-Eleven app 30 Nov, Lawson and FamilyMart 1 Dec).
  • Limited quantities: KFC's barrels, packs and premium items stop when they sell out.
  • Keep it cold: cream cakes need a fridge; ask your hotel if your room has one before you order a whole cake.

One more thing: the cake's already in the bucket

Both 2026 Party Barrels include a chocolate mousse cake about 18 cm across. So a barrel for four is dinner and dessert in one reservation, and you don't need to queue at a cake counter on the 24th. Want the strawberry version too? That's what the ¥896 Lawson mini cake is for.
