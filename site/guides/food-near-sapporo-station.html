Food Near Sapporo Station: 7 Places From a 6:30 Hokkaido Breakfast to Zangi at 23:00

Kaiten sushi and seafood bowls upstairs, soup curry along the underground walkway, and fried chicken until late by the North exit.

MorningLunchDinner and late

Hungry right now? Go up to Stellar Place 6F: kaiten sushi at Nemuro Hanamaru, or a seafood bowl at Dekitateya, both open until 22:00. When it's snowing, Sapporo's soup curry is five minutes away along the Chi-Ka-Ho underground walkway, so you never go outside. Breakfast is the station's weak spot, so we picked two places worth the short walk. All seven had 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked, and the hours come from their own listings or Sapporo's tourism site.

Who this list suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • Snowy days: four picks are inside or under the station area
  • First night in Hokkaido: sushi, soup curry, miso ramen and zangi in one list
  • Late arrivals from New Chitose: zangi until 23:30

Think twice

  • Walk-in ramen: Ichiryuan takes bookings online
  • Short layovers at Hanamaru: there's a numbered-ticket queue
  • Cash-free travellers at Zangi Honpo: it's cash only
We suggest: soup curry at Suage4 for lunch, Nemuro Hanamaru for an early dinner (be there at opening), and zangi by the North exit if you land late. Not sure which side of the station you're on? Our Sapporo Station exit guide explains South vs North.

A proper Hokkaido breakfast, or coffee for the walk (from 6:30)

  • Northern Terrace Diner (ノーザンテラスダイナー), Sapporo Grand Hotel, East Building 1F: a Western and Japanese "Hokkaido breakfast" buffet from 6:30 to 10:00, ¥4,000 for adults and ¥2,000 for children. It's about 10 minutes from the station down Ekimae-dori, and the restaurant takes public bookings online and by phone. Cards accepted.
  • Marumi Coffee Stand (マルミコーヒースタンド), Kita 2 Nishi 3, B1: a take-away specialty-coffee stand open 7:30–20:00, about 3 minutes from the subway's Sapporo Station. Besides hand-drip coffee and espresso, it makes a soft serve with espresso in it. Closed only over New Year.

Miso ramen and soup curry without going out in the snow

  • Ramen Sapporo Ichiryuan (ラーメン札幌 一粒庵), Hokuren Building B1: about 2 minutes east of the South exit. Miso ramen is the house bowl, there's a seafood shio ramen with Hokkaido milk, and the gyoza are handmade. It's small (14 seats), makes about 200 bowls a day, and takes online bookings: same-day lunch by 10:00, dinner by 16:00. Lunch 11:00–15:00, dinner 17:00–21:00; closed Monday evening, Tuesday and 1–3 January. From about ¥1,000, plus a surcharge on menu prices (¥110 at lunch, ¥220 at dinner). Cards, IC cards and QR payments accepted, with an English menu.
  • Soup Curry Suage4 (スープカレー スアゲ4), miredo 2F: soup curry from Suage, a well-known Sapporo name, with toppings like Hokkaido pork kakuni or crispy chicken. Its building connects to the Chi-Ka-Ho underground walkway, about 5 minutes from the South exit. Open every day 11:00–21:00 (last order 20:30), about ¥1,000–2,000.
  • Dekitateya (できたて屋), Stellar Place 6F: kaisendon seafood bowls and grilled overnight-dried fish sets, right in the station building. Open 11:00–22:00; 68 seats and no bookings. About ¥1,000–4,000.

Sushi off the belt, then zangi until 23:30

  • Nemuro Hanamaru (根室花まる), Stellar Place Center 6F: the Sapporo Station branch of a kaiten-sushi chain from Nemuro, on Hokkaido's eastern tip. No reservations: take a numbered ticket from the machine at the entrance. A Japanese guide site says about 30 groups are usually waiting, and up to about 90 at peak. Open 11:00–22:00 (last order 21:30). About ¥2,000–5,000.
  • Sapporo Zangi Honpo (札幌ザンギ本舗), 3 min from the North exit: zangi is Hokkaido's take on karaage fried chicken, and here it comes in about ten flavours. The zangi set meal is ¥968. Open every day 11:30–23:30 (last order 23:00). Cash only; there's a ¥275 charge per person in the evening if you order alcohol.

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Ichiryuan takes online bookings (same-day lunch by 10:00, dinner by 16:00) and closes on Tuesdays, Monday evenings and 1–3 January.
  • Nemuro Hanamaru takes no reservations; the queue is a numbered ticket.
  • Cash: keep some yen for Zangi Honpo; the others take cards.

One more thing: a miso ramen made without pork

Sapporo's miso ramen usually starts with a pork-based broth. Ichiryuan, two minutes from the South exit, also lists a miso ramen made without pork or alcohol, and a vegan ramen if you book ahead. That's rare among Sapporo's ramen shops, and useful if someone in your group doesn't eat pork. The shop doesn't name a halal certifier, so ask when you book.
