Minoh Falls in Autumn: Leave Osaka at 1, Back Before Dark?

An afternoon is enough on a weekday. Here is the clock, a shorter version, and when to set off earlier.

Full walkShort walkLeave earlier

Yes, on a weekday. Leave Osaka-umeda at 13:00, reach Minoh at 13:26, walk about 45 minutes uphill and you are at the falls around 14:15. Spend 20 minutes there, walk down, and you are back at the station around 15:30, more than an hour before the 16:48 sunset. At weekends in the leaf season the path is packed, so leave by 11:00 instead.

Answer A · The full outing from Umeda

Station to the falls and back

≈2 h 30 min door to door

Take the 13:00 Hankyu Takarazuka Line express from Osaka-umeda, change at Ishibashi handai-mae (13:15, cross to the Minoo Line, 13:20) and arrive at Minoh at 13:26. The waterfall path starts about 5 minutes from the station and climbs gently along the river for about 2.7 km. The park office gives 40 to 50 minutes to the 33 m falls. Entry to the park is free.

Answer B · Shorter, with a snack stop

Momiji tempura and the red bridge at Ryuan-ji

≈1 h 15 min round trip

Walk up the shopping stretch at the start of the path, where shops fry momiji tempura, Minoh's famous snack of salted maple leaves in sweet batter. Carry on past the insect museum (15 to 20 minutes from the station) to Ryuan-ji temple and its red bridge, about 5 minutes further, then turn around. You see the autumn colour on the lower path without the climb to the falls.

Answer C · If the waterfall is the priority

Leave two hours earlier

Umeda by 11:00

Leaving at 13:00 works, but with only about 20 minutes at the falls. Leave Umeda around 11:00 and you can stop for lunch near the station, take the climb slowly, spend an hour at the falls and still walk down in daylight. At weekends and on holidays from 14 November to 6 December the park office expects a solid stream of people on the path all the way from the station, so the early start matters even more then.

Our verdict

We suggest: on a weekday, the 13:00 train from Osaka-umeda is enough: falls by about 14:15, back at Minoh Station around 15:30, before the 16:48 sunset. That changes at weekends and on holidays until 6 December, when we would leave by 11:00, or flip the plan and arrive around 15:30 to walk up for the light-up (sunset to 21:30). If you set off after 14:00, do the short walk to Ryuan-ji instead.

One more thing: the brewery on your way back

Get off one stop early on the way back, at Makiochi, and walk about 10 minutes to Minoh Beer Warehouse. Minoh Beer is a family-run brewery founded in 1997 that has won awards abroad, and this is its own taproom in a quiet residential street, with the beer at its freshest. After the falls and the momiji tempura, it is the local way to finish the day.

MINOH BEER WAREHOUSE, 3-14-18 Makiochi, Minoh; about 10–12 min walk from Hankyu Makiochi Station. 11:00–21:00 (last order 20:30), Sundays to 20:00; closed Thursdays. Sources: Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau (osaka-info.jp), Tabelog.
