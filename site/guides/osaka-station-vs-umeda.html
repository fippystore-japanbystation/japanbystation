Osaka Station vs Umeda: Same Place? Which Side for Your Hotel

Six stations, one walkable hub. Which one your train uses, where the airport bus stops, and the right side of the station to sleep on.

Which stationWhich sideWhere to stay

Yes, it is the same place. JR calls its station Osaka Station; the private railways and the subway call theirs Umeda or Osaka-umeda. They form one hub you can walk across, so the station you need depends only on your train line. For your hotel, the side matters more than the name: pick the side your trains and airport bus use, and you skip the maze with your luggage.

Who a base at Osaka Station suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit

  • JR Pass holders and anyone doing day trips: JR, Hankyu and Hanshin all start here
  • Travellers arriving on the HARUKA or the airport limousine bus
  • Rainy days: much of the area is linked by covered decks and underground walkways
  • Families who want big department stores and food halls next to the hotel

Think twice

  • Travellers who want to walk out of the hotel into old streets and small bars: that is Tenma or Nakazakicho, a short walk away
  • Tight budgets: hotels on top of the station cost more (see the prices below)
  • Anyone who hates crowds at rush hour: the main concourses are busy

Osaka Station or Umeda: which one do you need?

Look at the line on your ticket or app, then use this list. All of these stations sit inside the same few blocks.

  • JR lines (Loop Line, Kyoto and Kobe lines, the HARUKA, the Kuroshio): JR Osaka Station. A JR Pass works here.
  • Hankyu (to Kyoto-Kawaramachi, Kobe-Sannomiya, Takarazuka): Hankyu Osaka-umeda, platforms on the 3rd floor of its own terminal. It was renamed from "Umeda" on 1 October 2019, which is why older maps still show the old name.
  • Hanshin (to Kobe and Koshien): Hanshin Osaka-Umeda, underground next to the Hanshin Department Store. Renamed on the same day.
  • Osaka Metro: Umeda for the Midosuji Line (Namba, Tennoji, Shin-Osaka), Higashi-Umeda for the Tanimachi Line, Nishi-Umeda for the Yotsubashi Line. Three names, three stations, all reached on foot underground.
  • JR Tozai Line: Kitashinchi, a separate JR station south of Osaka Station, within walking distance.
Our suggestion: type the line name, not just "Umeda", into your map app. "Umeda" alone can send you to the wrong one of six stations.

Which side? Start from where you are going

  • Going to Kansai Airport on the HARUKA? Use the underground platforms on the Umekita side: track 21 for the airport, track 24 for Shin-Osaka and Kyoto. The nearest gate is the West Gate. These platforms opened in March 2023, so older guides miss them.
  • Going to Kansai Airport by bus? Walk to Hankyu Sanbangai, the bus terminal under the Hankyu buildings. The limousine takes about 60 minutes to Terminal 1 and costs ¥1,800 (child ¥900), with no reservation.
  • Going to Hotel Granvia Osaka? Leave JR by the Central Gate and turn right: the hotel is part of the station building.
  • Going to Grand Front Osaka or Hotel Hankyu RESPIRE? Use the 3rd-floor bridge gate and walk north over the deck: RESPIRE says 3 minutes.
  • Going to Kyoto or Kobe on Hankyu? Head for the Hankyu Department Store side, follow the 1st-floor passage, then ride up to the 3rd-floor Hankyu gates.
  • Going to Namba or Shin-Osaka by subway? Same direction, towards the Hankyu Department Store, then take the escalator down to Midosuji Line Umeda.
  • Going to Hilton Osaka or Herbis Osaka? They are across the road on the south side; the Osaka Convention and Tourism Bureau puts the Hilton 2 minutes from JR Osaka.

Eat around Osaka Station: the famous bite and the local one

What most visitors go for: the ikayaki (pressed squid pancake) from the Snack Park on B1 of the Hanshin Umeda Main Store. It is the store's speciality, sold by the thousand every day, and the line moves fast. It is a quick stand-up snack, not a meal.

Dig a little deeper: under the JR tracks, the old Shin-Umeda Shokudogai food alley is where office workers eat. On its ground floor, Kushikatsu Matsuba Sohonten has served deep-fried skewers standing-only for about 70 years. JTB's GLTJP says to start with the beef; most skewers cost in the ¥100s and none is over ¥300. The rules: never dip twice in the shared sauce (use the free cabbage to scoop more), and no credit cards. It opens weekdays 14:00 to 22:00 and weekends 11:00 to 21:30, and closes on Mondays.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • Landing late at Kansai Airport? The limousine to Osaka Station runs a late-night bus: the last one leaves Terminal 1 at 0:15 and takes cashless payment only (timetable checked 24 November 2026). A hotel on the bus side of the station saves the walk at 1 a.m.
  • Hotel names can mislead: "Umeda" in a hotel name can mean anywhere from Osaka Station to Nakazakicho or Dojima. Check the walking time to the gate you'll use before you book.
  • Free cancellation: many listings around the station offer it on Booking.com (check each one), so you can hold a room while you compare the other side.

One more thing: let the airport bus do the station for you

Coming in from Kansai Airport with big suitcases, the limousine bus beats the train for one reason: it ends at the street, not deep inside the station. Besides Hankyu Sanbangai, buses from the airport stop at Herbis Osaka (for the Hilton side and Osaka Station Hotel, 2 minutes away), and some also stop at Hotel Hankyu RESPIRE in Yodobashi Umeda Tower and at Candeo Hotels Osaka The Tower in Dojima. Check which stops your bus makes on the timetable and pick your hotel to match. No escalators, no gates.
