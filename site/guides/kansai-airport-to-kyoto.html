Kansai Airport to Kyoto: Haruka, Limousine Bus or via Osaka?

One train, one bus, one cheaper detour. The right one depends on your hotel, your bags and your landing time.

The three optionsBy hotel areaWhere to stay

For most first-time arrivals we'd take the JR Haruka: about 75 to 80 minutes from the airport to Kyoto Station with no change, and ¥2,700 on the visitor one-way ticket. Pick the limousine bus (¥2,800) if you land late, because it runs until 23:20 while the last Haruka leaves at 22:16, or if you want your suitcases in the hold. Go via Osaka on ordinary trains only if your hotel is near Shijo or Kawaramachi and you travel light: it's the cheapest, but it means changes.

Who the Haruka suits, and who it doesn't

Haruka is right for

  • Hotels around Kyoto Station, or anywhere you'll reach by taxi or subway from there
  • Daytime and early-evening landings
  • Foreign visitors who can use the discounted HARUKA One-way Ticket

Think about the bus or Hankyu if

  • Your flight lands after about 21:30 and immigration is slow
  • You have more cases than hands: the bus takes them in the hold
  • Your hotel is on Shijo-dori and you'd rather not change at Kyoto Station

The three ways in

A · JR Haruka

75–80 min · ¥2,700

Fastest, no change

About every 30 minutes; the first weekday train leaves the airport at 06:31 and the last at 22:16. The HARUKA One-way Ticket for foreign visitors on a temporary-visitor stay costs ¥2,700 to Kyoto (¥1,350 child) if bought from 1 November 2026, and covers ordinary reserved or unreserved seats. Without it, the regular unreserved fare is about ¥3,100.

B · Limousine bus

85–90 min · ¥2,800

Late landings, big bags

From Terminal 1 (and most from Terminal 2) to Kyoto Station Hachijo Exit, roughly every 20 minutes. The last bus leaves T1 at 23:20 and arrives at 0:45. No reservations towards Kyoto: first come, first seated. Pay cash or tap ICOCA; suitcases go in the hold.

C · Ordinary trains via Osaka

¥1,670 · about 1 h 50

Cheapest, for light packers

Nankai to the Sakaisuji subway, then the Hankyu Kyoto Line straight to Karasuma or Kyoto-kawaramachi: about ¥1,670 and about 1 hour 50 minutes. JR local and rapid trains via Osaka to Kyoto Station are about ¥1,900. Every change means stairs and crowds with your bags.

Our verdict: Haruka for most arrivals, on the ¥2,700 visitor ticket if you qualify. The answer changes when you land late (take the bus: it runs over an hour after the last Haruka), when you have heavy luggage and a hotel near the Hachijo side (bus), or when you travel light to a Shijo hotel and want the cheapest ride (Hankyu via Osaka).

Choose by where your hotel is

  • Kyoto Station, south side (Hachijo): the limousine bus stops right outside, in front of the Avanti building opposite the Hachijo Exit, so you skip the station crowds entirely.
  • Kyoto Station, north side or Kyoto Tower area: the Haruka is simplest; walk out through the central gates.
  • Shijo-Karasuma or Kawaramachi: either the Haruka plus a short subway or taxi ride, or the Hankyu route via Osaka that ends right under Shijo-dori.
  • Arashiyama: GOOD LUCK TRIP lists Haruka plus the JR Sagano Line to Saga-Arashiyama at about 1 hour 45 minutes from ¥3,300.

Eat like a local on arrival night

Landed hungry? Go up, not out. Kyoto Ramen Koji on the 10th floor of the Kyoto Station building has nine ramen shops from around Japan, open 11:00 to 22:00 (last order 21:30), and Navitime puts a meal at about ¥1,000 to ¥3,000. It's the easy first dinner for anyone off a daytime Haruka. If you arrive on the last train or bus, the convenience stores around the station are your friend; save the real Kyoto dinner for tomorrow.

📅 Good to know before you book

  • The visitor ticket went up on 1 November 2026, from ¥2,200 to ¥2,700 to Kyoto. Tickets bought on or before 31 October stay valid with nothing extra to pay. It's one per person, and Japanese nationals can't use it.
  • Going home by bus? The Kyoto-to-airport limousine needs a reservation (up to a month ahead); the airport-to-Kyoto direction doesn't take them.
  • Door to door: Yasaka's shared airport shuttle is ¥4,300 per person, but must be booked by 12:00 two days before. A taxi is about ¥40,000.
  • Most Kyoto hotels on booking sites show a free-cancellation date, handy if your flight time moves.

One more thing: Kyoto's first view is free, above the platforms

Before you head to the hotel, take the escalators beside the station's Great Stairs, 171 steps lit by about 15,000 LEDs, and walk the Skyway, a glass corridor 45 metres above the central hall that starts from the 11th floor. It's free and open 10:00 to 22:00, and it's the easiest way to see Kyoto Tower and the city at night without paying for an observatory.
