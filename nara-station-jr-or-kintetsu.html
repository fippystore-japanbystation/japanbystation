🦌 Doing Osaka, Nara and Kyoto on Kintetsu? A Kintetsu Rail Pass (1 day ¥1,900) also covers the Nara buses as far as Horyuji, so the ride from Namba and the bus to Todaiji are both on one ticket.

JR Nara or Kintetsu-Nara: which one is yours?

Kintetsu-Nara suits

  • Anyone coming from Osaka-Namba (about 40 minutes) or Kintetsu Kyoto
  • A first visit: the park, Kofukuji and the deer start a few minutes away
  • A big suitcase: 49 extra-large lockers in the station

JR Nara suits

  • Japan Rail Pass holders: the pass doesn't cover Kintetsu
  • Coming from JR Kyoto: 45 minutes on the Miyakoji Rapid, ¥720
  • Hotels on the JR side of town

From Kyoto without a pass, Kintetsu is quicker: about 35 minutes by limited express (¥1,280) or 45 minutes by express (¥760). IC cards such as ICOCA and Suica work on both railways and on Nara's buses.

We suggest: come in on Kintetsu, walk east from the East Gates through Nara Park to Todaiji, and take the ¥100 Gurutto Bus back if your legs are done. With a Rail Pass, ride JR to JR Nara and hop on a Nara Kotsu bus toward the park.

Which gate at Kintetsu-Nara, by where you're going

  • Going to Todaiji, Kofukuji, Kasuga Taisha or the deer? Use the East Gates and take the stairs or lift up to 1F, where the exit by the fountain is signed for all three. Todaiji is about 20 minutes on foot and Kasuga Taisha about 25.
  • Need a map or bus help first? The general tourist information counter is on 1F right by that fountain exit, with money exchange machines next to it.
  • Catching the airport bus to Kansai Airport? Use the West Gates. Its stairs lead up to the stops for the airport limousine, expressway and sightseeing buses.
  • Shopping on Konishi-dori? From the East Gates, follow the B1 passage signed for Konishi-dori and Nalala.
  • Arriving at JR Nara instead? Walk east for 15–20 minutes to reach Kofukuji and the park, or ride a Nara Kotsu city loop bus to Todaiji Daibutsuden / Kasuga Taisha-mae and walk about 5 minutes.

A one-day Nara Bus Pass (¥600) covers the central buses. It's sold at the bus ticket office opposite Kintetsu-Nara and inside JR Nara.

Eat near the station, two ways

The one most visitors go for: Nara's own dishes along the walk to the temples. Our Nara day trip guide covers kakinoha-zushi (sushi wrapped in persimmon leaves) at Izasa-chaya near Todaiji, and the fast-pounded mochi at Nakatanidou, 5 minutes from Kintetsu-Nara.

Dig a little deeper: Kamameshi Shizuka, opposite the Nara National Museum, about 15 minutes' walk from Kintetsu-Nara. It has cooked rice pots to order since 1959. The signature "Nara nanashu" kamameshi, with prawn, crab, conger eel and chicken, takes about 20 minutes, and the scorched rice at the bottom is the prize. There's a queue at lunch most days, so come at 11:00 or mid-afternoon. About ¥2,000, cards accepted, closed Tuesdays (plus one irregular day a month), last orders 15:00 on weekdays and 16:00 at weekends. It was 4★+ on Google Maps when we checked (27 Nov 2026).

📅 Good to know before you go

  • Rail Pass: the Japan Rail Pass is not valid on Kintetsu, so the "closer" station costs extra if you hold one.
  • Coins: Kintetsu-Nara's lockers want ¥100 coins; on a busy day the coins run out faster than the lockers.
  • Closing times: Todaiji's Great Buddha Hall closes at 17:00 from November to March (see our Nara day trip guide), so a late train in means a short visit.

One more thing: the ¥100 bus for tired legs

The walk out to Todaiji is pleasant; the walk back after the Great Buddha, Nigatsudo and the deer is where legs give up. Nara's Gurutto Bus loop runs from Kintetsu-Nara to the Daibutsuden-mae parking lot by Todaiji, and the fare is ¥100. Walk out through the park, then ride back to the station for half the price of a ¥200 pack of deer crackers.

Quick answers

Which Nara station is closer to Todaiji and the deer park?

Kintetsu-Nara. Leave by the East Gates and go up to street level by the fountain; Nara Park starts a few minutes east, and Todaiji is about 20 minutes on foot (Todaiji's own access page). JR Nara is a 15–20 minute walk further west, or a Nara Kotsu bus ride. Checked 27 November 2026.

Should I take JR or Kintetsu to Nara?

From Osaka-Namba or Kyoto without a rail pass, Kintetsu: it arrives at the edge of the park (about 40 minutes from Osaka-Namba). With a Japan Rail Pass, take JR: the pass doesn't cover Kintetsu, and JR from Kyoto takes 45 minutes on the Miyakoji Rapid (¥720 without a pass).

Are there coin lockers at Kintetsu-Nara Station?

Yes, 551 of them: 49 extra-large (¥800–1,500 a day), 248 large (¥600–1,000), 91 medium and 163 small (¥400–700), per Kintetsu's list dated 10 October 2026. They take ¥100 coins only, so change notes at the money exchange machines first.

How do I get from Kintetsu-Nara to Todaiji by bus?

Take the Gurutto Bus loop (¥100) to the Daibutsuden-mae parking lot stop, or a Nara Kotsu city loop bus to Todaiji Daibutsuden / Kasuga Taisha-mae and walk about 5 minutes. Both are listed on Todaiji's access page. Walking takes about 20 minutes.
